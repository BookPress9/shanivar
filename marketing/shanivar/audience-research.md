# Shanivar audience research
Prepared 9 September 2026 by the Social Media Manager role. Desk research and first-week recommendation; no customer interviews, account analytics, ads or publication performed.

## Recommendation
**Founder update, 9 September 2026:** [Current audience direction](audience-direction.md) expands the core audience to Millennials and adult Gen Z, adds younger adults supporting their parents' mental wellbeing, and establishes self-realization across life stages as the editorial direction. The age priorities below document the original research hypothesis and are superseded by that brief. The evidence remains background context, not validation of the expanded audience.

Start with an **Instagram-first, English-language organic pilot for adults 22–34 in India who work or create on screens and want a small, repeatable pause in their day**. This is a testable audience hypothesis, not a validated customer profile. Use ordinary transitions—opening an app, finishing work, replying to a message—as hooks. Position Shanivar by The Hybrid Monk around returning attention to daily life.

India, English, Instagram and the age range are working assumptions because geography, audience analytics, account handle and existing customer data were not supplied. The brand's Indian contemplative roots inform this choice; they do not prove demand. Do not infer religious affiliation from someone's location or name.

## Editorial choices
- Theme: **A small return to your day.** Seven posts; four images and three silent, readable motion-graphic Reels.
- Sequence follows the existing Week 1 “Return to Body” architecture: pause, attention, body scan, breath/reaction, naming, digital awareness, reflection.
- Tone: observant and spare; invite a choice without implying that somebody is broken.
- Visual direction: void black, bone text, bronze rings; generous space; no lotus, generic chakra art, fabricated UI or fake testimonials.
- CTA: try the action, save the post, and optionally comment “Tried” after actually doing it. No app-store or waitlist link is claimed.
- Publish time proposal: 19:30 Asia/Kolkata daily, chosen for a consistent first test. This is **not** a researched best posting time. Compare actual account insights later.
- Discovery and retention are separate: social views do not show that a practice happened.

## First-week learning plan
**Single measurable objective:** collect 10 distinct voluntary, self-reported practice attempts across the week, with at least 3 of those people reporting a return on another day. These are internal learning thresholds, not forecasts. No existing audience size or baseline is known.

After publication is authorized, the founder can manually tally generic “Tried” comments at 24 hours and seven days. Deduplicate locally without copying personal profiles, emotions or journal content into the campaign folder. A commenter’s report remains self-report, not independently observed practice completion. Avoid unsolicited DMs or requests for sensitive disclosures.

Record reach, saves, shares, Reel watch time and profile visits from the account's available insights; unavailable fields remain “unavailable.” Keep self-reported attempts separate from those engagement metrics. Compare each post after the same exposure period; do not declare a format winner from seven different topics.

If voluntary follow-up research is later authorized, invite five adults from the priority segment to describe the last automatic app opening, then observe whether they understand and perform one post's action. Ask what confused them and where it would fit. Do not pitch features first or equate compliments with demand. No participants have been recruited.

**Next decision:** hold topic sequence and visual style steady. If people try once but report difficulty returning, test exactly one change next week: an explicit everyday trigger in the first sentence. If evidence is thin: “Hold steady. Gather data from 5 more users before changing anything.”
