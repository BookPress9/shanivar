## Accepted baseline — 11 September 2026

**Source:** Founder direction, product direction, audience/voice briefs and current social research.

**Observed:** Shanivar should speak to adult Gen Z, Millennials, parents and older adults through automatic scrolling, attention, connection and self-understanding across life stages. The founder wants the writing to become deeper and to serve the long-range aim of expanding human consciousness.

**Interpretation:** “Expanding human consciousness” should appear in the work as greater voluntary awareness, embodied noticing, reflection and choice. It is not a public effectiveness claim or a guaranteed spiritual outcome.

**Craft change:** Begin with specific lived moments, uncover one honest contradiction and leave the reader with one question or practice. Give recurring images and phrases continuity so Shanivar feels present across posts.

**Review after:** The first two authorized content cycles have comparable 7-day results, or when founder/product direction changes—whichever comes first.
