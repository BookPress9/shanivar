# Shanivar engagement and Reels playbook

Updated 11 September 2026. Owner: Social Media Manager. This is a working creative and measurement standard, not a promise of reach. Recheck Instagram's in-app Professional Dashboard → Best Practices before each production cycle because its guidance is personalized and changes over time.

## 12 September 2026 — rolling-month operational update

The [30-day campaign](month-02/README.md) is the current working proposal. Its feed cadence is a capacity decision: three originals plus one language companion each week, with optional Stories. It supersedes generic daily-post or Reel-frequency defaults for this month. Account-specific Best Practices must still be checked; neither a fixed duration nor a fixed posting time is an algorithmic guarantee.

The founder's first-post URL is captured in [the intake](month-02/first-post-analysis.md). Creative and private Insights could not be fetched. There is no verified performance baseline yet. Never backfill missing reach, saves, watch time or publication dates from a draft or a public URL.

Follow [the month operations standard](month-02/operations.md) for metric definitions and maturity windows. Keep language tests observational; separate Trial Reels from normal delivery, alternate language order, and log topic/weekday/voice confounds. Do not sum post reach into unique audience coverage. Average watch time divided by duration is a watch-time ratio, not completion; it can exceed 100% with repeat viewing.

Weekly reviews should protect one active hypothesis rather than continuously changing creative. Hold when evidence is insufficient. Give the Creative Writer one proposed adjustment for the next cycle, with raw evidence and uncertainty. Fix false claims or unreadable text immediately and log them.

## What current platform evidence supports

Instagram's official Best Practices hub covers creation, engagement, reach, monetization and guidelines, including personalized advice about posting frequency, attention, Reel length, trending audio, hashtags and the relationship between Reels and follower growth. This means Shanivar should use the current in-app guidance for the account instead of treating a universal posting schedule or duration as fact.

Meta made Trial Reels broadly available to show a Reel to non-followers first. Its internal analysis of more than 400,000 creators found that, among creators who posted Reels more often after trying the feature, 80% saw increased non-follower Reel reach. Meta explicitly says performance is not guaranteed. Use Trial Reels for creative-risk testing when the account has access; record whether the feature was available.

Instagram provides total and average watch time, replays and a retention chart for Reels. Meta describes watch time and the retention curve as tools for finding where viewers leave and improving the opening. These are diagnostic signals, not measures of reflection or practice.

Meta reported in January 2026 that 75% of Instagram recommendations in the US came from original posts after increasing original-content prevalence. This is a US platform-level result, not an India reach guarantee, but it supports making original Shanivar work rather than recycled watermarked clips.

## Shanivar's engagement principle

Earn attention by naming something true before asking for attention back. A strong post should leave the person with more agency than they had when it began. Do not manufacture anxiety about scrolling, exploit spiritual insecurity or build an endless cliffhanger loop. Engagement supports discovery; the work ultimately serves a voluntary pause, a useful question or a practice in ordinary life.

“Deeper” has a concrete meaning here:

1. Start with a lived detail rather than a category such as burnout or mindfulness.
2. Reveal a contradiction the reader can recognise without being told who they are.
3. Turn toward one question, choice or embodied action.
4. End before explaining the reader to themselves.

## Reels production standard

- Use 9:16, 1080 × 1920 as the house master. Keep essential text and faces away from interface edges; inspect the cover crop and the full mobile frame.
- Make the opening visible and understandable without sound. The first line should identify the scene or tension immediately. Avoid logo-only openings.
- Keep one core idea per Reel. House structure: recognition → tension → turn → optional action → quiet close.
- Use human voice, room tone or music when it adds meaning. Caption all speech and keep essential meaning onscreen. Do not force trending audio into a contemplative piece.
- Use original visuals, intentional edits and a designed cover. Generated footage must be labeled internally and checked for anatomy, continuity, cultural detail, typography and misleading product UI.
- Treat length as a test variable. Begin with two comparable bands—15–25 seconds and 25–40 seconds—then use retention and average watch time to decide. These bands are a Shanivar experiment, not an Instagram rule.
- If Trial Reels is available, test a materially new opening or visual treatment with non-followers before moving it to the main audience. Do not use the feature to publish unreviewed claims.

## Carousel production standard

- Slide 1 must work as a complete statement and create honest recognition at profile-grid size.
- Use one thought per slide, large type, strong contrast and a clear visual rhythm. A swipe must deepen or turn the idea.
- Build a real arc: doorway → lived moment → tension → reframing → question/practice → return point.
- Make the final slide worth saving. Prefer a concise practice or question to a generic request for comments.
- Publish separate English and Hindi editions when both are justified. Preserve cadence through translation; do not treat Hindi as a literal word swap or as a proxy for every Indian language.

## Engagement that fits the mission

Good invitations include saving a practice for a real moment, sharing privately with someone who would welcome it, answering a non-sensitive question, choosing between useful next topics, or trying a small action. Avoid “comment YES,” forced vulnerability, guilt about parents, unsolicited DMs and prompts that solicit birth data or mental-health disclosures in public.

Community replies should acknowledge what was actually said and leave room. Do not turn a person's comment into a diagnosis, testimonial or sales opening. Crisis or self-harm content follows the existing deterministic safety route and is never handled as engagement.

## Measurement

Record raw counts and rates after equal 24-hour, 7-day and 28-day windows when available.

| Question | Primary signals | Interpretation limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Did the opening hold attention? | Average watch time; opening retention; first major drop | Topic, audience and distribution also affect the curve. |
| Did the idea travel? | Non-follower reach; shares/reposts; qualified follows | Reach does not show that the idea helped someone. |
| Was it worth returning to? | Saves per reach; replays; later profile visits | A save or replay is not practice completion. |
| Did the story deepen interest? | Profile visits per reach; follows per profile visit; destination clicks | Attribution may be incomplete. |
| Did anyone try the practice? | Voluntary self-report or product completion event with consent | Keep self-report and observed product events separate. |

For each learning cycle, change one major variable: opening line, opening visual, duration band, language edition, narration, or CTA. Keep topic and publication conditions as stable as practical. Report uncertainty and do not declare a winner from one post.
