# Shanivar — 30 days of aura

Status: production calendar, not a publication record  
Cadence: 08:30 and 19:30 Asia/Kolkata  
Purpose: make Shanivar recognizable through restraint, useful observation and a consistent visual world.

## The rhythm

- **Morning — Presence:** one arresting image, sentence or short Reel. Build recognition before explanation.
- **Evening — Practice:** one small action, question or reveal. Give the person something they can actually use.
- **Feed priority:** Monday, Wednesday and Friday evening entries are the primary feed packages. Other entries can be Stories, Notes or low-production vertical clips. This preserves two daily touchpoints without demanding fourteen polished feed posts each week.
- **Visual grammar:** black space, bone, bronze, electric blue, restrained violet, one living detail. Slow camera movement. No generic lotus stock art, fake testimonials or motivational shouting.
- **Copy grammar:** name one observable moment, offer one choice, then stop. “Aura” is the felt coherence of the work, never a score or supernatural claim.

## Feed production queue

Prioritize these twelve entries as finished feed packages: Days **3, 5, 10, 12, 17, 19, 22, 24, 26, 29 and 30**, plus Day **1** as the campaign opener. Render the remaining placements with reusable Story templates so frequency does not dilute the visual standard.

Every package needs a content ID (`SHN-AURA-D01-AM`, `SHN-AURA-D01-PM`, and so on), final asset, caption, alt text or subtitles, source/claim check, rights record and internal approval revision. A calendar entry is `Draft`; it is not scheduled or published until the platform returns verifiable evidence.

## What to measure

Record reach, saves, shares, profile visits, follows, Story replies and poll response counts at comparable ages. For practice prompts, saves and voluntary replies indicate content usefulness only; they do not prove that a practice occurred or changed someone. After 30 days, keep the three strongest visual structures and the three clearest audience prompts. Remove anything that earned attention by exaggerating the claim.
