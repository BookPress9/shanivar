## First render procedure

1. Manager locks one [pilot brief](ugc-pilot-2026-09-30/briefs.md), source snapshot and exact spoken wording. Inspect connected credit balance, generation estimate and authorized resource budget before a charge. No auto-top-up or new subscription.
2. Producer confirms stock-avatar license or original character route and submits the first shot/presenter draft. Preserve request ID, model, prompt and resulting files. Review before rendering the rest; at most two candidates per shot within the established budget.
3. Editor assembles the actual clips, adds exact captions, truthful AI disclosure, room tone/cleared sound and a cover. Export final MP4 and SRT. Watch the full output and decode it; keep failed candidates out of the publishing folder.
4. Manager checks claims, voice, readability, authenticity/disclosure and source revision. Publisher records the approved file hashes, final caption, account, actual authorization and proposed time; then uses the connected service and captures its job/post IDs.
5. If access/rendering fails by T−24 hours, manager substitutes a reviewed reserve carousel in that slot. Move the video to a new proposed slot with an explicit blocker. Do not call a text animation or slideshow completed UGC.

Use a legible opening label and caption disclosure for realistic synthetic footage, plus the current platform disclosure setting where required. Verify platform-specific rules at the point of publication. Ordinary staged narration is allowed; invented customer outcomes, misleading app demonstrations and unconsented likeness/voice use are excluded from the creative brief.

Tool evaluation is intentionally limited to current primary documentation and actual integration discovery. No video has been generated, account connected, subscription purchased or post scheduled by this document.
