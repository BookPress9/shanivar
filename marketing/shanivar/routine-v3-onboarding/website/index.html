The problem

Stop leaving your life to chance.

You follow calendars for meetings, deadlines and other people. Then you expect your own growth to survive on memory and mood.

That is backwards. Put it on the calendar.

And there is the other part. Boredom, effort or discomfort shows up, and the hand is already reaching for the phone. You opened it for one thing. What was it?

Shanivar is not here to keep you busy. It is here to make what matters harder to ignore.

Strict, the way a good coach is strict.

High standards. A clear next action. An expectation that you do the work. No pep talk, no wellness wallpaper, no shame when a day goes sideways. A missed day is information. Start again tomorrow.

Strict does not mean caged. Every boundary in Shanivar is one you set, and one you can stop.

Routine · in development

Wake. Focus. Protect time. Put your personal work on the calendar.

Routine is the first place Shanivar opens. Here is the setup flow as it is being built. It is not released and has not finished testing on real phones yet.

  1. Your day

    Give the day two fixed points.

    Not an ideal morning. Your actual life.

    Two questions to start: I usually wake around and I make room for sleep around. Your answers place the day you can actually follow.

    • Add personal activities with a name, time, duration and Every day, Weekly or Custom days.
    • Gym, Read a book and Extend Sadhana are just editable starting points.
    • Activities organise your calendar. They never set an alarm or start protection on their own.

  2. Guardrails

    Set it once. Then get on with life.

    Two tools. Both editable. Both optional. Neither should need your attention every day.

    • Wake alarm: start when you said you would. It can use your wake anchor. The wake screen may ask you to stand or move, but an accessible exit with no physical task is always there, and a hard time limit always ends the ringing.
    • App protection: choose what loses access to you. Pick distracting apps once, then set protection windows: several a day, across the week, overnight if you need. Every day, Weekdays, Weekends or Custom.

  3. Honest boundaries

    Voluntary and stoppable. Never unbreakable.

    You already know which apps take more than they give. Select them once.

    • Essential access stays available. Calls and system apps are never selected.
    • Stop protection anytime: no waiting, no payment, no forced practice, no server.
    • It is designed to be hard to undo from bed, not impossible to undo. A determined person can always get past an app on their own phone, for example from Android Settings, a browser or a second device. We say so plainly.

  4. Saved is not Active

    The app says only what is true.

    A saved plan is not proof that protection is on. Each window shows its real state, and "Active" is meant to appear only when the phone confirms it.

    • Saved
    • Needs device access
    • Ready
    • Active
    • Needs attention
    • Off

    If you decline a permission, your plan is kept. Shanivar will say it needs device access. It will not pretend you are protected.

Today

Routine combines your wake and sleep anchors, your activities and your device boundaries into a single readable day. Only device-backed items, the alarm and protection windows, carry a status. It does not calculate an "ideal" biological timetable. The schedule follows your stated life.

Sadhana · in development

Do the work. Need fewer prompts.

Train attention until the practice can leave the phone and enter your life. The practice is working when you can carry it without the player.

Steady · natural breath · as designed

Natural breath. Nothing else.

Do not make it deeper. Do not slow it down. Do not count it. Attention will move. When it does, return to the next breath.

  • One minute, after you confirm you are still and somewhere suitable.
  • Shanivar does not pace the breath or ask you to hold it.
  • Pause or end at any time. Reflection afterwards is optional, and "it felt uncomfortable" is a valid answer.

What is decided, what is not

Progression waits.

Steady is the only Sadhana practice in internal builds today. Its content still needs review before any public release.

How Sadhana grows from here is still being designed with the founder. Two things hold already: no streaks, points or ranks in this build, and time in the app never unlocks more intense practice.

Less reliance on Shanivar can be a good outcome.

The stance

Notice the mind. Don't control it.

Notice what is already happening inside, before you react. Then choose what comes next.

  • A patient teacher.It notices something precise. It offers a question. It may suggest one small practice. Then it gives you space to find out for yourself.
  • Your judgement stays yours.You can disagree, skip, change direction or stop. Stopping is a valid answer, not resistance to overcome.
  • Leave more present.The aim is not more time in Shanivar. The aim is that you leave Shanivar more present than when you opened it.

This is the moment to change it.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Can I download Shanivar now?

No. Shanivar is in development for Android. There is no public release and no Play Store listing yet. Join the early-access list and you will be invited when there is a build to test.

Is it on iPhone?

No. Shanivar is being built for Android only.

Can it lock me out of my phone?

No. Essential access stays available, and calls and system apps are never selected. You can stop protection from inside Shanivar at any time, with no waiting, no payment and no practice required first.

Is app protection impossible to get around?

No, and we will never claim it is. It is meant to be deliberately hard to undo in a weak moment. Someone determined can still get past it, for example through Android Settings, a browser or another phone. Shanivar does not use device-administrator tricks to stop you from uninstalling it. Your phone stays yours.

Do I need an internet connection?

The wake alarm, protection windows and Stop protection are being built to run on your phone without needing a network or a server. That is still being verified on real devices.

Is this a meditation app?

Not in the usual sense. Sadhana starts with Steady, a one-minute practice with your natural breath. The goal is that you can do it without the app.

Does it use astrology?

No. Astrology is not part of what is being built now. The Saturn image on this site is a visual motif only.

What will it cost?

Pricing has not been decided, and nothing on this site is a price. One thing is ruled out: Shanivar will never charge you to end a protection window early.
