1Wake alarm

  • Rings louder in steps, through Do Not Disturb.
  • Dismiss by walking. QR code or photo also possible.
  • An accessible way out, with no physical task, is always on screen.
  • Stops by itself after 15 minutes at most.

2Protection window

  • Choose distracting apps once. Calls and system apps can't be chosen.
  • Up to eight windows: every day, weekdays, weekends or custom, overnight too.
  • Open a chosen app and Shanivar's screen appears. A brief flash is possible.
  • Needs Usage access and Display over other apps.
  • Stop protection anytime: no network, payment, timer or practice.
  • Hard to undo from bed. Not impossible.
