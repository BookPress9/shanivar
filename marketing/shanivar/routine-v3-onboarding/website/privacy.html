Privacy

Privacy notice (draft)

Last edited 6 October 2026. Applies to this website and to the Shanivar Android app in development.

This website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • The early-access form is collected by Netlify Forms, the service that hosts this site. It stores your email, your optional phone model, your 18+ confirmation and the campaign tag you arrived with. We use it only to invite you to early testing and never sell it. Reply to any invite to have your entry deleted.
  • If you arrive through a link with a campaign tag (for example ?utm_source=instagram), the page reads it in your browser only, to note where a sign-up came from. If you submit the form, that tag is stored with your sign-up.
  • This site is hosted on Netlify, which keeps standard server logs.

The app: designed to be local-first

  • Your Routine plan stays on your phone. Wake and sleep times, activities, selected apps and protection windows are stored locally on the device.
  • Device boundaries do not depend on a server. The phone is the authority for when an alarm or protection window runs, and Stop protection works locally.
  • No cloud backup of app data. The app is set to opt out of Android's automatic backup.
  • Steady keeps practice data local. The breathing practice makes no network calls, and a feeling you choose is not stored.
  • No analytics or push messaging in the app today. Anything added later, such as crash reporting, must be described here before release.

Device access the app asks for

  • Alarms & reminders: to schedule the wake alarm and protection boundaries exactly.
  • Usage access: to notice when a selected app is open so protection can respond.
  • Display over other apps: to show the protection screen.
  • Notifications: so the wake screen can appear.

These are optional until you want the alarm or protection to work. Declining never deletes your saved plan.

Accounts

Some app features can use a Shanivar account on a server, which can include preferences, alarm and protection schedules.

Contact

For privacy questions until a dedicated address is published, reply to any early-access email.
