About

Built by someone who needed it first.

I built Shanivar because I needed it: to hold a routine and stop losing hours to the phone.

The Hybrid Monk

Shanivar is made by The Hybrid Monk.

The practical problem is ordinary. Boredom, effort or discomfort appears, and the hand reaches for quick stimulation. The founder sees that unfilled moment as worth keeping. Presence starts with being able to notice the urge without immediately acting on it.

Shanivar is meant for people who are already willing to look at their own experience. It does not ask you to adopt anyone's worldview, including the founder's. It should help you examine your experience, not hand you someone else's conclusions about it.

If Shanivar works deeply, you may eventually need it rarely, or not at all. Use is a means, not the goal.

How we work

  • Honest status. The app should say what the phone actually confirms. "Saved" is not "Active".
  • No invented proof. No made-up testimonials, user numbers or results, on this site or anywhere else.
  • Traditions kept distinct. Where Shanivar draws on contemplative traditions, it names them and does not merge them into one claim.
  • Your phone stays yours. Strong boundaries, but always essential access, a deliberate way out and clear recovery.
