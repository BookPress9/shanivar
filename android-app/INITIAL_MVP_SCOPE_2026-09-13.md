# Shanivar v1: alarms, focus modes and presence

Status: founder-revised scope, 13 September 2026; implementation pending. Alarms and app locks are both required. This supersedes the earlier focus-only PM recommendation. Read the [Claude task](CLAUDE_MVP_STREAMLINE_TASK_2026-09-13.md), [marketing handoff](../marketing/shanivar/v1-product-direction-handoff.md) and [competitor reference](../research/shanivar-v1-alarm-focus-patterns.md).

## Offer and audience

**Begin when you choose. Protect time for what matters.**

Alarms help begin a chosen action. Focus protects chosen time. Preserve **one chosen activity → protected time → optional pause → brief review**. Neither tool requires the other; no automatic alarm-to-lock chain.

The founder intends these tools across ages: children, younger self-directed users, parents and older adults. Worried adult children can introduce willing parents; participants own their controls and private information. Age does not establish need, faith or mobility.

Build readable, age-inclusive controls and non-walking alarm dismissal now. The existing adult shakedown is the first validation step, not a permanent audience exclusion. School-age availability has an explicit readiness gate: report unresolved age-appropriate account, consent, privacy and comprehension requirements before claiming readiness for every age. Do not silently create a minors account flow or claim all-age validation.

Offer optional, changeable Lord Shani-inspired framing around discipline, responsibility and attention. No divine impersonation, punishment, mandatory belief or assumed adoption. Apply the [tone guide](../docs/strategy/shanivar-tone-and-values.md); no guaranteed hyperfocus, enlightenment or intelligence-ranking claims.

## First use

1. Welcome, English/Hindi choice, self-use entry and a secondary helper guide ending in handback to the parent. Make optional Shani framing discoverable without another mandatory page.
2. Optional pre-account one-minute local pause: “This pause does not block other apps.” Reuse the exercise; users ready for a tool can skip directly to it.
3. One home with **Start focus** and **Set alarm**, showing real states. Prioritize a ringing alarm or active focus without hiding the other's management. No broad content tabs.
4. Account and permissions only where the selected tool actually needs them. No birth/chart intake, role quiz, feature tour, paywall or compulsory review.
5. Complete and review all core strings, errors and permission explanations for each pilot language. Hindi-speaking recruitment requires complete Hindi; English-only results do not establish Hindi parent fit.

## Focus modes: required

- Editable Study/work, Reading/quiet and Family time presets on one engine. Presets suggest activities/durations, not different enforcement strength. Begin with one target; support a small selected app set through the existing package-list contract. Never default to essential communication or system apps.
- Confirm activity, whole-app targets and duration; start immediately through existing status/start/release contracts. Remember the last plan for the correct identity. Saved is not scheduled or active. Automatic recurring focus schedules remain later.
- Truthful checking, permission, starting, active, ending, complete and failure states. Reconcile restart, expiry, revocation and uncertain network outcomes.
- Block surface recalls the plan. “Return to my day” goes to device home. Optional local pause never unlocks; deliberate early release remains inside Shanivar, with no five-more-minutes control on the block surface.
- One skippable quick-answer review and clear exit. Preview completion is not protected activation.
- Whole-app coverage only: no feed-only, browser-blocking or impossible-to-bypass promise. Preserve the existing no-Device-Admin/no-accessibility-service enforcement stance.

## Alarms: required

- One editable wake-up alarm initially: time, selected repeat days, on/off, next occurrence, short intention and test sound. Multiple independent alarms remain later.
- Destination cue and modest step target, or an accessible non-walking dismissal. Reuse movement detection; no required QR/camera. Counted movement does not verify arrival at a room or prove someone is fully awake.
- Show sound readiness and relevant permissions at alarm setup. Confirm a next occurrence only after native scheduling succeeds; reconcile backend status and distinguish sync failure from device scheduling.
- Repeat days require durable next-occurrence scheduling, including reboot, clock and timezone changes. Alarm recurrence is in scope; automatic focus scheduling is not.
- Preserve bounded escalation, synchronous ringer stop before teardown/network, offline dismissal and independent maximum-ring watchdog. Sensor failure exposes the accessible alternative immediately. The non-walking alternative cannot require standing or walking even where older wording conflicts.
- Alarm-only use needs no focus setup or app-lock permissions. Focus-only use needs no alarm permissions. An alarm remains manageable during focus, and dismissal never silently starts protection.

## Later

Astrology, charts, AI scroller/memory, large practice library, payments, WhatsApp/referrals, family dashboards, gamification and apparel integration remain outside v1. Also defer camera/QR missions, sleep tracking, wake-up rechecks, multiple alarms, automatic focus schedules and complex mode rules. Preserve code/data and safe migration. See the [backlog](../docs/strategy/shanivar-launch-backlog.md).

## Verification and learning

- Demonstrate alarm-only, focus-only and combined use on physical hardware. No unmanaged services or misleading readiness.
- Verify selected/unselected apps, release, process recovery, account isolation, large text/TalkBack and offline preview.
- Verify alarm fire while locked/backgrounded, stop before network failure, sensor failure/non-walking fallback, hard cap, edit/cancel, repeat recurrence, reboot and clock changes. No local emulator; report physical gaps explicitly.
- Start with three willing parents introduced by adult children and three independent young adults. Test both tools, record assistance and independent repeat separately, then prioritize family invitation learning. Use the [worksheet](../ai_org/shanivar_mvp_pilot_worksheet.md).
- Track alarm schedule/fire/dismissal independently of focus start/completion and self-reported chosen activity. Alarm dismissal is not proof of waking; inactivity is not proof of independence. Test voluntary transfer later.

Pitch: **Shanivar helps you begin with a chosen alarm action, protect time from distracting apps, and notice what helps you stay with your intention. Families can introduce it; each person stays in control.** The family/Shani proposition remains a hypothesis, not proof of an unoccupied market.
