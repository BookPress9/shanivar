## Constraints this draft follows (checked by reading and by a unit test on banned phrases)
- Stationary and safe setting only (safe-setting note and "I'm somewhere safe and still" chip before Begin).
- Unpaced. Step 3 (scan) mentions no breath at all. No counted or directed breath, no "deep breath", no "breathe into", no holds.
- The one breath word is in step 4: an OPTIONAL, skippable, quiet "I allow" on the exhale. Never required.
- Eyes closed only "if that's comfortable". Nothing is asked of memories, emotions, pain, or injury.
- Skip and End on every step. The middle (steps 2-4) is one 3-minute timer: Settle 0:00-0:30, Scan 0:30-2:00, Stay 2:00-3:00.
- No claim of effect, release, healing, health, blood flow, nervous system, pain relief, energy, awakening or progress.
- Step 5's line is the person's own (60 characters, this phone only, never synced or scored). No streaks, scores or counts.

## Script as built (each step has Skip and End; step 1's End reads "Not now")
1. **Stop.** "Find a safe place for three minutes." "You can end this at any time." + safe-setting note + chip "I'm somewhere safe and still". Begin / Skip / Not now.
2. **Settle.** "Close your eyes if that's comfortable." "Notice how your body is held up."
3. **Scan, top to bottom.** "Head, face, jaw, neck, shoulders, chest, stomach, hands, legs, feet." "Notice where there's sensation, and where there's none."
4. **Stay with one spot.** "Pick the strongest sensation." "No need to fix or explain it. Let it be there." Optional: "Optional: on the exhale, you may quietly say "I allow". Or leave this out."
5. **Where do you want to reach?** "Your own words. One line, up to 60 characters." Keep / Clear / Skip / End.
6. **The time has passed.** "Where you want to reach: <their line>" Done.
