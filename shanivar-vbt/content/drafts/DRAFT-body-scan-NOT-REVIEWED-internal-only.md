## Constraints this draft follows (checked by reading, not by a tool)
- Stationary and safe setting only. Not driving, cycling, in water, or operating anything.
- Unpaced. The breath is never mentioned as something to do, count, change or follow.
- Eyes open is allowed. Nothing is asked of memories, emotions, pain, or injury.
- Every area is optional. "Skip" and "stop" are named as fine at the start and the end.
- No claim of effect, release, healing, energy, awakening, or progress. No goal feeling.
- About 3 minutes if read slowly with pauses (about 10 short steps, roughly 15 to 18 seconds each).

## Script (for the founder's own voice; each block is one beat, leave a pause after it)

**Arrive**
1. "Sit somewhere safe. You can keep your eyes open or let them rest on one spot."
2. "Whatever is happening, you can stop at any moment. Skipping a part is fine."

**Scan (move at your own pace; any part can be skipped)**
3. "Notice where your body meets what holds it up. Feet, seat, back, or whatever is touching."
4. "Notice your feet. Just that they are there. Warm, cool, or nothing in particular is all fine."
5. "Let attention rest on your legs, wherever they are."
6. "Let attention rest on your hips and the base of your spine, where your weight settles."
7. "Notice your belly and chest, as they are. There is nothing to change."
8. "Notice your hands and arms, and where they rest."
9. "Notice your shoulders, neck and jaw. Nothing needs to loosen. Just notice."
10. "Notice your face, and the top of your head."

**Close**
11. "Let attention take in the whole body at once, sitting here."
12. "Look around the room when you are ready. Notice something you can see."
