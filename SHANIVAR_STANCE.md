## Founder instructions already recorded

The latest brief directly instructs us to help people move from automatic mental loops toward embodied observation and conscious choice; preserve the patient-teacher voice; leave authority with the practitioner; distinguish traditions and evidence; retain astrology only as an optional interpretive lens; expand research internationally; and make depth available without requiring it. These are recorded product intentions, **not empirical or metaphysical findings**.

Those instructions supersede the authority claim and six-level ranking in [the historical ontology](ai_org/ontology/philosophy.md). They also supersede India-only assumptions for long-term research scope, while preserving existing willing parent and younger self-use audiences. They do not authorize releasing unreviewed content, rewriting the whole application, fabricating a guru persona or changing enforcement safety.

**Accepted product form, 30 September 2026:** [decision 0014](docs/decisions/0014-routine-sadhana-shell-and-routine-v3.md) establishes Routine and Sadhana as the two primary destinations, removes Dashboard, makes Routine the normal launch destination and authorizes Routine implementation. Sadhana progression remains a separate founder collaboration. This product-form acceptance does not convert any ST entry below from PROPOSED to ACCEPTED.

## ST-001 — Mind models remain tradition-specific

**Topic:** Manovigyana as a research umbrella, not one ancient psychology.

**Status:** PROPOSED

**Traditional source:** Separate textual families; see the [tradition matrix](research/deep-foundation/TRADITION_SEPARATION_MATRIX.md).

**Tradition:** Sāṃkhya, Pātañjala Yoga, distinct Vedāntas, Buddhist traditions and Śaiva/Śākta traditions, not one school.

**Primary text:** Named passages catalogued in the ancient source ledger; no synthesized ancient quotation.

**Commentary:** Each commentary is attributed to its school/author and kept separate from the text.

**Modern scholarship:** [Ancient knowledge map](research/deep-foundation/ANCIENT_KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md) and [psychology bridge](research/deep-foundation/MODERN_PSYCHOLOGY_BRIDGE.md).

**What the sources actually say:** The maps identify different accounts of mind, selfhood, cognition and release. A shared word does not guarantee a shared referent.

**Areas of disagreement:** Self/no-self, consciousness, ultimate reality, aims of practice and the authority of sources.

**Shanivar interpretation:** Use comparison as inquiry; never claim that all schools secretly assert the same metaphysics.

**Product implication:** Preserve tradition/text namespaces beneath a plain interface.

**Practice implication:** Similar-looking exercises retain distinct purposes and adaptation notes.

**Claim limitations:** A brief exercise does not adjudicate philosophical disagreement.

**Founder decision:** Pending; no acceptance inferred from commissioning research.

**Date accepted:** Not accepted.

**Topic:** Different anchors for different circumstances.

**Tradition:** Source-specific adaptations and separately labelled modern exercises.

**Claim limitations:** No universal safety, anxiety/insomnia treatment or nervous-system engineering promise.
