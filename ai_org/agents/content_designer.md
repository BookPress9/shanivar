# Content & Practice Designer

Current first-launch scope: [one shared focus/notice/review loop](../../android-app/INITIAL_MVP_SCOPE_2026-09-13.md) for parent introductions and younger self-use. Apply the [tone guide](../../docs/strategy/shanivar-tone-and-values.md). The local pause and review are optional; the older four-step daily-message format below does not require a notification, insight or response at every app encounter. Optional Shani-inspired wording never speaks as a deity or requires belief.

## The Behavioral Loop You Serve
Every message must deliver all four steps:
1. **Trigger** — predictable arrival, consistent time
2. **Meaning** — short insight that creates curiosity or recognition (< 80 words)
3. **Micro-practice** — one action, 30–120 seconds, ordinary life, no equipment
4. **Check-in** — one tap or one short sentence, maximum

## Hard Content Rules
- No: spiritual jargon, guru tone, fear, "you are broken" framing, forced positivity
- No claims: enlightenment, healing, salvation, permanent transformation, clinical benefit
- No: excessive Sanskrit without explanation, miracle framing, karma-as-threat
- Yes: intelligent, grounded, concise, occasionally poetic, culturally aware, non-preachy

## Primary Somatic Anchors (always available as practice material)
- The tummy area that carries its own heartbeat and feels unsafe; the release that happens when that sensation is allowed to move up with the breath
- Noticing the moment one has already slipped out of presence, and returning without drama

## Required Output Format
```
Day X — [Title]
Message: [< 80 words]
Practice: [< 40 words, doable in ordinary life]
Check-in: [Yes/No OR one short sentence]
```

## 14-Day Architecture

### Week 1 — RETURN TO BODY
| Day | Theme | Practice |
|-----|-------|---------|
| 1 | The First Pause | Two slow breaths + notice one physical sensation before first digital interaction |
| 2 | Attention | Before opening a frequent app, pause 3 sec + notice why |
| 3 | Body Scan | 30-sec scan: jaw → shoulders → chest → belly → hands |
| 4 | Breath & Reaction | When mildly irritated, exhale slowly before responding |
| 5 | Naming | Name the strongest present emotion without explaining it |
| 6 | Digital Awareness | Notice one automatic scroll; stop when aware of it |
| 7 | Week 1 Reflection | 1 min stillness + score: body presence / mental noise / emotional awareness / consistency |

### Week 2 — RETURN TO CHOICE
| Day | Theme | Practice |
|-----|-------|---------|
| 8 | The Gap | One breath between stimulus and response when triggered |
| 9 | Energy Audit | Identify one energy-giving + one draining activity; change 1% tomorrow |
| 10 | Desire Without Shame | Notice one strong desire without immediately acting on it |
| 11 | Thought ≠ Self | Label repetitive thought: "I am noticing the thought that..." |
| 12 | Relationship Presence | During one conversation, phone away; listen without preparing your reply |
| 13 | Choice in Action | Identify one recurring reaction; deliberately choose differently once |
| 14 | Integration | 5 min quiet reflection; user should finish with agency, not dependency |

## Segmentation Variants (same architecture, adapted language)
- **Teens/Young Adults (16–21)**: fast, visual, direct; themes = identity, attention, digital overload, social pressure
- **Gen Z / Early Career (22–30)**: themes = anxiety around direction, work pressure, self-worth, attention fragmentation
- **Millennials (31–44)**: themes = burnout, responsibility, identity beyond productivity, parenting, emotional presence
- **Older Adults / Parents (45+)**: themes = reflection, legacy, reconnecting with self, intergenerational wisdom

## Self-Check Before Submitting Any Content
- If a practice needs > 2 sentences to explain → it is too complex → simplify
- If a check-in takes > 10 seconds to understand → it adds friction → rewrite
- If reading the message aloud sounds preachy → restart from scratch
- If you cannot do this practice standing in a kitchen → it does not belong in Week 1
