# Proposed foundation experiments

29 September 2026. **No experiments executed.** Designs and numerical gates below are proposed formative learning rules, not validated benchmarks, statistical power calculations or predicted results. Owners must agree primary outcomes before recruitment. Safety-critical failure stops the relevant test irrespective of averages.

## Shared research discipline

Recruit willing adults for the first study, preserving the wider age ambition without claiming minor readiness. Consent and compensation do not depend on praise, task completion or continued use. Separate source/script review from usability research. Participants may stop, choose an external anchor or decline a question. Do not ask people to induce distress or demonstrate real emergencies. Record assistance, missing observations and withdrawals rather than excluding inconvenient cases.

Keep observation, device state and self-report distinct. A timer ended is not proof of meditation; a successful block is not proof of a chosen task; an app uninstall is not automatically failure or independence. Collect the minimum data, avoid private app contents, set retention/deletion dates, and obtain separate consent for any public quote. No outreach or data collection is authorized by this proposed protocol alone.

## EX-01 — Minimum useful first experience

- **Source/confidence:** UX-01, existing Steady chooser, progressive disclosure; high for current multi-choice structure, low for comparative benefit.
- **Principle/product:** experience before irrelevant setup, while preserving informed choice.
- **Design:** eight adults, counterbalanced within-person tasks on two otherwise matched prototypes: current chooser vs direct external-anchor offer with Change/source access. Do not combine this with new branding or AI personalization.
- **Primary signal:** unaided start and end with accurate explanation of what the exercise asked. Proposed gate: at least six of eight succeed per variant; report individuals and assistance, not only an average.
- **Secondary:** time to instruction, number of decisions/help requests, anchor choice and optional depth.
- **Failure:** safety context obscured, inability to stop, pressure to breathe, wrong assumptions about treatment or source. Stop for any unsafe routing.
- **Decision:** TEST; prefer the simpler route only if it remains understandable and suitable. Prototype success cannot close native lifecycle gates.

## EX-02 — Provenance and chosen depth

- **Source/confidence:** tradition matrix + J-01 + audience evidence; high need for truthful attribution, unknown preferred disclosure.
- **Principle/product:** source visibility without doctrine-first onboarding.
- **Design:** 12 adults with differing practice experience, counterbalanced concise attribution vs upfront detailed explanation of the same reviewed adaptation. Include curious non-practitioners and established practitioners; do not assign levels.
- **Primary:** can the person distinguish traditional passage, commentator interpretation and modern script? Can they find more context and decline it?
- **Success:** at least ten explain the distinction and find the requested depth without moderator instruction; report differences rather than infer population prevalence.
- **Failure:** source erasure, fabricated authenticity, confusion between a worldview and a measured result, or forced depth.
- **Decision:** TEST; ST-001 acceptance is a separate founder choice.

## EX-03 — International demand and cultural comprehension

- **Source/confidence:** audience report; mixed surveys/small studies, low direct Shanivar demand evidence.
- **Principle/product:** recruit by lived situation and investigate language/trust instead of assigning them.
- **Design:** 18 discovery interviews across India, diaspora and international non-Indian explorers, six in each provisional quota; include app abandoners. Problem interview before pitch; optional subsequent prototype task. Preferred language must be recorded, not guessed.
- **Primary:** concrete recurring situation, current workaround and independently described reason to attempt a practice. Report cases and disconfirming evidence; no conversion forecast.
- **Secondary:** terminology comprehension, trust signals, actual prior purchases, willingness to attempt, desired community role. WTP remains hypothetical unless an honest authorized paid offer is tested later.
- **Failure:** interviewer has to teach the problem; respondents like philosophy but cannot identify a useful action; cultural framing feels appropriative or coercive.
- **Decision:** TEST as discovery, not market validation. Founder later chooses language/geography based on evidence and capability.

## EX-04 — Focus reliability before stronger modes

- **Source/confidence:** current receiver/recurrence code and engineering review; high static concern, physical outcomes unverified.
- **Principle/product:** Scheduled is distinct from Active; stale boundaries must not create unexpected restrictions.
- **Design:** engineering tests plus physical devices for START delivered after END, cold process, restart, reboot, offline, expired auth, denied/revoked permissions, midnight, DST/timezone and interacting manual/scheduled sessions. No emulator on this machine.
- **Primary:** restriction only while the chosen effective rule is valid; deterministic local release/recovery; truthful status.
- **Success:** every enumerated invariant verified with raw evidence and no false Active state. No pass-by-average.
- **Failure:** unintended long session, wrong-rule release, inability to recover, or status falsely claiming protection.
- **Decision:** HOLD persistent claims and larger pilots until corrected and tested. This is an engineering gate, not a clinical experiment.

## EX-05 — Essential-access recovery

- **Source/confidence:** founder emergency requirement, current stance tension, competitor limitations; high importance, untested interaction.
- **Principle/product:** deliberate boundaries cannot consume or condition emergency access.
- **Design:** after founder accepts the exact recovery design, simulate mistaken app selection with nonessential dummy content, including offline/restart states and users with assistive input. Never create a real urgent need or block a real essential tool for the experiment.
- **Primary:** every participant reaches the intended local recovery path unaided; actual calling/essential access verified separately with safe test procedures.
- **Failure:** timer, quota, payment, server, inaccessible gesture, physical task or stale callback prevents recovery. Any case halts testing.
- **Decision:** revise until recoverable; do not trade a safety failure against better retention.

## EX-06 — Voluntary return and useful departure

- **Source/confidence:** founder independence goal and behavioral bridge; low evidence of lasting Shanivar transfer.
- **Principle/product:** support should fit life rather than become another dependency.
- **Design:** after source/device gates and useful first-loop evidence, 14-day voluntary ordinary Focus/practice pilot with optional 3-day baseline and 7-/28-day follow-up. Participants choose support levels; compensation independent of adherence.
- **Primary:** self-reported chosen activity and usefulness, paired with verified technical operation where available. Ask why a boundary or practice was changed or discontinued.
- **Secondary:** off-app use of an anchor, assistance required, unwanted dependence, regret, disabled reminders and technical failures.
- **Failure:** continued use driven by guilt, reassurance-seeking or misunderstanding; dropouts classified without evidence.
- **Decision:** descriptive learning first; no causal persistence claim from a self-selected pilot. A powered intervention study is a separate design.

## EX-07 — Social as an experience prototype

- **Source/confidence:** [social protocol](UGC_AND_SOCIAL_EXPERIMENTS.md), existing voice; no current performance baseline.
- **Principle/product:** one recognizable moment can support the same voluntary action on Instagram and in the app.
- **Design:** exact same moment/format/language, question-only versus question-plus-small-external-invitation after creative/source review and explicit publication authorization. Use comparable 24-hour/7-day windows and disclose organic-delivery confounds.
- **Primary:** understandable voluntary reports of an actual attempt, not saves or views. Denominator is known respondents/exposure where available, with missing data explicit.
- **Secondary:** saves/shares per reach, qualified visits to a verified destination; no private disclosure requested.
- **Failure:** misunderstood medical/source claims, shame, false UGC, or high engagement without any evidence of use.
- **Decision:** TEST content comprehension; do not call it product efficacy or acquisition-market fit.

## Prioritization

First: exact content/claim review and EX-04/05 safety design/testing for any enforcement pilot. In parallel: non-enforcing EX-01/02 prototypes and EX-03 discovery once approved and ready. Then EX-06 with voluntary follow-up. EX-07 follows actual product availability and authorized production. Ancient-text research can deepen throughout; there is no need to delay all learning until every philosophical question is settled.
