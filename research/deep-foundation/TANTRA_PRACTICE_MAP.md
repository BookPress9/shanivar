Exact VBT coverage stays in the [25 September inventory](../shanivar-vbt-breathing-catalog-2026-09-25.md); do not generate a second contradictory verse index.

## Keep three maps separate

1. **Historical map:** community, text, ritual system, period and commentary. This describes where an instruction belongs.
2. **Experiential map:** sensory target and attentional action. This helps explain what an adaptation asks a user to do.
3. **Product map:** comfort, setting, duration, consent, exit and purpose. This is modern design and does not retroactively become traditional teaching.

An instruction can be historically well-attested yet unsuitable for a generic app. A modern grounding exercise can be useful to test without receiving an invented Sanskrit origin. Neither authenticity nor simplicity alone demonstrates clinical safety or effectiveness.

## Important VBT provenance correction

The beginner feature's no-hold policy is a **deliberate modification**. Wallis's 24–27 commentary includes instructions and interpretations involving breath pauses, retention and subtle centers. Replacing them with unpaced observation changes the practice. The public origin note should say so, without making beginners study the removed details. [A13; commentary](https://hareesh.org/blog/2022/11/30/vijana-bhairava-tantra-verses-22-27-the-foundational-practice).

Likewise, substituting ordinary mental words for a sacred breath-mantra is original adaptation; a generic tone is not an ancient recording; contextualizing emotions for phone use is a modern recommender hypothesis. Do not use a traditional source citation as evidence that any of these exact app scripts were historically prescribed for attention switching or circadian support.

## Editorial record required for every proposed practice

Store: stable ID; status; original script version; target experience; traditional source ID or `modern_synthesis`; exact verse label; translation edition/date; commentary dependency; elements retained; elements omitted; elements added; intended setting; anchor preferences; stop/switch route; review owner; rights status; evidence tier; success/failure observations; founder decision and date.

Keep `textual_confidence`, `usability_confidence` and `benefit_evidence` separate. A source can be clear while usability is untested; an enjoyable experience does not validate a metaphysical claim. A user's positive feedback does not unlock a more intense protocol.

## Context rules proposed

- Offer a stationary, ordinary setting; never ask for inward absorption while driving or operating equipment.
- Let breath, sound, contact and room-orientation preferences override recommendation logic. “Not for me” is valid evidence, not resistance to overcome.
- Do not require eyes closed, controlled breathing, a special posture or emotional disclosure.
- At bedtime reduce interaction and allow immediate sleep. Time-of-day labels use user schedule and intent; they do not measure circadian phase.
- A strong emotion calls for less demand and an easy exit. No app exercise is a requirement before emergency access, essential communication or an alarm dismissal.
- Sensitive or disorienting experiences stop the experiment and trigger review; they are never celebrated as purification or advancement.

These are proposed product boundaries, not comprehensive clinical screening criteria. Any later intervention study or higher-intensity program needs an appropriately scoped independent review.
