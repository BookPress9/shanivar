## Deliberately not used as authoritative evidence

Search surfaced forum debates about cultural appropriation and meditation meaning. They helped identify questions but were not systematically sampled, verified or used to estimate audience prevalence. No personal profiles or comments were copied into the dataset. A Hong Kong mixed-methods study was located (DOI 10.1007/s12671-025-02690-w), but the primary full record did not load in this pass; no numerical claim from secondary snippets enters the report. These access gaps are follow-up candidates, not hidden confirmations.
