# Social media as a small experience, not just promotion

29 September 2026. **PROPOSED** experiments. No posts, messages, recruitment, payments, referrals or live schedules were executed. Existing [voice](../../marketing/shanivar/voice-direction.md) and [creative learning log](../../marketing/shanivar/creative-learning-log.md) remain the editorial baseline; the audience now includes international explorers by founder direction.

## What research allows us to claim

An actual product experience can support a truthful personal account. A scripted founder concept is not a customer testimonial. The FTC's current endorsement guidance requires honest experiences and clear disclosure of material connections; it is a US source, not a worldwide compliance certification. Before an international paid campaign, check the applicable local rules. [FTC endorsement guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking).

The reviewed product sources document scheduled boundaries and interventions, but they do not prove which story will acquire Shanivar users. Our local campaign ledger contains no new verified Insights. Therefore claims about conversion, optimal posting times, audience preference or “winning” hooks remain unavailable. This is a proposed learning program, not a growth forecast.

## NOTICE → QUESTION → PRACTICE prototype

Original concept for a future approved post:

**NOTICE:** “You opened the phone to check the time. You are still here.”

**QUESTION:** “What were you about to do?”

**PRACTICE:** “If you are safely settled, notice one ordinary detail around you. Then choose what comes next.”

This is a modern external-attention invitation, not a VBT quotation or treatment. It asks for no breath holding, eye closure or sensitive disclosure. The source status should be recorded in the production manifest even when the caption stays short. A person who is driving or doing an attention-critical task should not be prompted to engage.

The product version should keep the same operation: recognizable context, optional small action, immediate exit. Do not convert the post into a long philosophical onboarding sequence just because the app has more room.

## Acquisition hypotheses and countermetrics

| Format | Honest premise | Evidence required | Useful signal | What would mislead or harm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transformation story | A real person describes a specific chosen boundary | Consent, actual build/use, original words, disclosed compensation | Interested viewers understand the concrete use case | Actor reading a fabricated “my life changed” testimonial |
| Before/after ritual | Compare a person's chosen morning routine over stated days | Same observation window, clear self-report, technical failures included | Voluntary account of a useful change | Implied causal cure or selective perfect days |
| Screen-time screenshot | A user elects to show their own aggregate statistic | Consent and redaction; same periods/device scope | Starts a useful discussion about agency | Treating reduced screen time as proof of meditation or health |
| Creator experiment | A creator tries a verified feature and reports what happened | Honest access, transparent sponsorship, freedom to criticize | Qualified interest and accurately understood limitations | Suppressing inconvenient outcomes or scripted endorsement |
| Focus challenge | An optional self-chosen boundary for a short period | Safe override, no essential-app lock, no public ranking | Chosen activity reports, not longest blockade | Escalating lock strength or rewarding refusal of emergency access |
| Milestone | A self-authored observation worth sharing | Opt-in preview, no sensitive app list/history by default | Meaningful voluntary sharing | “Advanced soul” badges or disguised consciousness scores |
| Referral | Invite someone who might find a utility useful | Existing useful experience, consentful recipient flow, clear incentive | Recipient's own choice and useful activation | Contact scraping, repeated DMs or guilt about helping parents |
| Waitlist | A clear future offer with accurate availability | Real destination, consent/withdrawal, tested form, named purpose | Eligible opt-in and later activated users | Counting page views or fake urgency as demand |

## First editorial test

**SOC-01:** compare the same everyday situation as a question-only post versus a question plus one brief external-attention invitation. This is a proposed test; do not change language, format, hook and audience all at once. Organic delivery is not randomized: equal 24-hour and 7-day windows make description more comparable but do not remove audience/algorithm confounds.

Primary learning: voluntary, explicitly self-reported attempts among exposed respondents. Report the numerator, known denominator and missing data; never claim a viewer attempted the practice from a save. Secondary: saves/shares per reach and qualified visits when exposed by account analytics. Countermetrics: confusion, shame, requests to stop, misunderstood medical/source claims and extra app consumption. Review actual wording people voluntarily use without copying private messages into the knowledge base.

**Success proposal:** enough concrete accounts to identify whether the instruction was understood and attempted, followed by a consented usability check. No arbitrary engagement uplift is a clinical or spiritual result. **Failure:** many saves but no understandable practice accounts; comments interpret the app as a guru or treatment; participants feel pressured to share private feelings. Decision: TEST only after content/availability review and publication authorization.

## Product ↔ social learning handoff

Social Media Manager submits a dated observation with post ID, exact copy/media revision, language, observed window, numerators/denominators and uncertainty. User Researcher separates comprehension and practice evidence from engagement. Practice Translator checks whether a useful wording change preserves context and safety. Textual Provenance checks attribution. Staff Manager records a PROPOSED change. Founder acceptance is required before an important stance change; content/release review remains separate.

Do not automatically fine-tune, update scripts or personalize from comments. Do not infer a diagnosis, religious preference or private mental state from engagement. Marketing and product may learn from the same question without making social media a covert assessment tool.
