# Instagram Content Calendar: Mon 19 Oct – Sun 15 Nov 2026

**File:** `/workspace/content-calendar.xlsx`, with one sheet per brand and a Guide sheet
20 posts per brand (5 per week). Each row has: date, IST post time, format, concept, 3-sec hook, solo phone shot list, on-screen text, caption, hashtags, audio, and audience value.

**Approach:** "Aura farming" here means calm, confident, unhurried visuals that still teach something real.
