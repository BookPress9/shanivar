## R01: Your Hand Moved First

**Concept name:** Your Hand Moved First
**Campaign phase:** 1, Recognition (Day 1, Sat 24 Oct)
**Audience insight:** People think they "decide" to check their phone. Most reaches start before any decision: at red lights, in lifts, mid-sentence, after checking the time.
**Saturn/Shani principle:** Slowness. *Śanaiścara*, "the one who moves slowly": slow enough to see the reach before it happens.
**Source and interpretation (INTERNAL: not for public copy):**
- **Source:** Monier-Williams, *Śani* "probably 'slow-moving'" (Wisdomlib).
- **Psychology:** notifications alone disrupted attention, comparable to using the phone (Stothart et al. 2015); self-control is most linked to automatic behaviour (de Ridder et al. 2012).
- **Shanivar reading:** "be the slow one" means watch the urge without obeying it for 10 seconds. A practice invitation, not a claim.
**Format:** Phone interruption (UGC format 3). Founder-shot POV hands; no face needed.
**Duration:** 30 s

**Hook:** Sound of a notification. The hand is already moving. Text: *Your hand moved before you did.*

**Full script word-for-word with timestamps** (VO = founder, low, unhurried, close to the mic):
- **0:00–0:03 (A, interruption).** SFX: one notification ping. No VO. Text appears.
- **0:03–0:12 (B, confrontation).** VO: "Red light. Unlock. Lift. Four floors of reels. Mid-sentence, while she's still talking. You checked the time. You still don't know the time."
- **0:12–0:20 (C, Saturn reframe).** VO: "In Sanskrit, Saturn has an old name. Shanaishchara. The one who moves slowly. Not late. Slow. Slow enough to see the reach coming."
- **0:20–0:27 (D, participation).** VO: "Don't scroll. Ten seconds. Watch your thumb." (Silence for 6 seconds.) VO: "What did it want?"
- **0:27–0:30 (E, Shanivar).** VO: "Be the slow one."

**Visual direction shot by shot (phone, 9:16, 4K/30 or 1080/60 for slow-mo inserts):**
1. Extreme close-up: phone face down on a dark wooden table; the screen glows through the edge on the ping. The hand enters frame **before** the second ping. Abrupt cut.
2. Car, red light, phone mount: the thumb unlocks (0.6 s). Hard cut.
3. Lift interior, floor numbers in the reflection: the thumb flicks reels (0.8 s). Hard cut.
4. Over-the-shoulder at a café: a friend talking, out of focus; the hand lifts the phone (0.8 s). Hard cut.
5. Clock widget opened and closed (0.5 s). Hard cut to black for 4 frames.
6. **Long take (8 s), locked off:** hand resting beside the face-down phone; window-grille shadow slowly sliding across the knuckles. Afternoon sun, real time, no speed ramp.
7. Top-down: the thumb hovering 1 cm above the screen of R01 itself. **Hold 6 s.** No movement. A thin brass line slowly draws across the bottom of the frame (graphic, no numbers).
8. Black frame. Thin gold Saturn ring mark fades in. Text.

**Performance direction:**
- Hands only, ordinary: chipped nail polish or a kada is fine.
- In shot 6 the hand must be truly relaxed, not posed. Shoot 3 takes and choose the least "acted".
- Founder VO: say it as if to one person at 1 a.m. No performance and no smile. Pause after "Slow."

**Sound design:**
- 0–12 s: real environment audio (traffic, lift chime, café murmur), cut hard with each picture cut.
- 12 s: **full sound drop-out** to room tone, with the faint tick of a wall clock.
- 20–27 s: complete silence (−60 dB room tone; not pure digital zero).
- 27 s: one low brass tone (singing-bowl-free; use a struck steel tumbler recorded at home).

**On-screen text with timing:**
- 0.0–3.0: *Your hand moved before you did.*
- 3.0–12.0: *red light · lift · mid-sentence · "what time is it"* (one word-group per cut)
- 12.5–20.0: *Śanaiścara: the one who moves slowly.*
- 20.0–27.0: *Don't scroll. 10 seconds. Watch your thumb.*
- 27.0–30.0: *Be the slow one.* / *SHANIVAR · in development for Android*

**Active-passive moment:** The world pings, the hand jumps (abrupt cuts), then 8 seconds of a still hand while only the shadow moves.
**Shanivar integration:** Brand mark and status only. No feature. (The in-app twin practice, "The moment before the reach", stays unmentioned in Phase 1.)
**CTA:** "What did your thumb want? One word in the comments."

**Caption ready to publish:**

## R02: Starting Monday Since March

**Concept name:** Starting Monday Since March
**Campaign phase:** 1, Recognition (Day 4, Tue 27 Oct)
**Audience insight:** "Starting Monday" is a socially acceptable way to postpone your life. People laugh at it because it's true, and they keep doing it.
**Saturn/Shani principle:** Time and patience. Saturn is slow but never postponed. *Slow is not late.*
**Source and interpretation (INTERNAL: not for public copy):**
- **Source:** NASA, a Saturn orbit is about 29.4 years; Monier-Williams "slow-moving".
- **Psychology:** procrastination is predicted by task aversiveness and impulsiveness (Steel 2007) and works as short-term mood repair (Zhang et al. 2019).
- **Shanivar reading:** doing the first two minutes now turns "later" into "slow".
**Format:** Uncomfortable admission (UGC format 1), creator CB1 to camera.
**Duration:** 35 s

**Hook:** Creator, deadpan, to camera: "I've been starting Monday since March."

**Full script word-for-word with timestamps** (creator's own words for ⟨ ⟩ lines; the framing lines stay as written):
- **0:00–0:03 (A).** ON CAMERA: "I've been starting Monday since March."
- **0:03–0:13 (B).** ON CAMERA: "⟨The course I paid for: still on lesson two.⟩ ⟨The call to my dad I'm saving for 'a proper time'.⟩ ⟨The book by my bed. It's a coaster now.⟩" (Each line gets a 1-second insert.)
- **0:13–0:23 (C).** ON CAMERA: "Then I learnt this. Saturn's old Sanskrit name means 'the slow mover'. It takes twenty-nine years to go round the sun once. And it has never once said 'starting Monday'. Slow isn't the problem. Later is."
- **0:23–0:31 (D).** ON CAMERA: "So. Pick one. Say it out loud. Do the first two minutes before you scroll. ⟨I'll go first: I'm opening lesson three.⟩" (They really do it; cut to the screen or the page.)
- **0:31–0:35 (E).** VO (founder or creator): "Slow is not late." Text: Shanivar.

**Visual direction shot by shot (phone, 9:16):**
1. Medium close-up, phone propped on a stack of books, natural window light from the side, slightly underexposed. Locked off. One take for the hook.
2. Inserts (1 s each, abrupt): a course dashboard at "Lesson 2" (real, creator's own); the call log with Dad's name; tea cup on the book, lifted to reveal a **ring stain on the cover**. The chai ring is the Saturn motif; hold it 0.5 s longer.
3. Back to medium close-up for the reframe. Slow push-in by hand (5 s), shoulders down.
4. Real-time action: the creator picks up the laptop or book and starts. **One unbroken 8-second take**; we see them actually begin.
5. Black end card with the gold ring mark.

**Performance direction:**
- Dry and self-mocking, not ashamed. Never lecture.
- The Saturn line is said like a surprising fact they just read, not a sermon.
- The "I'll go first" moment must be real: if they don't do it, don't post it.

**Sound design:**
- Room tone and a fan.
- Each insert gets its own diegetic sound: a laptop click, a phone ringtone cut off, a cup set down.
- A slight sound drop at 0:13: the fan stops (turn it off on set).
- End: a single tumbler strike.

**On-screen text with timing:**
- 0–3: *starting Monday since March*
- 3–13: *lesson 2 · "a proper time" · coaster* (match the creator's real list)
- 13–23: *Saturn: "the slow mover" · \~29 years per orbit*
- 23–31: *Name it. Two minutes. Now.*
- 31–35: *Slow is not late.* / *SHANIVAR · in development for Android*

**Active-passive moment:** Fast list of postponements (abrupt inserts), then one unbroken real-time take of actually beginning.
**Shanivar integration:** None beyond the end card.
**CTA:** "Comment the thing you're 'starting Monday'. Then do two minutes of it."

**Caption ready to publish:**
> Not lazy. Just always Monday.
\>
> Saturn takes about 29 years to go round the sun. Slow. Never postponed.
\>
> Name one thing below. Then give it two minutes before you scroll.
\>
> Shanivar · in development for Android
> #Shanivar #SlowIsNotLate #Procrastination #StartingMonday #Saturn

**Cover text:** *Starting Monday since March.*

**UGC adaptation (3 creators):**
1. **Fitness creator:** the gym bag in the boot of the car since August; does 2 minutes of mobility in the parking lot.
2. **Musician:** the guitar with a dust ring; plays one scale.
3. **Working parent:** the will, the insurance form or the photo album; opens the file.
Each list must be their real list.

**Repurposing:**

- **Carousel:** "Things we're starting Monday", from comments (consented, no handles).

**Measurement:**

- Shares; saves.

**Funnel line:**
- Problem: postponing life.

## R03: They Sent "k."

**Concept name:** They Sent "k."
**Campaign phase:** 1, Recognition (Day 6, Thu 29 Oct)
**Audience insight:** We blame others' tone and timing, and react within seconds. The real cost is the next message we send.
**Saturn/Shani principle:** Consequence and responsibility. Tradition ties Shani to consequences. Shanivar's reading: not punishment; the next action is yours.
**Source and interpretation (INTERNAL: not for public copy):**
- **Source:** Gita 2.62–63 (Telang): pondering → attachment → desire → anger → "want of discrimination".
- **Tradition:** Shani is associated with karma and justice.
- **Psychology:** distress tolerance is the capacity to withstand aversive states (Leyro et al. 2010; heterogeneous construct).
- **Shanivar:** the 30-second pause is a creative practice, not a tested intervention.
**Format:** Uncomfortable admission with built-in practice (UGC format 1). Comedic. CB2 creator.
**Duration:** 33 s

**Hook:** Phone buzz. Screen: a single message bubble: "k."

**Full script word-for-word with timestamps:**
- **0:00–0:03 (A).** SFX buzz. Text: *They sent "k."* Creator's eyes, close-up, one blink.
- **0:03–0:12 (B).** VO (creator, fast, funny): "And I've already written three paragraphs. In my head I've won the argument, lost the friendship and moved cities. It's always their tone. Their timing. Their fault."
- **0:12–0:20 (C).** VO (slower): "Shani's the one our grandparents tied to consequences. Not punishment. Consequences. And the only consequence I control in the next hour is the next thing I send."
- **0:20–0:30 (D).** Text instruction. Silence for 8 seconds. VO at the end: "⟨Okay. Different message.⟩"
- **0:30–0:33 (E).** Text: *Not a curse. A receipt.* SHANIVAR.

**Visual direction shot by shot:**
1. Macro on the phone screen: the "k." bubble arrives (use the creator's real chat with a willing friend; blur the name). Cut to an extreme close-up of the eye. Abrupt.
2. Fast thumb typing, delete, retype; three quick jump-cuts with the time-stamp advancing (real, not faked). Insert: the creator pacing in a corridor, sped up 2x (labelled as is; no fakery needed).
3. Hand sets the phone face down on a kitchen counter. Cut to a locked-off wide: the creator stands still at the counter; a pressure cooker or a fan moves in the background.
4. **Long take (10 s):** steel tumbler of water, the shadow of the window frame crossing it. On-screen instruction.
5. Hand picks up the phone; types a short, calm message (their real one). Send.
6. End card.

**Performance direction:**
- B is comedy, played straight-faced and quick.
- C drops 30% in pace and volume.
- In D the creator does nothing on camera, and must not "act" calm.
- The final message must be what they would really send.

**Sound design:**
- B: layered typing clicks, a sped-up corridor and an inner-monologue whisper track (optional).
- C: sound drops to the fridge hum.
- D: **near-silence** with only the fridge hum. A single water drip if natural.
- The send whoosh is the only sound at 0:29.

**On-screen text with timing:**
- 0–3: *They sent "k."*
- 3–12: *3 paragraphs · won the argument · lost the friend · moved cities*
- 12–20: *Shani: consequence, not punishment.*
- 20–30: *Dwell on it and it grows: wanting, then anger. 30 seconds before you reply. Phone down. Watch what the anger wants to type.*
- 30–33: *Not a curse. A receipt.* / *SHANIVAR · in development for Android*

**Active-passive moment:** Typing frenzy and pacing (sped up), then a tumbler and a shadow in real time while the creator stands still.
**Shanivar integration:** None.
**CTA:** "Send this to the friend who replies in paragraphs."
