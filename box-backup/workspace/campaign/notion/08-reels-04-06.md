## R04: The Slow Planet

**Concept name:** The Slow Planet (campaign anthem)
**Campaign phase:** 2, Saturn Reframe (Day 8, Sat 31 Oct)
**Audience insight:** Their days feel fast but their life feels stuck. Speed is everywhere; progress is not.
**Saturn/Shani principle:** Time and patience. Fast inside, slow on the path.
**Source and interpretation (INTERNAL: not for public copy):**
- **Source:** NASA, a Saturn day is 10.7 hours and an orbit is about 29.4 Earth years. Monier-Williams, *Śani* "slow-moving"; *Śanaiścara*.
- **Psychology:** habit strength builds slowly and plateaus over weeks (Gardner et al. 2012).
- **Shanivar reading:** move slowly on purpose, for 20 steps.
**Format:** Saturn perspective in plain language (UGC format 4). Brand film with founder voice-over, shot in Jaipur.
**Duration:** 40 s

**Hook:** Black frame. Text: *A day on Saturn: 10.7 hours.* Hard cut to traffic.

**Full script word-for-word with timestamps** (VO founder):
- **0:00–0:03 (A).** No VO. Text only. SFX: a single clock tick, then traffic slams in.
- **0:03–0:12 (B).** VO: "Your days spin fast too. Thirty tabs. Podcasts at two-x. 'On my way', typed from bed. Lunch eaten over a keyboard."
- **0:12–0:24 (C).** VO: "But one trip around the sun takes Saturn about twenty-nine years. In Sanskrit it's called Shanaishchara. The one who moves slowly. Fast inside. Slow on the path. And it still arrives."
- **0:24–0:34 (D).** VO: "The next twenty steps you take: take them at half speed. Feel your feet. Nobody needs to know."
- **0:34–0:40 (E).** VO: "Slow is not late." Text: Shanivar.

**Visual direction shot by shot (phone):**
1. Pure black with type (2 s).
2. MI Road or Tripolia Bazaar traffic at 4x. Phone on a ledge, locked off, 1/50 shutter for motion blur.
3. Abrupt inserts, 0.5 s each:
   - a laptop with 30 tabs (real),
   - a podcast player at 2x,
   - a typed "on my way" with the bed visible in the reflection,
   - fingers typing while eating a roti.
4. **Long take (12 s):** a person stands still at the edge of a stepwell (Panna Meena ka Kund, if permitted; or any rooftop) while pigeons lift and settle and the shadow line moves on stone. Real time.
5. Low tracking shot at ankle height: bare feet or chappals on stone steps, taken at half speed. Shoot at 60 fps and play at real time (no slow-mo). The slowness must be performed, not faked.
6. End: black, gold ring mark drifting by 2 px (no pulsing).

**Performance direction:**
- The walker isn't "meditating". They are just walking slowly, as if carrying a full cup.
- Founder VO: calm and plain. Let "And it still arrives." sit.

**Sound design:**
- Traffic and horns, cut on each picture cut.
- At 0:12 the city audio **drops out** to wind and pigeons' wings only.
- 0:24–0:34: **footsteps only**, close-miked (phone in a pocket recording separately).
- End: silence, then a soft tumbler tone.

**On-screen text with timing:**
- 0–3: *A day on Saturn: 10.7 hours.*
- 3–12: *30 tabs · 2x · "on my way" · lunch at the keyboard*
- 12–24: *One orbit: \~29 years.* / *Śanaiścara: the one who moves slowly.*
- 24–34: *Next 20 steps: half speed.*
- 34–40: *Slow is not late.* / *SHANIVAR · in development for Android*
- Small credit at 12–24: *Saturn data: NASA*

**Active-passive moment:** This reel is the signature. 4x city, then a 12-second still figure, then slow feet with only footsteps heard.
**Shanivar integration:** Brand line only.
**CTA:** "Save this. Take the 20 steps before you open your next app."

**Caption ready to publish:**
> A day on Saturn is 10.7 hours.
> One trip around the sun takes about 29 years.
\>

> Its old Sanskrit name, Śanaiścara, means the one who moves slowly.
\>

**Repurposing:**

## R05: Child of the Shadow

**Concept name:** Child of the Shadow
**Campaign phase:** 2, Saturn Reframe (Day 10, Mon 2 Nov)
**Audience insight:** Everyone has an automatic self they're a little embarrassed by. Naming it with humour makes it watchable instead of shameful.
**Saturn/Shani principle:** Shadow and automaticity. Shani as *Chhāyā's* son.
**Source and interpretation (INTERNAL: not for public copy):**
- **Source:** Vishnu Purana III.2 (Wilson 1840). Sanjñā leaves Chhāyā ("shadow") in her place, and Chhāyā bears "Śanaiścara (Saturn)".
- **Tradition:** Shani is associated with time and karma.
- **Psychology:** self-control relates more strongly to automatic behaviour (de Ridder et al. 2012); habits are context-cued automatic responses (Gardner et al. 2012).
- **Shanivar reading (labelled as ours):** shadow habits are the part of you not yet watched.
**Format:** Saturn perspective in plain language (UGC format 4). CB5 culture-explainer creator, to camera with their shadow.
**Duration:** 42 s

**Hook:** The creator's shadow on a wall moves a beat **before** the creator does. Text: *Saturn's mother was a shadow.*

**Full script word-for-word with timestamps:**
- **0:00–0:03 (A).** ON CAMERA: "In the old story, Saturn's mother is a shadow."
- **0:03–0:13 (B).** ON CAMERA: "You've got a shadow too. The one who opens the fridge when bored. The one who says 'five more minutes' at one a.m. The one who agrees in the meeting and complains in the car."
- **0:13–0:28 (C).** ON CAMERA: "The story: the sun's wife can't bear his heat. She leaves behind Chhaya, literally 'shadow', and goes to the forest. Chhaya's son is Shanaishchara. Saturn. In later tradition he's the planet of time and karma. Here's our reading: your shadow habits aren't your enemy. They're just the part of you nobody's watched yet."
- **0:28–0:38 (D).** ON CAMERA: "Tonight, catch your shadow once. Give it a name out loud. 'That's the five-more-minutes one.' That's it. Just watch it."
- **0:38–0:42 (E).** Text: *Meet the one who follows you.* SHANIVAR.

**Visual direction shot by shot:**
1. Single hard lamp (a phone torch through a steel strainer for texture) on a lime-washed wall. Creator in profile; the shadow is dominant. To make the shadow "lead", film a second take of the shadow alone and cross-cut it 4 frames early. Shoot both takes in-camera with no VFX.
2. Abrupt vignettes, 1.5 s each, all in shadow-play on the same wall: the fridge door opening, the phone glowing at 1 a.m., a nodding head, then arms gesturing in a car (real footage).
3. Medium close-up, warmer practical lamp, creator speaking plainly. Insert: an illustrated card of the genealogy (Sūrya, Sanjñā, Chhāyā, Śani), hand-drawn in ink on paper. No AI, no stock.
4. A long take of the creator's hand, still, and its shadow, still (8 s).
5. End card.

**Performance direction:**
- A storyteller's warmth, like explaining to a younger cousin.
- Respectful about the story: no smirk on the story itself; the humour stays on our habits.
- Name the habits with affection.

**Sound design:**
- The room is quiet.
- B: each shadow vignette gets one real sound (fridge seal, phone buzz, car indicator).
- C: soft paper rustle on the genealogy card.
- D: a ceiling fan stops (turned off), then silence.
- End: a low tone.

**On-screen text with timing:**
- 0–3: *Saturn's mother was a shadow.*
- 3–13: *the fridge one · the "five more minutes" one · the yes-then-complain one*
- 13–28: *An old story* · *Chhāyā = "shadow"* · *Our reading: the part of you not yet watched.*
- 28–38: *Tonight: catch it once. Name it.*
- 38–42: *Meet the one who follows you.* / *SHANIVAR · in development for Android*

**Active-passive moment:** Shadows act out the automatic self in quick cuts, then a still hand and a still shadow, with nothing moving.
**Shanivar integration:** None.
**CTA:** "Comment your shadow's name."

**Caption ready to publish:**
> In the old story, Saturn is born to Chhāyā, whose name means "shadow".
\>
> Our reading, not the story's: your shadow habits aren't the enemy. They're the part of you nobody's watched yet.
\>
> Tonight, catch yours once. Give it a name.
> Mine is "five more minutes". Yours?
\>
> Shanivar · in development for Android
> #Shanivar #Saturn #Shani #Shadow #Habits

**Cover text:** *Saturn's mother was a shadow.*

**UGC adaptation (3 creators):**
1. **Illustrator:** draws their shadow habit as a character in ink over 40 s (time-lapse), then holds the drawing still.
2. **Dancer:** the shadow moves first, the body follows; then both stop.
3. **Hindi storyteller:** tells the Chhāyā story in Hindi with fluent review; same structure.

**Repurposing:**
- **Stories:** "Name your shadow" text sticker; a highlight of the names (consented).
- **Carousel:** "Four layers" format for the story: the old story, tradition, psychology, our reading (no text or translator named on slides).
- **Shorts:** same master.
- **Long caption on Threads.**

**Measurement:**
- Comments with a named habit (meaningful).
- Saves.
- Sentiment check for devotional offence.
- Retention through 0:28 (the story section is the risk).

**Funnel line:**
- Problem: automatic habits.
- Human moment: agree in the meeting, complain in the car.
- Insight: the shadow is unwatched, not evil.
- Practice: catch it once and name it.
- Feature: none.
- CTA: comment the name.
- Destination: comments.

**QC:**
- Interrupts ✔ (the shadow leads).
- Specific ✔. Recognisable ✔.

- Attributed ✔ internally (VP III.2, Wilson); public copy says "the old story" and labels our reading.

## R06: Shani Isn't Coming For You

**Concept name:** Shani Isn't Coming For You
**Campaign phase:** 2, Saturn Reframe (Day 12, Wed 4 Nov)
**Audience insight:** Bad weeks get blamed on Shani. Half-joking, half-scared, it lets everyone off the hook, including the 2 a.m. reels the night before the exam.
**Saturn/Shani principle:** Karma as action. Consequences, not curses.
**Source and interpretation (INTERNAL: not for public copy):**
- **Source:** Gita 2.47 (Telang): "Your business is with action alone; not by any means with fruit." *Karma* (Sanskrit *karman*) means action or deed.
- **Tradition:** Shani is linked to karma and justice; the fear content is popular culture.
- **Psychology:** the intention–behaviour gap (Webb & Sheeran 2006).
- **Shanivar reading:** Shani as "the receipt for what you repeated". Labelled as our playful reading.
**Format:** Saturn perspective in plain language, comedic (UGC format 4). CB5 or CB1 creator with a family-chat bit.
**Duration:** 38 s

**Hook:** ON CAMERA: "In my family, every bad week is Shani's fault."

**Full script word-for-word with timestamps:**
- **0:00–0:03 (A).** ON CAMERA: "In my family, every bad week is Shani's fault."
- **0:03–0:13 (B).** ON CAMERA (fast): "Missed the train? Shani. Exam went badly? Shani. Phone on two percent? Also Shani." Cut to a night-before flashback: "Meanwhile me, at 2 a.m., the night before the exam: 'one more reel'."
- **0:13–0:27 (C).** ON CAMERA (slower): "Here's what I found. The old advice is simple: the doing is yours, the result isn't. It's about doing, not getting. Tradition links Shani to karma, and karma just means action. So maybe Shani isn't coming for me. Maybe he's the receipt. For what I repeated."
- **0:27–0:35 (D).** ON CAMERA: "So today: one action you'd do even if nobody ever saw the result. Do it before nine p.m. ⟨Mine: I'm sleeping by eleven.⟩"
- **0:35–0:38 (E).** Text: *Not a curse. A receipt.* SHANIVAR.

**Visual direction shot by shot:**
1. A tight two-shot setup with the creator alone, on the sofa, family-drawing-room light. Locked off.
2. Rapid gag inserts (0.7 s each): a train leaving (real), a marksheet face down, a battery icon at 2%.
3. "Flashback": phone light on a face in a dark bedroom, timestamp 2:04 a.m. on the lock screen (real or reenacted and clearly comedic).
4. Back to the sofa, a slow handheld push. Insert: a printed thermal receipt (a real till roll) the creator holds up. Handwritten: "2:04 a.m. · 1 more reel ×37". This is the Territory C motif, used once.
5. Real action: the creator puts the phone on charge in another room, and the lights go off. One take.
6. End card.

**Performance direction:**
- Affectionate about the family, never mocking belief.
- The joke is on ourselves, not on devotees.
- Deliver "the doing is yours, the result isn't" plainly, without a "spiritual voice". Never present it as a quotation.

**Sound design:**
- B: comic rhythm, each gag on a beat (a tabla "na" hit from a royalty-free library, or a real household sound like a pressure-cooker whistle).
- C: music drops out entirely.
- The receipt-paper tear is the only sound at 0:24.
- D: a light switch, then silence.

**On-screen text with timing:**
- 0–3: *Every bad week = Shani.*
- 3–13: *train · exam · 2% · 2 a.m. "one more reel"*
- 13–27: *The doing is yours. The result isn't.* · *karma = action*
- 27–35: *One action, no audience. Before 9 p.m.*
- 35–38: *Not a curse. A receipt.* / *SHANIVAR · in development for Android*

**Active-passive moment:** Gag cuts at speed, then one unbroken take of the phone going to another room and the light going off.
**Shanivar integration:** None.
**CTA:** "Save this for the next time someone blames Shani."

**Caption ready to publish:**
> Missed the train? Shani. Exam? Shani. 2% battery? Shani.
> 2 a.m. "one more reel"? …
\>
> The old advice: the doing is yours, the result isn't.
> Karma means action. Our reading: Shani isn't a curse. More like a receipt.
\>
> With respect to everyone who keeps Saturday for Shani. This is our reading, not a ruling.
\>
> One action today that nobody will see. Before 9 p.m.
\>
> Shanivar · in development for Android
> #Shanivar #Shani #Saturn #Karma #NotACurseAReceipt

**Cover text:** *Shani isn't coming for you.*

**UGC adaptation (3 creators):**

2. **Finance creator:** "My bank balance isn't Shani. It's food delivery at 1 a.m." They pick one no-spend action.
