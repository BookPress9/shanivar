# 11. Five creator briefs

## Rules every creator signs (the truth contract)
1. **Your words, your life.** We give you a structure, not a script. Every observation, result and admission must be yours and must have actually happened. Placeholders ⟨like this⟩ are yours to fill. If it didn't happen, cut it.
2. **No invented results.** Never "I'm calmer", "I focus better", "I sleep better" or "my anxiety…". You may say what you noticed or did.
3. **Nothing scripted passed off as spontaneous.** If a moment is a re-enactment, the on-screen text or caption says so ("re-enacted").
4. **No health, spiritual, financial or astrological promises.** No "this will fix", "Shani will bless", "detox", "dopamine".
5. **Disclose (ASCI).** If you're paid, gifted or have any material connection, use a permitted label up front ("Ad", "Sponsored", "Collaboration" or "Partnership") **and** Instagram's "Paid partnership" tag.
   - If your video has no caption: the label stays on screen for at least 3 s on videos ≤15 s, and for at least a third of the length on videos of 15 s – 2 min.
   - The label is not buried in hashtags.
6. **The app is not available.** Say "being built" or "in development for Android". Never "download", "link to app" or "I've been using it". Exception: an invited tester using the labelled test build.
7. **Respect.** Shani is sacred to many people. Joke about our habits, never about devotees or deities.
8. **No AI people.** Don't use AI to generate yourself, other people, voices or app screens. AI B-roll (landscapes, objects) only with an "AI-generated" label.
9. **Never mention** other wellness brands, living teachers or gurus, or the paused umbrella brand (the founder will tell you which name).
10. **Consent.** Anyone else in frame has agreed, including family. Children only with a parent's consent, and never as the subject of a claim.

**Review:** Shanivar checks facts, claims and disclosure only. We don't rewrite your voice. **Usage (proposal, founder to decide):** 6 months organic reposting on @app.shanivar with credit; no paid ads/whitelisting without a separate agreement. **Fee:** founder to decide (suggestion: 3 paid, 2 seeded/unpaid with a clear "Collaboration" label if any value is exchanged).

## CB1: The Postponer
- **Who:** 20–28, student or early-career, India-based, English/Hinglish. Funny about their own procrastination. 5k–50k followers; comments full of "same".
- **Why them:** owns the "starting Monday" joke without self-help energy.
- **Deliverables:** R02 own version (35 s); 7 Experiment Stories (Days 1–7); R08 contribution (4 s line + notebook); 1 Shanivar Hour Story.
- **The honest experiment:** do the 7 days. Film a 10-second diary at the same place and time daily. Show missed days.
- **Must:** a real list of postponed things; really start the 2-minute task on camera.
- **Must not:** pretend the 7 days changed them; use "productivity hacks" language.
- **Hook to adapt:** "I've been starting Monday since ⟨month⟩."
- **CTA:** "Comment your Monday thing." / Phase 3: "DM @app.shanivar SATURN."
- **Timeline:** brief 26 Oct; R02 draft 30 Oct for the founder's fact check; Experiment 12–18 Nov.

## CB2: The Reactor (phone-first professional)
- **Who:** 24–34, office or agency worker, lives in chats; deadpan humour; relationship and work comedy.
- **Why them:** owns "They sent 'k.'" and the reach at work.
- **Deliverables:** R03 own version (33 s); an R01-style "reach at work" reel (30 s); a "Saturday folder" Story for R11; R12 Story.
- **The honest experiment:** the 30-second pause before replying, for 3 days. Show at least one time they failed to pause.
- **Must:** real messages (with consent from the other person, names blurred).
- **Must not:** frame anyone as toxic, or give relationship advice.
- **Hook:** "They sent '⟨k. / fine. / ok⟩'."
- **CTA:** "Send this to the paragraph-replier."

## CB3: The Skeptic
- **Who:** 22–35, fitness, tech, finance or engineering creator who is openly sceptical of wellness.
- **Why them:** sceptic credibility; the audience trusts their "fine, it's hard".
- **Deliverables:** R09 own version (35 s, one continuous 60-second take filmed in full); R08 contribution; 1 Shanivar Hour Story.
- **The honest experiment:** 60 seconds of nothing, once a day for 7 days. Write down the number of exits each day. Never fake a better number.
- **Must:** a visible real timer and real outtakes; cite the study as given ("Wilson et al., Science 2014").
- **Must not:** convert to a believer on camera, or claim calm or focus gains.
- **Hook:** "Sitting still is a scam. I'll prove it in 60 seconds."
- **CTA:** "Comment your number."

## CB4: The Diarist
- **Who:** 22–38, journaling, notebook or slow-living creator, aesthetic but honest.
- **Why them:** turns the 7-day experiment into a believable diary, missed days included.
- **Deliverables:** lead voice for R08 (45 s compilation); a full 7-day diary reel (45 s); 7 daily Stories; a Shanivar Hour before/after (8 p.m. line).
- **The honest experiment:** the full Slow Planet Experiment (1 hour, 1 minute, 1 line). Notebook on camera daily.
- **Must:** show crosses as well as ticks; read lines verbatim; keep the dullest true line.
- **Must not:** use "glow-up", "transformation" or "healing" words.
- **Hook:** "7 days. I missed ⟨n⟩. Here's what I actually saw."
- **CTA:** "Start tomorrow. DM SATURN for the rules."

## CB5: The Culture Explainer
- **Who:** 24–40, explains Indian mythology, history or Sanskrit words with care; bilingual Hindi/English a plus; known for accuracy.
- **Why them:** carries R05 and R06 with authority and respect, and protects the brand from mistakes.
- **Deliverables:** R05 own version (42 s); R06 own version (38 s); one "Why an app is named after Saturday" explainer (30 s) for Phase 4.
- **Source pack (only these):**
  - Vishnu Purana III.2 (Wilson): Chhāyā and Śanaiścara.
  - Gita 2.47, 2.14, 6.35, 18.37–38 (Telang, SBE 8).
  - Katha Upanishad I.2.1–2 (Müller).
  - Monier-Williams: *Śani* "slow-moving", Saturday.
  - NASA Saturn facts.
  Any other claim must come with its source **before** filming.
- **Must:** separate "the text says" from "tradition associates" from "our reading", out loud or on screen; name the translator when quoting.
- **Must not:**
  - spread fear (Sade Sati warnings, remedies, "Shani's gaze");
  - make predictions or present astrology as science;
  - name living teachers;
  - invent shlokas or quotes.
- **Hook:** "Saturn's mother was a shadow." / "In my family every bad week is Shani's fault."
- **CTA:** "Save this for the next time someone blames Shani."
