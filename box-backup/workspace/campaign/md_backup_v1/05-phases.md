# 5. The four phases

Dates are proposals (Europe/Berlin calendar dates; post times in IST). Day 1 is Sat 24 Oct 2026, a Shanivar. The Diwali window, Fri 6 – Wed 11 Nov (Diwali is Sun 8 Nov), runs at low cadence.

## Phase 1: Recognition (Days 1–7, Sat 24 – Fri 30 Oct)
**Audience insight.**
- They don't lack information about discipline. They lack a mirror for the 40 tiny automatic moments a day.
- They admire discipline and keep postponing it. "Starting Monday" is a running joke they are tired of.

**Core message.** *That is exactly what I do.* (Interrupt + Expose.)

**Creative concept.** Catch the behaviour on camera before naming it:
- the reach,
- the "starting Monday",
- the three-paragraph reply to "k."

The Saturn reframe is light: one line, the name *Shanaishchara*.

**Deliverables.**
- Reels R01, R02 and R03.
- 2 carousels: "Seven automatic moments", and "That's exactly what I do", built from real comments with consent.
- Daily Stories (poll, question box).
- A pinned comment on each reel inviting a specific admission.

**Creator brief.** CB1 (Postponer) and CB2 (Reactor) shoot their own versions. See Creator briefs.

**CTA + destination.** No product CTA. "Comment the thing you're 'starting Monday'." / "Send this to the friend who replies in paragraphs." Destination: comments, shares, profile follow.

**Publishing sequence.** R01 (Sat 24) → Stories poll (Sun 25) → R02 (Tue 27) → carousel (Wed 28) → R03 (Thu 29) → comment carousel (Fri 30).

**Success metrics.**
- 3-second hold rate; average watch % and completion.
- Shares per reach; saves per reach.
- **Meaningful comments**: a specific personal admission or a report of doing the practice.
- Profile visits; follows from reels.
- Views are logged but are not a success criterion.

**Learning loop.** At 24 h and 7 d, tag every comment by behaviour (reach / postpone / react / other) and capture Insights.
- The behaviour with the most meaningful comments per 1k reach becomes the confrontation in R08/R09 and the Phase 2 carousel.
- The top hook style feeds the hook tests (see Testing plan).

## Phase 2: Saturn Reframe (Days 8–13, Sat 31 Oct – Thu 5 Nov)
**Audience insight.**
- Many have grown up with Shani as a figure of fear (Sade Sati talk, "bad week = Shani").
- Many educated urban viewers have quietly dismissed astrology, but still feel the pull of the culture.
- A fear-free, sourced reading lets them keep the culture without the superstition.

**Core message.** *Saturn isn't coming for you. Slow is not late.* (Reframe.)

**Creative concept.** Three sourced lenses:
- *The Slow Planet* (etymology + NASA),
- *Child of the Shadow* (Vishnu Purana III.2),
- *Not a curse, a receipt* (Gita 2.47, karma = action).

Each one ends in a physical act.

**Deliverables.**
- Reels R04, R05 and R06.
- 1 "Four layers" carousel showing what the text says, what tradition says, what psychology says and what we think. This builds trust and is very saveable.
- An Experiment announcement carousel (Thu 5 Nov).
- Stories quiz: "Shanaishchara means…".

**Creator brief.** CB5 (Culture Explainer) for R05/R06, working from the source pack. The founder records the R04 voice-over.

**CTA + destination.**
- Soft: "Save this for the next time someone blames Shani."
- Thu 5 Nov: "Join the 7-day Slow Planet Experiment: DM **SATURN**." Destination: DMs → the three rules + an optional waitlist link (shanivar.com, only if confirmed).

**Publishing sequence.** R04 (Sat 31) → Four-layers carousel (Sun 1) → R05 (Mon 2) → quiz Stories (Tue 3) → R06 (Wed 4) → Experiment announcement (Thu 5).

**Success metrics.**
- Saves per reach; shares per reach.
- Comment sentiment on the Shani framing: respectful vs offended (flag any devotional offence).
- DM keyword count (SATURN), profile visits, follows.

**Learning loop.**
- Check offence signals and soften lines if needed.
- Compare explicit-Saturn-name hooks with implicit hooks.
- The lens with the highest saves becomes the "why" in the R10 founder piece.

## Diwali window (Days 14–19, Fri 6 – Wed 11 Nov)
**Insight.** Attention belongs to family and festival, and pushing product here reads as tone-deaf.

**Output.**
- One quiet static on Sat 7 Nov: "Let the world wait." (lamp, no product, no CTA).
- Stories only otherwise.
- Experiment prep Stories on Tue 10 and Wed 11: "Choose your one hour."

**Metric.** None beyond reach/saves. Rest.

## Phase 3: The Experiment (Days 20–27, Thu 12 – Thu 19 Nov)
**Audience insight.** People distrust transformation content. They will trust a 7-day attempt that shows missed days and dull truths.

**Core message.** *Long time, and come back.* (Activate + Invite.)

**Creative concept.** *The Slow Planet Experiment*, 7 days, phone-only, no app needed. Each day:
1. **One boundary:** one chosen hour with the apps that can wait, out of reach (moved off the home screen or the phone in another room).
2. **One deliberate minute:** sit and do nothing. No phone, no music. No breathing instructions.
3. **One honest line at night:** "What pulled me away?"

A missed day is recorded, not punished.

**Deliverables.**
- R07 (founder field notes, Day 3), R09 (skeptic), R08 (7 days, 2 missed: 3 creators).
- Daily Stories: Day X/7 with a "missed" sticker allowed.
- An end-of-week carousel built from consented participant lines (verbatim, with first name or initials only, opt-in).

**Creator brief.** CB3 (Skeptic), CB4 (Diarist), and CB1/CB2 continue.

**CTA + destination.** "Start tomorrow. DM SATURN for the three rules." / "Missed one? Write why. Come back." Destination: DMs → participant list (manual, consented); the optional waitlist.

**Publishing sequence.** Day 1 Thu 12 (Stories + rules carousel) → R07 Sat 14 → R09 Mon 16 → Day 7 Wed 18 (Stories) → R08 Thu 19.

**Success metrics.**
- Experiment enrolments (DM keyword).
- Day-3 and Day-7 self-reported participation, with missed days counted as participation.
- Repeat practice: the share of enrolled people who report 4 or more of 7 days.
- Story replies, saves, meaningful comments.

**Learning loop.**
- Which of the three practices people kept vs dropped decides the lead practice for launch.
- The pull-away reasons people report (in their own words) feed R11 confrontation lines; quote them only with consent.

## Phase 4: Launch (Days 28–30 and beyond, Fri 20 – Sun 22 Nov)
"Launch" here means the **waitlist launch** of an app in development, not a store launch.

**Audience insight.** After the experiment, some people want a tool that holds the boundary for them, and the honest "it's not ready yet" builds credibility.

**Core message.** *Some things can wait. Shanivar is being built to help you choose which.*

**Creative concept.** The founder's why, then the product line with labelled test-build footage, then a weekly ritual (*Shanivar Hour*, every Saturday) that keeps people practising before the app exists.

**Deliverables.**
- R10 (founder, Fri 20), R12 (Shanivar Hour, Sat 21, posted in the morning), R11 (launch hero, Sun 22).
- Bio update (founder approval) and a link-in-bio or DM flow.
- A pinned "What Shanivar is / isn't" carousel.

**Creator brief.** All creators post their own Shanivar Hour Story on Sat 21. Paid ones disclose.

**CTA + destination.**
- R10/R11: "Join the Android waitlist." Destination: **link in bio → shanivar.com early-access form** (only after founder confirmation; UTM `utm_campaign=m1_waitlist`). Fallback: DM **WAIT** → manual waitlist.
- R12: "Saturday, 7–8 p.m., phone in another room. Tell us one thing you noticed." Destination: comments.

**Success metrics.**
- Profile visits → link clicks → waitlist sign-ups. Conversion: link clicks per profile visit; sign-ups per link click.
- DM WAIT count.
- Shanivar Hour participation in week 1 and **repeat** in week 2.
- Later, when testers start: activation (first boundary saved + first minute done) and repeat practice in week 2.

**Learning loop.** Weekly review on Mondays:
- keep the top 2 hooks,
- kill the bottom 2,
- carry the winning practice into the next Saturday,
- log everything in `marketing/shanivar/creative-learning-log.md`.
