# 12. Repeatable UGC production template

Use one copy per reel. File naming follows the repo convention: `SHN-YYYYMMDD-CODE-EN` (e.g., `SHN-20261024-REACH-EN`).

## A. One-line brief
- **Behaviour (specific, filmable):** ⟨e.g., checking the time and still not knowing it⟩
- **North-star job (pick 1–2):** Interrupt / Expose / Reframe / Activate / Invite
- **Target reaction:** "That is exactly what I do" / "I need to sit with that" / "Let me try this"
- **Phase and awareness level:** cold (P1–2), warm (P3), hot (P4)

## C. Engine grid (15–45 s)
| Beat | Time | Job | Your line / action | Shot | Sound |

## D. Active-passive motion signature (choose at least 2)
- Busy street vs a still body. Fast scroll vs a still hand. A notification storm, then silence.
- Clocks; a shadow crossing a wall; footsteps; repetition (the same frame on 7 days).
- Abrupt cuts = automatic behaviour. Long takes (6–12 s, locked off) = observation.
- Close-ups of hands, eyes, feet and objects (phone face down, a steel tumbler, the chai ring).
- Palette: deep blacks, pewter/steel, brass accent. Underexpose by 1/3 stop; no orange-teal grading.
- **Vary the register:** in every 3 reels, at least 1 is comedic or absurd.

## E. Phone camera settings (Android)
- 9:16, 4K/30 for long takes, 1080/60 for motion.
- Lock exposure and focus (tap-hold). Shutter 1/50 for natural blur in "fast world" shots.
- Mount for long takes (books, ledge, clamp); no gimbal moves on observation shots.
- Record room tone (10 s) in every location. Record footsteps and objects close, with a second phone in a pocket.
- Keep the text-safe area: no key text in the bottom 20% or top 12%.

## F. AI B-roll (optional)
Google Flow / Veo for **objects and places only**:
- shadow on a lime-washed wall,
- dust in window light,
- a tortoise crossing stone (a nod to iconography; label it).

Rules:
- On-screen "AI-generated" label for the clip's duration, and a caption note "includes AI-generated B-roll".
- Use the platform's AI label setting where it applies.
- Never generate people, creators, hands acting as "users", voices, testimonials or app UI.
- Log the model, prompt, date, edits and rights in the asset manifest.

**Example prompt:**
> Vertical 9:16, locked-off macro of a steel tumbler on a dark wooden table, late-afternoon window light, the shadow of an iron window grille slowly sliding across the table in real time, muted metallic tones, deep blacks, no people, no text, no camera movement, 8 seconds.

## G. Captions, text and disclosure
- Burned-in captions for all VO, at least 48 px, cream `#f7f5ee` on black, high contrast.
- Caption body: 3–6 short lines; a source line when quoting; "Shanivar · in development for Android"; 3–6 hashtags.
  - Never use: #meditation, #wellness, #detox, #selfcare, #horoscope, or the paused umbrella-brand tag.
- Paid: a label in the first line of the caption, the platform "Paid partnership" tag, and on-screen per ASCI timing.

## H. Asset manifest (one row per asset)
`id, date, phase, reel, creator, consent_on_file(Y/N), paid(Y/N), disclosure_label, ai_broll(Y/N + model), sources_cited, product_claims(verbatim + status), founder_fact_check(date), status(draft/approved/published + permalink)`

## I. QC gate (all must be yes before founder review)
1. Does it interrupt in 0–3 s without text-reading?
2. Is the behaviour specific and filmable?
3. Would a viewer say "that's exactly what I do"?
4. Is the Saturn link meaningful (not decoration)?
5. Is every quote accurately attributed (text, verse, translator) and is our reading labelled?
6. Does it demonstrate rather than explain?
7. Is there a real participation moment the viewer can do now?
8. Is it human and credible (real footage, real results, own voice)?
9. Could a generic meditation app post it unchanged? **If yes, rework.**
10. Does the CTA match awareness (no product CTA before Phase 4)?
11. Is every product claim verbatim and true per the feature table?
12. Is it shareable (would someone send it to a specific friend)?
13. Disclosure, consent and the AI label are done. No breath-pacing instruction. No living teacher. No paused umbrella-brand name.

**Reject outright:** generic motivation, recycled astrology, empty dark aesthetics, fake UGC, forced mysticism, fear of Saturn.

## J. After publishing

- Tag comments: meaningful / generic / question / offence.
- Log one learning in `marketing/shanivar/creative-learning-log.md`.
