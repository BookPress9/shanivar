# 9. Reels 07–09 (Phase 3: The Experiment)

**The Slow Planet Experiment** runs 7 days, Thu 12 – Wed 18 Nov, and needs only a phone (no app). Each day:
1. **One boundary:** one chosen hour with the apps that can wait out of reach (moved off the home screen or the phone in another room).
2. **One deliberate minute:** sit and do nothing. No phone, no music, no breathing instructions.
3. **One honest line at night:** "What pulled me away?"

Missed days are written down, not hidden.

## R09: The Skeptic Sits Down

**Concept name:** The Skeptic Sits Down
**Campaign phase:** 3, The Experiment (Day 24, Mon 16 Nov)
**Audience insight:** A lot of the audience thinks "sitting still" is a wellness scam. Watching a sceptic struggle for 60 seconds is funny, and disarming.
**Saturn/Shani principle:** Bearing discomfort, and the restless mind.
**Source and interpretation:**
- **Source:** Gita 6.35 (Telang): the mind "is difficult to restrain, and fickle… it may be restrained by constant practice".
- **Psychology:** Wilson et al. 2014 (Science). Across 11 studies people disliked 6–15 minutes alone with their thoughts. In one study 12 of 18 men gave themselves at least one electric shock rather than just think.
- **Shanivar reading:** silence is loud the first time, and one minute is an initiation.
**Format:** Skeptic's experiment (UGC format 6). CB3.
**Duration:** 35 s

**Hook:** CB3 to camera: "Sitting still is a scam. I'll do sixty seconds of nothing to prove it."

**Full script word-for-word with timestamps** (real outtakes only):
- **0:00–0:03 (A).** ON CAMERA: "Sitting still is a scam. I'll do sixty seconds of nothing to prove it."
- **0:03–0:14 (B).** A real timer on screen. ⟨At 0:09 his hand goes to his pocket: "That was a notification. Doesn't count."⟩ ⟨At 0:19 he scratches his nose.⟩ ⟨At 0:31: "Is this a minute yet?"⟩ (Use only what really happened in the take.)
- **0:14–0:24 (C).** ON CAMERA, after: "Then I read this. 2014, Science. People left alone with their thoughts for six to fifteen minutes. Lots of them chose to give themselves an electric shock instead. In one study, twelve of eighteen men did. So fine. Not a scam. Just hard."
- **0:24–0:32 (D).** ON CAMERA: "Your turn. Sixty seconds. No phone, no music. Count how many times you leave. Comment your number. ⟨Mine was four.⟩"
- **0:32–0:35 (E).** VO (founder): "Shanivar starts with one deliberate minute." Text: *in development for Android*.

**Visual direction shot by shot:**
1. Medium shot: gym or office chair, phone propped, a timer overlay (real stopwatch app visible on a second phone in frame, so it's verifiable).
2. **One continuous take of the full 60 seconds** at real speed. In the edit, compress it with honest jump-cuts at each "leave", and show the timer each time.
3. A close-up of his hands fidgeting, a natural insert from the same take.
4. Medium close-up, post-sit, slightly sheepish.
5. Type card with the study citation.
6. End card.

**Performance direction:**
- Real scepticism. Don't convert him.
- He shouldn't claim to feel calmer. "Just hard" is the honest takeaway.

- A ticking stopwatch is audible.

- End: tone.

**On-screen text with timing:**
