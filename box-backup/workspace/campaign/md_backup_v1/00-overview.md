## The idea in five lines
1. One of Saturn's oldest Sanskrit names is *Śanaiścara*, "the one who moves slowly". It takes about 29.4 years to go around the sun, yet one Saturn day is only 10.7 hours (NASA). Fast inside, slow on the path.
2. Shanivar means Saturday, Shani's day. The campaign treats Saturn as a lens, not a force: time, patience, consequence, boundaries, return.
3. The world moves and you notice. Every reel puts one moment of stillness inside motion, then asks the viewer to try it for real: watch the reach, wait 30 seconds, take 20 slow steps, do one minute of nothing.
4. There is no fear and no fortune-telling. Shani is never the punisher. At most he is the receipt for what you repeated.
5. The line: **Slow is not late.** Supporting lines: *Be the slow one.* *Some things can wait.*

## Standing rules applied everywhere
- Voice: dark atmospheric editorial, few words, poetic second person. Experience comes before explanation.
- No medical, therapy, energy or guaranteed outcomes. No breath pacing or counted-breathing instructions. No living teachers or gurus are named.
- Shani/Saturn and the classical texts are attributed accurately: the text, the translator and the verse. Astrology is never presented as science.
- The paused umbrella brand and its hashtag appear nowhere in campaign assets.
- AI video (Google Flow / Veo) is for B-roll only, labelled "AI-generated" on screen. It is never used for a person, a creator, a testimonial or a product UI.
- Paid partnerships are disclosed per ASCI: label up front, with the Instagram "Paid partnership" tag as well.
- Draft only. Nothing goes live without the founder's go-ahead (marketing was paused on 6 Oct; see Open questions).
