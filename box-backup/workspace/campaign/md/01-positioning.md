# 1. One-page launch positioning

**For** India-based, English-comfortable adults aged about 18–34 who work, study or create on screens (secondary: 31–44 balancing work and care). They admire discipline, keep postponing their own life, and already know which apps take more than they give.

**Shanivar is** an Android app in development that turns Saturn's old lessons into small acts you can do today:
- one chosen boundary on the apps that can wait,
- one deliberate minute,
- one honest line about what pulled you away.

**Unlike** meditation apps that sell calm, astrology pages that sell prediction, and self-help that sells motivation, **Shanivar offers responsibility you can practise and come back to.**

**Campaign line:** *Slow is not late.*
**Signature:** *Be the slow one.*
**Product line (launch assets only):** *Some things can wait.* This is in-app copy in the current build.

## Why Saturn, and why it's honest
**INTERNAL: not for public copy.** The layer table below holds exact attributions for fact-checking. Public lines follow the Naming rule.

| Layer | What we can truthfully say |
|---|---|
| What sources actually say | Vishnu Purana III.2 (H. H. Wilson, 1840): Śanaiścara (Saturn) is the son of the sun and Chhāyā, whose name means "shadow". Monier-Williams: *Śani*, probably "slow-moving" (cf. *manda*), and also "Saturday". NASA: a Saturn year is about 29.4 Earth years and a Saturn day is 10.7 hours. |
| What tradition associates with Shani | Devotional and astrological traditions link Shani with karma (action and its fruit), time, justice, discipline and Saturday observance. Iconography includes a crow banner and a dark complexion. Popular culture also links him with fear and hardship. **We do not use the fear.** |
| What psychology supports | Procrastination is strongly predicted by task aversiveness and impulsiveness (Steel 2007) and is often short-term mood repair (Zhang et al. 2019). Habits form slowly, and one missed day did not seriously derail habit formation (Gardner, Lally & Wardle 2012). Many people struggle to sit alone with their thoughts (Wilson et al. 2014). Notifications cost attention even when ignored (Stothart et al. 2015). Willpower-as-fuel did not replicate (Hagger et al. 2016). |
| Shanivar's creative interpretation | Saturn is a lens for patience, consequence and boundaries. "Slow" means noticing the reach before it happens. "Consequence" is what repetition becomes over time. It is never a curse, a prediction or a promise. |

## Naming rule (founder decision, 17 Oct 2026)
- **May name:** Shani, Saturn, Śanaiścara, Chhāyā, Saturday/Shanivar, karma (as "action"). Science sources (NASA) and psychology studies may be credited by name.
- **Never name in public copy:** any classical text (Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, Vishnu Purana or any Purana, Yoga Sutras, etc.), any verse number, translator or teacher (living or historical). This covers scripts, on-screen text, captions, cover text, CTAs, hashtags, UGC adaptations, carousels, Stories, pinned comments and the bio.
- **How to carry the wisdom:** paraphrase in Shanivar's own voice ("the old story", "the old advice", "practice takes root slowly"), never as a quotation.
- **Where attributions live:** each reel's "Source and interpretation (INTERNAL: not for public copy)" field, the Research page and the Sources database.
- **Product-thesis claim fixes:**
  - "Enter Flow" stays out of public copy. If it is ever used, define it plainly as "acting on purpose", never as a focus, productivity or performance promise.
  - Don't say or caption "Make room for one uninterrupted hour." Say: "one protected hour, with the apps you chose stepping back".
  - Don't say "you choose what reaches you". Shanivar doesn't control notifications. Say: "you choose which apps can wait, and when".
  - Research findings (e.g., the habit study on missed days) are always credited as third-party research and kept apart from product lines. They are never a Shanivar result.
- **Bio (checked; compliant):** "Shanivar · Saturday, Shani's day / Some things can wait. / Android app in development · Early access ↓ (or DM WAIT)".

## Promise boundaries
- We promise an invitation and a tool in development, never an outcome.
- No claims of "less anxiety", "more focus", "dopamine detox", healing, energy, or a spiritual or astrological effect.
- App protection is never called "unbreakable" or "impossible to bypass". The browser and other devices are not blocked, and protection is stoppable inside Shanivar.

## Tone
- Few words. Second person. Concrete scene first: *Red light. Lift. Mid-sentence.*
- Dark editorial with a dry smile. At least 1 in 3 reels uses humour or everyday absurdity (R02, R03, R06, R09).
- Sadhana is framed as an initiation: "This is a beginning, not a finish line. The next path is yours. Shanivar helps you align."
