## R10: A Slow App in a Fast City

**Concept name:** A Slow App in a Fast City
**Campaign phase:** 4, Launch (Day 28, Fri 20 Nov)
**Audience insight:** By now they know the ideas. They need to know who is building this, why, and what is honestly real.
**Saturn/Shani principle:** Discipline without fear, boundaries and time. Shanivar = Saturday, Shani's day.
**Source and interpretation (INTERNAL: not for public copy):**
- **Source:** Monier-Williams, *Śani* also means "Saturday".
- **Tradition:** Shani is associated with discipline, time and consequence.
- **Shanivar reading:** "minus the fear".
- Product claims come from code only (see Feature verification).
**Format:** Founder field notes (UGC format 7), with a labelled test-build screen recording.
**Duration:** 45 s

**Hook:** Founder on a Jaipur street, traffic behind: "Every app on my phone wants more of me. I'm building one that wants less."

**Full script word-for-word with timestamps** (founder confirms each ⟨ ⟩ is true):
- **0:00–0:03 (A).** ON CAMERA: "Every app on my phone wants more of me. I'm building one that wants less."
- **0:03–0:12 (B).** ON CAMERA: "⟨I'd set alarms and snooze them. Block apps and unblock them. Download meditation apps and never open them.⟩"
- **0:12–0:22 (C).** ON CAMERA, quieter location: "Shanivar means Saturday, Shani's day. Shani's tied to discipline, time, consequences. I grew up with the fear part. I wanted the discipline part, minus the fear."
- **0:22–0:36 (D) product + participation.** VO over the test build: "You choose one protected hour. You pick the apps that step back during it. Essential and emergency apps stay. Then one deliberate minute. And there's no streak. 'Your orbit stays whole when you miss a day.'" ON CAMERA: "It's not ready yet. So before anything: choose your one hour for tomorrow. Tell me in the comments."
- **0:36–0:45 (E).** ON CAMERA: "Shanivar. Android. In development. Waitlist in bio, or DM me 'WAIT'."

## R11: Some Things Can Wait

**Concept name:** Some Things Can Wait (launch hero)
**Campaign phase:** 4, Launch (Day 30, Sun 22 Nov)
**Audience insight:** Everything pretends to be urgent. The relief of deciding what can wait is the feeling they'd pay attention for.
**Saturn/Shani principle:** Boundaries and time. The slowest of the five planets the ancients watched.
**Source and interpretation (INTERNAL: not for public copy):**
- **Source:** NASA, Saturn's orbit is about 29.4 years (the longest of the five naked-eye classical planets).
- **Psychology:** notification cost (Stothart et al. 2015).
- **Shanivar reading:** you choose which apps can wait, and when. (Not "what reaches you": Shanivar does not control notifications.)
- The product lines are verbatim in-app copy.
**Format:** Brand film with the product line. Founder-shot; a direct product CTA.
**Duration:** 32 s

**Hook:** A wall of real notification sounds, then a hard cut to silence. Text: *Some things can wait.*

**Full script word-for-word with timestamps:**
- **0:00–0:03 (A).** SFX: 6 overlapping notification sounds. Hard cut to silence on black. Text.
- **0:03–0:12 (B).** VO (founder): "The group chat. The sale that ends tonight. The 10 p.m. 'quick call?'. The score. The reel someone sent. All of it says now."
- **0:12–0:20 (C).** VO: "Saturn is the slowest of the five planets the ancients watched. It never says now. Shanivar is being built on one idea from it: you choose which apps can wait, and when."
- **0:20–0:27 (D).** VO: "Tonight, pick one app that can wait until morning. Move it into a folder. Call the folder 'Saturday'."
- **0:27–0:32 (E).** VO: "Shanivar. Choose what can wait. Android. In development. Join the waitlist." Text: in-app lines.

**Visual direction shot by shot:**
1. Black, then the sound wall.
2. Abrupt real-life inserts, 0.8 s each:
   - a phone lighting up on a pillow,
   - a delivery-app style banner (generic; no brand),

3. **Long take (8 s):** a phone face down on a steel thali beside a chai cup. The phone vibrates twice (the visible jitter), and nobody touches it. The chai-ring stain on the steel echoes the Saturn ring.
4. A screen recording of the founder's own phone: moving one app into a folder he names "Saturday". Real, not a mock-up.
5. Type cards of the in-app lines on black (type-only, not UI): *Some things can wait.* · *Choose apps to make less accessible during your protected time.* · *Essential and emergency apps remain accessible.*
6. End card with the waitlist.

**Performance direction:** VO only: unhurried, close, slightly amused at "the sale that ends tonight".

**Sound design:**
- A layered notification wall, recorded from real devices with system sounds and **no third-party app jingles** (rights).
- A **full drop-out** at 0:03 and at 0:12.
- The long take has a vibration buzz on the steel, twice, then silence.

- End tone.

**On-screen text with timing:**

- 3–12: *group chat · "ends tonight" · "quick call?" · the score · the reel*

## R12: Shanivar Hour

**Concept name:** Shanivar Hour (weekly ritual)
**Campaign phase:** 4, Launch (Day 29, Sat 21 Nov, posted 10:00 IST for that evening)
**Audience insight:** People want to try this *together*, and a fixed weekly time beats a vague intention.
**Saturn/Shani principle:** Time, boundaries and return. Saturday as a recurring threshold.
**Source and interpretation (INTERNAL: not for public copy):**
- **Source:** MW, *Śani* = Saturday.
- **Psychology:** implementation intentions work through specified cues (Webb & Sheeran 2008). A fixed time and place is the cue.
- **Shanivar reading:** one hour every Saturday as a practice, not a detox claim.
**Format:** Participation ritual. Founder + 3 creators + community (Remix-friendly).
**Duration:** 35 s

**Hook:** Text over a clock face at 6:59: *This Saturday, 7 p.m. One hour. Phone in another room.*

**Full script word-for-word with timestamps:**
- **0:00–0:03 (A).** Text and the tick of a clock. VO (founder): "Tonight. Seven p.m. One hour."
- **0:03–0:13 (B).** VO, quick: "You'll want to check it at minute four. You'll invent an emergency at minute eleven. You'll wonder if anyone texted. They did. It can wait."
- **0:13–0:21 (C).** VO: "Shanivar is the Hindi word for Saturday, the day named for Shani, the slow planet. For one hour, be slow with it."
- **0:21–0:30 (D).** VO: "Seven to eight. Phone in another room. Do anything else: cook, walk, talk, nothing. At eight, one line: what did you notice?" Three creators each show the room where their phone will go (2 s each).
- **0:30–0:35 (E).** VO: "Every Saturday. Shanivar Hour. The app is in development; waitlist in bio."

**Visual direction shot by shot:**
1. Macro on an analogue wall clock at 6:59. The second hand is the only motion.
2. Abrupt cuts (1 s each):
   - a hand reaching for an empty table where the phone used to be,
   - a fake "emergency" glance at the door,
   - the founder's hand stopping mid-air.
   Played comically.
3. **Long take (8 s):** a phone on a chair in an empty, dim room. The screen lights once, and the camera doesn't move.
4. Three creators' doorways or rooms (vertical, their own homes), each placing the phone down and closing the door.
5. End: black, ring mark, *SHANIVAR HOUR · Saturdays 7–8 p.m. IST*.

**Performance direction:**
- Warm, inviting, a little funny. This is a party invitation, not a sermon.
- Creators keep their own words for their 2-second piece, e.g. "⟨Mine's going in the bathroom cabinet.⟩"

**Sound design:**
- The clock tick throughout A–B.
- B: comic stings from household sounds (a door creak, a cooker whistle).
- C: a sound drop-out.
- The long take: a buzz on a wooden chair, then nothing.
- End: a single tumbler strike.

**On-screen text with timing:**
- 0–3: *Tonight · 7 p.m. · one hour · phone in another room*
- 3–13: *minute 4 · minute 11 · "did anyone text?" · they did · it can wait*
- 13–21: *Shanivar = Saturday, Shani's day*
- 21–30: *7–8 p.m. · phone away · at 8: one line, "what did I notice?"*
- 30–35: *SHANIVAR HOUR · every Saturday* / *app in development · waitlist in bio / DM WAIT*

**Active-passive moment:** The clock moves, and a phone lights up in an empty room while nobody comes.

**Shanivar integration:** None needed. A soft waitlist line at the end.

**CTA:** "Do the hour. At 8, comment one thing you noticed." (A secondary waitlist line.)

**Caption ready to publish:**
> Shanivar Hour. Tonight, 7–8 p.m. IST.
>
> Phone in another room. Do anything else, or nothing.
> At 8, come back and write one line: what did you notice?
>
> Shanivar is the Hindi word for Saturday, the day named for Shani, the slow planet. For one hour, be slow with it.
>
> Every Saturday from now on.
> App in development for Android · waitlist in bio (or DM WAIT)
> #ShanivarHour #Shanivar #Saturday #Saturn #Attention

**Cover text:** *Tonight, 7 p.m. One hour.*

**UGC adaptation (3 creators):**
1. **CB4:** films 10 s at 7:00 and 10 s at 8:00 in the same frame, then reads their one line.
2. **CB1:** "What my roommates did when I did Shanivar Hour" (real, consented).
3. **CB5:** the family version: the whole family's phones in one basket. Consent from everyone filmed.

**Repurposing:**
- **Stories:** a countdown sticker to 19:00 IST; at 20:05 an "Add yours" sticker, "What did you notice?"
- **Carousel (Sunday):** "What you noticed", consented lines.
- **Shorts.**
- **WhatsApp:** **not used** (backlog; no unrequested outreach).

**Measurement:**
- Comments at 20:00–23:00 with a noticed line (**participation proof**).
- Countdown-sticker reminders set.
- "Add yours" entries.
- **Repeat participation the next Saturday** (the key metric of the ritual).

**Funnel line:**
- Problem: vague intentions.
- Human moment: minute four.
- Insight: a fixed time is the cue.
- Practice: the 7–8 p.m. hour.
- Feature: none.
- CTA: comment + waitlist.
- Destination: comments; link in bio / DM WAIT.
