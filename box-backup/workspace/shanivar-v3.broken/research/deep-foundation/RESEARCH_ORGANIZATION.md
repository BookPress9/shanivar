## Execution order

1. Complete registry audit before fresh external research; preserve original roles and identify missing expertise.
2. Review founder direction, tone, voice, audience learning, current product scope and source/release evidence. Record contradictions and supersession explicitly.
3. Research traditions/provenance, psychology and product patterns as bounded parallel or sequential assignments. Seven dedicated role charters do not require seven simultaneous runtime agents; limited slots are reused with explicit role briefs. Every role remains responsible for its distinct review questions.
4. Translate verified findings into practice, UX and journey proposals. Cross-reference source IDs; never silently treat a conceptual parallel as empirical validation.
5. Assemble Deep Foundation Report V1 and living knowledge files. Map each of the founder's 23 deliverables to artifacts. Audit links, claim provenance, source access, alternative explanations and product relevance.
6. Present concrete PROPOSED stance additions and prioritized decisions for founder acceptance. Research continues independently while decisions are pending; no entire-app redesign is implied.

## Knowledge governance

`SHANIVAR_STANCE.md` is the central manually governable intellectual stance. Entry states are PROPOSED, ACCEPTED, REJECTED and SUPERSEDED. New agent findings always enter PROPOSED. Acceptance needs an explicit founder decision reference and date, not a citation count, model vote, elapsed time or coordinator judgment. Founder direction already explicitly given may be recorded as direction with its source; inferred philosophy may not be silently accepted.

Each important entry records topic, tradition, primary text, commentary, modern scholarship, what sources say, disagreements, Shanivar interpretation, product/practice implications, claim limits, founder decision and date accepted. A revision preserves the old entry and states what superseded it. An accepted product experiment does not automatically accept a metaphysical proposition, certify clinical safety or authorize publication.

The source ledger distinguishes bibliographic discovery, abstract/summary access, inspected excerpt and inspected full text. Record publisher/author/year, edition/translator, original terms, location/verse, URL and access date, disagreement, confidence and precise supported claim. Do not write “read the book” when only a preview was available. Major topics need multiple independent sources; primary texts and scholarship answer different questions. Internet discourse can reveal concerns, not establish historical truth.

## Finding-to-product contract

Every major insight must include:

- SOURCE: what was actually learned, with inspectable citation and access depth.
- CONFIDENCE: why evidence supports that limited conclusion; contrary results and confounds.
- PRINCIPLE: transferable inference, labelled as such.
- PRODUCT IMPLICATION: smallest change worth considering, tied to current scope.
- UX EXPERIMENT: comparator, eligible situation, one changed variable and ethical exit.
- SUCCESS SIGNAL: observable comprehension, chosen action or voluntary report with denominator.
- FAILURE SIGNAL: confusion, coercion, distress, dependence, privacy cost or technical failure.
- DECISION: proposed adopt / test / hold / reject, with owner and evidence needed next.

Practice proposals additionally need intended setting, optional duration, source/adaptation separation, accessible external-attention alternative, stop/skip behavior and appropriate review level. Ordinary attention is distinct from intensive retention, fasting, sleep restriction, strong somatic or concentration work, kundalini/altered states and sexual practices. A text's historical inclusion is not public-release authorization.

## Reporting and QA

Each assignment reports: question answered; owned files; source/access ledger; findings; disputes; current evidence limitations; acceptance checks performed; pending dependencies; next action. The coordinator reconciles inconsistencies in writing. Reports identify charter invocation versus actual live process; no role is advertised as continuously working without a live runtime and operational evidence.

For this run the registry audit was completed first by a bounded Staff Manager invocation. Subsequent runtime assignments and their actual outputs must be recorded by the coordinator in the final report; this document does not pre-claim that all specialists executed or independently reviewed one another.

Quality checks: tradition separation; accurate verse/edition attribution; no false neuroscience equivalence; clear original synthesis; current product evidence; no fabricated interviews or analytics; useful off-app outcome; plain patient-teacher voice; agency and easy exit; no spiritual ranks; no covert expansion of launch scope. A source review is not a clinical review. An automated test is not a phone/device test. A rendered mockup is not a shipped feature.

Final five-minute founder review should present a small decision queue with the proposition, supporting and contrary evidence, minimum experiment and consequence of holding. Keep the intellectual depth beneath a simple surface; do not make the founder reread the entire library to make one product decision.
