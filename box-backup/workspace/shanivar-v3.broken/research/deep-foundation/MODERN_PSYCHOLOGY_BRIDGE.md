These parallels are Shanivar's comparative synthesis. Traditional source details and disagreement must accompany them before any authoritative knowledge entry is accepted.

## Evidence that changes the product decision

**The useful part of an interruption may be choice, not a profound message.** The [one sec experiment, P02](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36795756/) separates delay, deliberation and a dismissal option. Its results make a simple choice control essential when testing additional Shanivar content. Do not attribute its field reduction to breathwork or transfer its percentages into Shanivar marketing.

**Attention alone and a nonjudgmental stance are different interventions.** [Chin et al., P05](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6681655/) support studying acceptance alongside monitoring, with limitations documented in the ledger. Proposed principle: instructions should not turn noticing into another performance test. A phrase-level app experiment is still a new intervention.

**Promising breath findings coexist with active-control null results.** [Balban et al., P06](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9873947/) and [Fincham et al., P07](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-49279-8) tested different practices, controls and outcomes. They are not a direct replication pair. Their combination argues for modest wording, credible comparators and comfort-led selection rather than claims that one rhythm reliably resets the nervous system. No evidence here justifies clock-based hormonal optimization.

**Autonomy is an ethical commitment with context-dependent behavioral effects.** [P10](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30834136/) found no framing effect; the much larger [P11](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35622891/) found some motivational differences but no behavioral-intention effect. Merely writing “your choice” does not compensate for an inaccessible exit. Test actual control and comprehension, not friendliness alone.

**Body awareness should not become a single product score.** The [MAIA development study, P04](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0048230) provides reasons to distinguish dimensions. Shanivar's current interaction does not measure a nervous-system state, interoceptive accuracy or consciousness.

## Proposed source-to-experiment decisions

| Source / confidence | Principle and product implication | Experiment | Success / failure | Proposed decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P02; moderate for the reported mechanism, low transfer certainty | Add meaning only if it helps beyond an obvious choice | Compare dismissal choice alone with same choice plus one optional sensory invitation; keep delay constant | More chosen off-app action per eligible episode / extra irritation or no incremental value | TEST; remove content if it adds no value |
| P04/P05; moderate construct evidence, low micropractice efficacy evidence | Let people choose an anchor without trying to produce a state | Compare one suggested anchor with suggestion plus visible “try another”; both permit exit | Understood alternatives and voluntary return / choice burden or discomfort | TEST usability before outcome claims |
| P01/P15; context-dependent mechanism | Reflect concretely without demanding thought suppression | Compare one action-oriented question with existing generic reflection | Useful next action / recursive analysis or pressure to empty the mind | TEST; no treatment language |
| P03; causal study of a materially different restriction | Test practical focus boundaries rather than brain-repair claims | Compare user-selected scheduled windows with start-now focus for the same recurring goal | Independently useful repeated windows / bypass, essential-access failures, collateral blocking | TEST after engineering safety verification |
| P08/P09/P18; credible evidence of possible harms, uncertain app-specific rate | Stopping and alternative anchors are ordinary options | Moderated comprehension review of stop/skip, then consented low-intensity usability pilot | Participants can stop and explain alternatives / persistence through unwanted effects | ADOPT control requirement as proposal; HOLD stronger safety claims |

For every experiment predefine an observation window, denominator, comparator and stopping rule before examining results. Missing reports stay missing. Small usability cohorts establish comprehension problems, not treatment efficacy. Do not deliberately induce distress, unsafe breath changes or sleep loss to test a safety boundary.

## What the bridge cannot currently answer

The inspected literature does not establish durable independent practice from a ten-second app pause, personalized protocol selection from emotion labels, a biological optimum tied to time of day, population-wide spiritual benefit, or the comparative efficacy of Shanivar's eight Steady scripts. International transfer, accessibility and meaning across languages require direct work. Evidence gaps should narrow public claims while research continues; they need not prevent a clearly labelled low-intensity prototype.
