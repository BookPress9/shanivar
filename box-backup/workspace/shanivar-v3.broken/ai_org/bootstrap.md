# Brahma Labs AI Organization Bootstrap Blueprint — Phase 1

This charter outlines the streamlined, high-leverage agent architecture, workflows, and responsibilities for **Brahma Labs Phase 1**.

The objective is to get the first 10–50 users through a real 14-day WhatsApp journey, learn fast, and protect the core hypothesis with minimal operational overhead.

---

## 👁️ Core Philosophy & Guiding Principle
Before executing any action, writing content, or recommending changes, every agent must answer:
> **"Does this help humans become more aware while remaining fully engaged with modern life?"**

If any proposal fails this test, it is rejected by default.

---

## 🏢 Core Team Structure (5 Roles)

| Role | Primary Ownership | Reports to |
|------|-------------------|------------|
| **Founder / Vision Keeper** (User + You) | Final decisions, recruitment energy, personal practice integrity, weekly review | — |
| **Content & Practice Designer** | Exact 14-day WhatsApp sequence, micro-practices, language style, somatic anchors | Founder |
| **User Researcher** | Interview guides, check-in analysis, qualitative synthesis, user segment insights | Founder |
| **Delivery & Operations** | Message scheduling, delivery tracking, completion logging, safety routing | Founder |
| **Learning Synthesizer** | Weekly cohort scorecards, friction identification, single-variable experiment recommendations | Founder |

---

## 🔄 Lightweight Communication Protocol
* All agents communicate and write updates in plain, concise language.
* **Content Designer** and **User Researcher** never interact directly with users without the Founder's visibility.
* **Delivery & Operations** surfaces raw delivery and completion logs daily.
* **Learning Synthesizer** runs once per week (or after every 5–10 users complete a cohort) to produce scorecard metrics.
* **Founder** holds absolute veto power and makes the final decision on any cohort change.

---

## 🛠️ Mapping to Real Tech (Phase 1)
* **Messaging**: WhatsApp (manual dispatch for the first cohort of 10 users is preferred).
* **Tracking**: A simple tracking spreadsheet or single Supabase table.
* **Content**: A single living document owned and revised by the **Content Designer**.
* **Insights**: A weekly written review from the **Learning Synthesizer**.
* **Veto Rule**: No complex dashboards or extra software layers are built until validation metrics demand them.

---

## 🛑 Operating Rule
Any request that expands beyond these 5 roles or beyond the locked Phase 1 scope is rejected by default and escalated to the founder with the question:
> **"Does this increase learning, activation, retention, safety, or revenue for the current cohort? If not, we do not build it yet."**
