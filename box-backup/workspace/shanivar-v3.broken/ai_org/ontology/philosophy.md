## 🧘 The Six Stages of Consciousness Evolution

1. **Survival (Autopilot)**
   * *State*: Fight-or-flight, reactive mind, stress-driven.
   * *Practice*: Somatic grounding, heavy belly box breathing.
2. **Social Identity (Conditioned Self)**
   * *State*: Inherited values, seeking external validation.
   * *Practice*: Self-inquiry, identity mapping journals.
3. **Self-Awareness (The Observer)**
   * *State*: The ability to watch thoughts without being consumed by them.
   * *Practice*: Silent meditation, witnessing transits.
4. **Intentional Identity (The Architect)**
   * *State*: Designing habits, beliefs, and purpose consciously.
   * *Practice*: Focus engineering, progressive habit building.
5. **Conscious No-Mind (The Flow State)**
   * *State*: Merging action and awareness; effortless execution.
   * *Practice*: High-intensity focus sessions, binaural beats integration.
6. **Cosmic Consciousness (Unity)**
   * *State*: Boundary dissolution between inner observer and external universe.
   * *Practice*: Advanced transcendental contemplation.

## ⚡ The Skeptic's Contemplative Framework
Every meditation script or daily note must verify its references:
* **Neurological Claim**: Must list the scientific analog (e.g. *Theta waves for neuroplasticity*, *parasympathetic activation via Vagus nerve stimulation*).
* **Mystical Translation**: Keep terms metaphorically clear. Do not claim absolute spiritual truths. Present them as "experiential experiments" for the user to validate.
* **Simplification**: Strip away 30% of adjectives in voice scripts. Let silence hold space.
