The laptop is closed

Caption

The laptop is closed. The work lamp is still on. You sit down for a break and open the same app again.

Sometimes a free evening begins with a question we have not asked yet: what would I like this time to be for?

Tea while it is warm. A few pages. A call. Doing very little. Your answer can be small.

If it suits tonight, put the phone beside you and begin that one thing. You can decide what happens after.

Save the question for the next evening that starts on autopilot.

Two cups, one question

Caption

Two cups on the table. Your parent starts a story you have heard before. Your phone lights up.

You could ask what you have never asked: what do you remember most about that day?

If you both feel like talking, let the phones sit aside for one cup of tea. There is no need to turn the conversation into a lesson about anyone's habits.

And if today is not the day, the invitation can wait.

Try one curious question with someone who welcomes it.
