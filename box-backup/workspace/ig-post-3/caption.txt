You let the world wait. You sat. You came back, again and again.

Thirty-three sittings are not a finish line.
They are a threshold. You have only just been let in.

What comes next is not handed to you.
The next path is yours.

Sit once more today, and notice who is sitting.

Shanivar. Discipline is freedom.
