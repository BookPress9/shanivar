# Shanivar V3 — Research and adaptation register

This register records what the wireframe is allowed to claim. It supports the founder's stronger coaching stance without turning direct language into unsupported health, psychological or spiritual claims.

## Product foundation

The experience is organized around two jobs:

1. **Routine** — convert intentions into visible personal structure.
2. **Sadhana** — practise a bounded skill until it can increasingly happen without the app.

The design may be forceful about action. It must remain truthful about what the product can observe or know.

## Daily rhythm and sleep boundary

The user supplies two anchors: usual wake time and intended sleep time.

Shanivar may use these preferences to place routine suggestions and help the user avoid scheduling personal practices in a way that obviously crowds sleep. It does **not** claim to calculate:

- circadian phase;
- cortisol timing;
- dosha;
- a biologically perfect wake time;
- an individualized medical sleep prescription.

NHLBI healthy-sleep guidance supports making enough time for sleep and maintaining a consistent schedule where practical. Traditional *dinacharya* provides cultural context for a structured daily routine, but not evidence that one fixed schedule fits every user.

Sources:

- https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep-deprivation/healthy-sleep-habits
- AYUSH daily-routine / health-and-wellness guidance where relevant to the broader foundation work

## App protection boundary

App protection is behavior design, not a diagnosis of addiction or compulsive use.

The product can let the user choose apps and repeated windows. A native build must distinguish configuration from actual device enforcement. Saving a rule does not prove that Android permissions are granted or that blocking is active.

The wireframe includes broad app categories because the founder specifically wants the user to choose the feeds, social apps, entertainment apps and easy escape routes that interfere with chosen work.

Essential access and immediate local recovery remain product requirements.

## Sadhana and practice boundary

The current foundation practice is **natural breathing**.

Product instruction:

- do not deliberately deepen the breath;
- do not slow the breath to match animation;
- do not count breaths;
- do not add breath holds;
- notice the breathing already happening;
- when attention moves, return to the next natural breath.

The moving Saturn visual is a visual marker for the practice session. It is **not** a respiration pacer and must not imply a target breathing rate.

The practice is a low-intensity attention exercise. Completion may record elapsed time and voluntary feedback, but does not establish calmness, awareness, enlightenment, nervous-system regulation or another mental/physiological state.

More intensive breath manipulation, retention, fasting, sleep restriction, heat/cold exposure, trauma excavation, sexual practices, kundalini practices and altered-state techniques remain outside generic challenge progression unless separately reviewed.

## Reflection boundary

Direct coaching can ask the user to notice a pattern, contradiction or chosen action. It should not infer hidden motives or diagnose mental health from product behavior.

Useful pattern:

`concrete observation -> useful question -> one action`

Avoid:

- interpreting intrusive thoughts as proof of trauma;
- diagnosing addiction, narcissism, learned helplessness or treatment need;
- claiming astrology or AI reveals inner truth;
- rewarding distress or intensity;
- requiring disclosure of private journal content.

## Serious voice without coercion

Founder direction asks for a voice that is wild, real, direct, clear, goal-oriented and serious.

That stance is compatible with user agency when Shanivar is decisive about the **task** without pretending certainty about the **person**.

Good:

> Put it on the calendar. Start today.

Good:

> Attention moved. Return to the next breath. No argument.

Avoid:

> I know why you keep failing.

Avoid:

> If you skip this, you are not serious about growth.

The app can challenge behavior; it should not shame identity.

## Soft gamification — intentionally open

V3 does not finalize Sadhana gamification.

The next design pass should decide:

- what counts as evidence of practice;
- what milestones represent competence rather than screen time;
- how chapters, challenges and independent practice relate;
- how missed days recover without streak shame;
- whether sensor-verified real-world actions belong in the system;
- what visual progression feels serious rather than childish;
- how the product rewards needing the app less, not more.

Current chapter labels are provisional information architecture, not a finalized progression model or spiritual hierarchy.

## Measurement principle

Keep distinct:

- app-observed event: timer ran / action button used / device capability status;
- sensor-derived event: only when technically implemented and disclosed;
- user self-report;
- interpretation.

Do not silently convert one category into another.
