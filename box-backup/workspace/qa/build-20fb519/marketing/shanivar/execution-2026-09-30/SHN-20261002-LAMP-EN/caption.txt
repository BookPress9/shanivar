The laptop is closed. The work lamp is still on. You sit down for a break and open the same app again.

Sometimes a free evening begins with a question we have not asked yet: what would I like this time to be for?

Tea while it is warm. A few pages. A call. Doing very little. Your answer can be small.

If it suits tonight, put the phone beside you and begin that one thing. You can decide what happens after.

Save the question for the next evening that starts on autopilot.
