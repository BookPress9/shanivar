## One active experiment: language accessibility

Pairs L1=P01, L2=P04, L3=P08 and L4=P11 compare native EN/HI editions of the same concept. Alternate first language across pairs. Use the same scene order, dimensions, proposition, CTA and baseline timing. Translate for comprehension and natural cadence; do not add a stronger promise to one edition. Record voice/reading-duration adjustments as confounds.

Each pair is three to five days apart. Audience overlap, weekday, topic, maturation and delivery can differ, so this is an **observational pilot**, not a causal A/B test. Do not describe summed reach as unique people or incremental Hindi reach. Story language polls are self-selected preferences, not population estimates. Hindi is one additional language, not a stand-in for all India or for older people.

During this month, hold the language plan and house treatment stable. Weekly reviews can correct errors, readability and false feature claims immediately. They should otherwise propose experiments for the next cycle rather than changing hook, length, time and language together. Check for duplicate-topic fatigue before releasing each companion; if omitted or changed, record the broken pair and exclude it from direct comparison.

Only Trial Reels with verified account access and approval may be used. Keep Trial and ordinary feed delivery in separate cohorts; never compare a Trial edition with a normal edition as a clean language comparison. Review auto-sharing settings before a trial so it does not unexpectedly enter the main feed. Trial metrics timing and product availability should be rechecked in the actual account.

## Capture metrics without manufacturing performance

`metrics.csv` contains blank 24-hour, 168-hour and 672-hour rows for BASE-01 and each of 16 proposed publications. Fill only actual observations. Preserve blank for unavailable fields; enter 0 only when Instagram explicitly reports zero. Use ISO timestamps with UTC offsets. `window_hours` is the desired observation age; record actual publication and snapshot times and disclose any offset.

| Signal | Calculation, only when inputs exist | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Save rate | 100 × saves / reach | Save is not practice completion. |
| Share rate | 100 × shares / reach | Shares are actions, not necessarily distinct people. |
| Profile-visit rate | 100 × post-attributed profile visits / reach | Do not substitute account-wide visits. |
| Follow rate | 100 × post-attributed follows / reach | Do not substitute total follower growth. |
| Non-follower share of reach | 100 × non-follower accounts reached / reach | Do not mix view-based percentages with reach. |
| Watch-time ratio | average watch seconds / video duration | Not completion rate; repeats can make it exceed 1. |
| Destination conversion | attributed signups / attributable landing sessions | Unavailable until the destination and events are verified. |

Denominator 0 or missing makes the rate unavailable. No invented slide-level retention, Reel completion, profile-to-follow funnel or audience demographics. Likes/comments are secondary descriptive counts, not evidence of self-realization. Show raw numerators/denominators beside rates. Separate formats and languages and compare equal exposure windows.

**Operational decision rule:** wait for at least three mature, comparable same-format observations per language before even considering a repeatable pattern. This is a house minimum, not statistical significance. This month's pairs alone may not meet it, particularly for Reels; then carry the test into the next cycle. A large rate based on one or two actions is inconclusive. Do not let the threshold force a winner.

Decision examples:

- Too few/unknown Insights: hold the creative direction and collect the next comparable snapshots. Do not increase frequency to repair an unknown problem.
- Several Reels lose viewers before the question and readability is sound: propose a shorter opening for next cycle while keeping concept/language/duration otherwise comparable. No guarantee of improved ranking.
- Useful save/share rates recur but profile interest is weak: review whether the actual profile explains Shanivar. Do not invent a waitlist link.
- Hindi consistently earns useful actions in comparable cohorts: propose one more Hindi original next month; retain English and do not infer nationwide coverage.
- A concept gets views with little evidence of interest: do not call it acquisition. Keep attention, signup and activation separate.
