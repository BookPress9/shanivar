## Campaign direction

**Central idea:** A familiar urge to scroll becomes an invitation to understand yourself. Shanivar speaks as a patient teacher: a precise observation, a useful question, room for an honest response. The week moves through attention, personalized guidance, family connection and growing older with awareness.

**Brand proposition:** Shanivar is being built to bring personalized astrological charts, current planetary context and AI-guided reflection into everyday self-discovery. Astrology provides an interpretive lens; users' own experiences shape the conversation. Self-realization is the direction of practice, never a guaranteed result.

**Creative treatment:** Professional editorial typography, bone text, charcoal backgrounds and restrained bronze lines. Pair human scenes with spacious message cards. Abstract orbital lines are decorative, never fabricated charts. All assets carry “SHANIVAR / BY THE HYBRID MONK.” Days 2 and 4 carry a readable “IN DEVELOPMENT · CONCEPT PREVIEW” label throughout, including covers.

**Formats:** Four single images at 1080 × 1350; three silent, text-led Reels at 1080 × 1920, 30 seconds each; three Reel covers. Keep essential text clear of platform controls and check profile crops. No sound-dependent meaning or rapid flashes. Generated scenes are illustrative, not customer photography.

**Learning objective:** Identify which situation earns the highest save rate after equal seven-day exposure. Report saves/reach and raw counts where available. This small pilot is directional learning, not a controlled format test or evidence of practice completion.

## Day 1 — Before the next version of you

**ID/channel:** W1-01 · Instagram feed. **Purpose:** Introduce the teacher voice through comparison and identity. **Audience:** Adult Gen Z navigating study, early work and online expectations.

**Exact image text:**

> Before the next version of you,
> a moment with this one.

**Caption — final draft:**

> Someone your age has moved abroad. Someone has built a company. Someone's morning looks remarkably organised.
>
> You haven't finished your tea.
>
> Before you turn another person's life into an instruction for yours, pause for two comfortable breaths. Feel your feet on the floor.
>
> What do you want to give your attention to today?
>
> I'm Shanivar. Let's begin with a question you can answer for yourself.

**Visual brief:** Young adult at an ordinary breakfast table, notebook open, phone face down. Warm side light, candid posture, generous headline space. Avoid luxury lifestyle cues and staged meditation poses. Target: `week-01-v3/assets/day-01.png`.

## Day 3 — After everyone else's questions

**ID/channel:** W1-03 · Instagram feed. **Purpose:** Connect responsibility with personal awareness. **Audience:** Millennials moving between work, household tasks and care for others.

**Exact image text:**

> You've answered everyone today.
> What would you ask yourself?

**Caption — final draft:**

> Can you move the meeting? What's for dinner? Did you book the appointment?
>
> By evening, you've answered a lot of questions.
>
> When there's a little space, let one be yours.
>
> What have I needed today that I haven't named?
>
> Notice your jaw, shoulders and hands. Then see whether a word arrives: rest, company, quiet, help. Something else entirely.
>
> You can begin with an answer you haven't worked out how to act on yet.
>
> — Shanivar

**Visual brief:** Adult in their thirties or forties at a kitchen counter after work: keys, shopping bag, unfinished tea. Quiet pause rather than exaggerated distress. Headline in negative space. Target: `week-01-v3/assets/day-03.png`.

**Alt text draft:** An adult pauses at a kitchen counter with keys, a shopping bag and tea. Text: “You've answered everyone today. What would you ask yourself?”

**CTA/destination:** Notice the body and privately name a need. **Source:** Body observation adapted from [Phase 1, Week 1, Day 3](../../ai_org/shanivaar_agents.md); naming a need is an original reflective prompt, not a treatment. **Status:** Copy ready for review; image pending; publication not authorized.

## Day 5 — Let the answer take its time

**Exact image text:**

> Ask how their day felt.
> Let the answer take its time.

**Caption — final draft:**

> “Did you eat?”
>
> “Yes. Did you?”
>
> Familiar questions can carry a lot of care. There may be room for another one.
>
> If a conversation with a parent feels welcome, try: “How has today felt for you?”
>
> Put the phone aside if you're together. On a call, close the other app. Listen without preparing advice. Let them decide how much to say.
>
> Caring about a parent's wellbeing can include making room for their own words.
>
> — Shanivar

**Visual brief:** Adult child and parent over two cups of tea; equal eye level and visual weight. Parent is an active participant. Avoid rescue/coaching poses. Target: `week-01-v3/assets/day-05.png`.

## Day 7 — What became clearer?

**ID/channel:** W1-07 · Instagram feed. **Purpose:** Close the arc and connect self-realization with ordinary noticing. **Audience:** Across adult life stages.

**Exact image text:**

> What became a little clearer
> when you paused?

**Caption — final draft:**

> The morning you compared yourself. The yes that arrived too quickly. The conversation you gave more time.
>
> Perhaps one moment from this week stayed with you.
>
> Take a quiet minute. Finish this sentence for yourself: “I'm beginning to notice…”
>
> It might be a habit, a need or something you want to make room for. If nothing comes, notice where you're sitting and begin there.
>
> Self-realization is the direction behind Shanivar: a continuing practice of seeing yourself more clearly and choosing how you want to live.
>
> Keep the sentence. See what life adds to it.

**Visual brief:** Open notebook on a lived-in table with “I'm beginning to notice…” and blank space below. Bronze pen, morning light, cup echoing Day 1. Headline separate from notebook text. Target: `week-01-v3/assets/day-07.png`.
