## Engineering coordination

The concurrently available [technical map](../../SHANIVAR_TECHNICAL_MAP.md), [engineering bridge](../../GPT_TECH_BRIDGE.md) and [integration review](../../CLAUDE_TECHNICAL_INTEGRATION_V1.md) are engineering inputs. Their feasibility classifications do not accept research proposals on behalf of the founder. Their recorded tests are attributed to that engineering review; this report did not rerun the suites. Source-inspected defects should be addressed within the engineer's authorized task without waiting for an entire philosophical program. Conversely, a P1 label cannot silently approve a new public feature.

No decision here authorizes migration work, AI integration, account changes, deployment, production writes, new notifications or recruitment. The next concrete founder review concerns a small set of alternatives, not approval of a vaguely defined “deep app.”

## Decision record template

For an actual disposition append: PD ID/version; accepted/rejected/test/hold; exact proposition; founder message or approved record; date; scope and limits; responsible owner; permitted next action; evidence/review trigger; superseded entry. Do not rewrite a proposal row into history without preserving the change. A decision to test validates neither a mechanism nor a doctrine.
