## Triangulation used

- Sāṃkhya: Sanskrit textual locus plus scholarly explanation; Yoga: selected primary/commentary locus plus scholarly doctrinal account and authorship debate.
- Upaniṣads/Vedānta: named historical passage and distinct school scholarship, with primary-commentary access gaps explicit. Broad distinctions are better supported than every compressed technical formulation.
- Buddhist frameworks: selected translated discourses plus later-tradition scholarship; unsuccessful access to a second translation is not counted as independent confirmation.
- Śaiva/Śākta/VBT: named VBT commentary plus historical scholarship and modern interpretive sources. Manuscript variants and restricted scholarly access remain visible.
- Behavioral design: distinct app-interruption research, broader restriction trials and habit/attention work. None tests the exact Shanivar bundle or proves a traditional doctrine.
- Audience: cross-national survey context, US barrier/motivation studies and situated cultural accounts. None establishes international product demand.
- Product patterns: 21 product profiles with official documentation; current local code constrains what Shanivar can responsibly borrow.

Triangulation means looking for independent viewpoints and limitations, not treating three summaries of one text or company claim as three confirmations. A primary study and a media article about that study are one underlying evidence source.

## Source-to-product claim rule

Every major insight in the workstream maps includes source, confidence, principle, product implication, experiment, success/failure and proposed decision. Implementation or marketing claims require evidence of the actual released behavior as well. A clinical result cannot be transferred to a one-minute script merely because both involve breathing.

No copyrighted book is reproduced here. Quotations are kept minimal; scripts and product proposals are original and identified as adaptations or modern exercises. Bibliographic leads remain leads until their relevant text is inspected. Public source links may change; preserve access dates and the exact edition rather than assuming a live page is immutable.
