## Main finding

Shanivar's distinctive opportunity is to connect a concrete everyday interruption with an optional, accurately attributed experience, then help the person return to life. The intellectual foundation must be plural and precise; the interface need not display that taxonomy at entry. **Simple access and deep provenance belong together.** Neither complexity nor Sanskrit vocabulary demonstrates depth.

Three boundaries change the product direction most:

- Similar attention exercises do not make their traditions philosophically interchangeable. Preserve the difference between the experience, its traditional interpretation and the app's adaptation.
- A short intervention may restore a choice point without changing a person's underlying condition. Test usefulness in context; do not sell physiological engineering, clinical treatment or a proven route to realization.
- Discipline is chosen and recoverable. Focus reliability, essential access and honest status matter more than making restrictions harder to escape. Strong protection should never become an authority over the person.

The first useful next step is a small comparison of entry experiences and a focused engineering reliability review, alongside deeper source work. It is not a complete navigation rewrite, a guru chatbot or a library expansion. The report below addresses all 23 requested areas; linked files carry the detailed evidence and limitations.

## 5. Ancient knowledge source map

The map follows named texts and interpretive traditions. Classical Sāṃkhya distinguishes cognitive instruments from consciousness; Pātañjala Yoga locates attentional discipline within ethical and liberative concerns. The Upaniṣads are a layered corpus, and later Vedāntas disagree about self, world and divine relation. Buddhist discourses and later analytical traditions supply different accounts of experience. Śaiva and Śākta systems require text- and lineage-specific treatment. [Detailed map](ANCIENT_KNOWLEDGE_MAP.md); [fifteen provenance records](SOURCE_LEDGER_ANCIENT.md).

For a concrete contrast, Ruzsa's scholarly account describes classical Sāṃkhya's plurality of puruṣas and distinction from prakṛti. This is not the same thesis as a single universal consciousness. A useful body-attention cue need not settle that disagreement. [Ruzsa, Sāṃkhya](https://iep.utm.edu/sankhya/).

Historical access problems cannot be explained by one preselected decline story. Research a region, period, institution and text family; distinguish manuscript survival, patronage, practice continuity, translation, colonial interpretation and modern commercialization. Current scholarship supports that differentiated method, not a marketing narrative that Shanivar has recovered a single lost science.

## 6. Manovigyana conceptual map

Use Manovigyana as an editorial umbrella for studying mind, not a claim that one standardized ancient discipline maps cleanly to modern psychology. The working model separates a situation, what is sensed, what is interpreted, a habitual tendency and a possible next action. That is Shanivar's synthesis, not a translated ancient diagram. [MANOVIGYANA_MODELS.md](MANOVIGYANA_MODELS.md).

Rumination, planning, attention switching, avoidance and reflection can look similar from outside while serving different purposes. “Out of the head” must not become hostility to thinking: deliberate analysis may be exactly what a person needs. A notification cannot infer avoidance, and inward bodily attention is not always the most comfortable anchor. Preserve an external option and the person's own explanation.

Keep historical provenance, experiential action and product routing as three separate maps. Traditional purpose does not establish a modern matching rule for a feeling or time of day. VBT is one important source, not all Tantra and not 112 universally safe breathing prescriptions. [Tantra practice map](TANTRA_PRACTICE_MAP.md).

They explicitly identify source inspiration or modern synthesis, retained/omitted/added elements, setting, alternatives and success/failure observations. The beginner no-hold policy is a deliberate adaptation of source material, not a discovery that the source contains only unpaced breathing. Source fidelity means showing the change honestly. Ritual, initiatory, subtle-body, intense or transgressive practices can be studied without becoming self-guided app instructions. [Safety boundaries](PRACTICE_SAFETY.md).

## 8. Tradition-separation matrix

The [matrix](TRADITION_SEPARATION_MATRIX.md) compares Sāṃkhya, Pātañjala Yoga, Upaniṣadic material, distinct Vedāntas, early Buddhist discourses, Abhidharma, Yogācāra, Madhyamaka and several Śaiva/Śākta contexts. It also identifies misleading lexical matches: manas/citta/vijñāna, saṃskāra, prāṇa, śūnyatā and dhyāna do not have one universal English equivalent.

Practical comparisons are useful: repeated return, examining identification, ethical consequences and careful attention. Those similarities do not establish identical ontology, common historical origin or a shared proven neural mechanism. Do not explain Buddhism as hidden Advaita, devotion as a lower stage of nondualism, or tantric energy as an instrument-measured physiological quantity.

## 9. Modern psychology bridge

The [bridge](MODERN_PSYCHOLOGY_BRIDGE.md) separates traditional model, modern construct, possible parallel and empirical finding. The [psychology ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER_PSYCHOLOGY.md) records study design, samples where inspected, access depth and confounds. This prevents attractive analogies from becoming scientific endorsements.

Two findings are especially useful for product restraint. The one sec study includes both a field sample and a separate dismantling experiment; an explicit decision opportunity and delay cannot be treated as equivalent to any chosen contemplative explanation. Separately, a 400-person coherent-breathing trial did not establish superiority over its active pacing comparator. These studies support testing mechanisms rather than transferring a generic “breathing works” claim to VBT or Shanivar. [one sec primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36795756/); [coherent-breathing trial](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-49279-8).

Attention to experience, acceptance, repetition and context may matter in studied interventions, but their doses/populations differ from our proposed micro-practices. Do not claim that one breath treats rumination, upgrades intelligence, repairs dopamine or optimizes the nervous system. Unusual or difficult experiences are neither automatic illness nor automatic spiritual progress.

## 12. Progressive disclosure from high-scale products

Transfer principles, not visual copies. A sequence can help someone start; saved choices can reduce rediscovery; explicit correction can improve relevance; a consistent action can be learned through use. These are hypotheses extracted from documented patterns, not comparative efficacy findings.

Do not borrow endless continuation from feeds, streak-loss pressure from gamified products, score reassurance from wearables, or a companion's emotional demands. A source/detail layer should deepen understanding on request, while a completed practice supplies a real exit. [UX pattern library and evidence](UX_BEHAVIOR_PATTERNS.md).

One meaningful decision per screen is compatible with a small number of screens. It does not mean spreading a long intake across a long wizard. Measure total effort, not the apparent simplicity of each page.

## 13. Proposed sadhak journey

Explorer, beginner, returning practitioner, committed sadhak and deep practitioner are chosen relationships to practice, not levels. The [journey](SADHAK_JOURNEY.md) specifies useful entries, optional depth, evidence of fit and departure for each.

A long-term practitioner can choose a basic external anchor; an explorer can read a source comparison immediately. No amount of app use unlocks spiritual status or proves readiness for intensive practice. Structured plans and deeper study need consent and context, not an engagement-derived rank.

## 14. Navigation architecture principles

Keep alarm and focus utilities recognizable and independent. Do not make someone understand Yoga/Tantra/VBT/astrology taxonomy to protect an hour. Present a useful current action, then allow Change and contextual depth. An active alarm, protection state or recovery need takes precedence over teaching.

Proposed loop: **recognize the moment → one optional experience → observe if useful → choose an ordinary action → leave**. Explanation and deeper study branch off when requested. NOTICE → UNDERSTAND → EXPERIENCE → OBSERVE → INTEGRATE is a teaching intention, not five compulsory screens.

The current Steady wireframe asks several feeling/time/context and comfort questions. A smaller external-anchor entry is worth comparing, but safety gates cannot simply be deleted to reduce taps. The research recommends a reversible prototype test before changing the default interface. [UX-01 and interaction grammar](UX_BEHAVIOR_PATTERNS.md).

## 20. Unresolved philosophical and product questions

Does Shanivar remain plural or eventually declare a specific doctrinal home? How should an experience be presented when traditions interpret it incompatibly? How much source context is necessary before a practice, rather than available afterward? Who independently reviews translations and practice adaptations, and how are corrections communicated?

What qualifies as a helpful ordinary observation without making inward attention compulsory? When does reflection become more thinking instead of useful inquiry? What support should remain available to a committed practitioner with an existing teacher? Where should study end and referral to qualified guidance begin?

For Focus, what exact local recovery interaction preserves essential access without disguising ordinary extension? How should local suspension outrank stale server/rule state? For international work, which language/context combinations can the team actually support? None of these is settled by app engagement.

## 22. Recommended experiments

The [experiment register](EXPERIMENT_REGISTER.md) specifies seven designs: minimum useful entry; provenance/depth; international discovery; focus reliability; essential recovery; voluntary return/departure; social experience prototypes. Each names source/confidence, mechanism, comparator or procedure, observable success, failure and proposed disposition.

Begin with exact content review and safety/reliability gates for enforcing trials. Non-enforcing comprehension prototypes and discovery can proceed independently once appropriately arranged. Later voluntary follow-up distinguishes useful support from guilt, misunderstanding, technical failure and unknown departure. Small cohorts identify design problems; they do not prove population-level efficacy.

The common outcome question is: **Did this help the person make a choice they wanted to make, with less confusion and no loss of agency?** The person can answer no. A useful experiment preserves that possibility.

## 23. What should not be built yet

Do not build a consciousness score, spiritual progression ladder, guru identity, inferred readiness engine, chart-based diagnosis, infinite reflection feed, universal nervous-system prescription or advanced-technique unlock. Do not release intensive retention, fasting, sleep restriction, altered-state, sexual or kuṇḍalinī protocols as generic self-guided app options.

Do not implement a global taxonomy dashboard, new social network, automated research-to-production ingestion, paid spiritual ranks, family surveillance, unsolicited acquisition messages or persistent locks ahead of verified recovery. Do not rewrite the entire app to express the research.

The next foundation is earned through source fidelity, a useful small experience, reliable boundaries and voluntary evidence. The philosophical questions can remain open while the immediate next action becomes clear.
