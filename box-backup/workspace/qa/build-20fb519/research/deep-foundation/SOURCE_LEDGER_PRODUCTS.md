## Coverage audit and next evidence

21 product entries: all 17 seeds plus Freedom, ScreenZen, Brick and Coherence. Each has problem, documented entry/unknown actual first screen, unobserved time-to-value/decision count, hidden complexity, learned-navigation hypothesis, personalization, deepening, retention hypothesis, utility/extraction judgment and transferable principle. High-confidence company documentation remains low-confidence evidence for causal retention. There are no invented onboarding counts, timed results, live app tests, current prices or independent efficacy endorsements.

Next evidence priorities: native/version-specific walkthroughs for six closest products; fresh readable Clubhouse/TikTok documentation; accessibility/recovery observation; blocker abandonment interviews; separate iOS entitlement research. Cite new observations with consent, date, version, locale and task. Retain old entries with superseded status when product behavior changes rather than silently rewriting the record.
