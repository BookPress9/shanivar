# Manual knowledge governance

29 September 2026. Implements the founder's requested manual boundary. Research content remains PROPOSED. No autonomous ingestion, vector-store pipeline, fine-tuning, scheduled crawler or live recommender is created.

## Three independent decisions

1. **Is the source claim correctly represented?** Textual Provenance/QA records access depth, edition, passage and disagreement.
2. **Does Shanivar accept the proposed stance or product principle?** The founder decides; the Staff Manager records the exact decision.
3. **May a particular practice or implementation be released?** Exact-version content, safety, language, engineering and physical-device gates apply independently. Founder acceptance of a philosophy does not prove clinical safety or a working alarm.

## Entry identity and record format

Use stable IDs: ST for stance, PD for product proposals, EX for experiments; domain ledgers use their own source IDs. A substantive revision increments the entry version in the decision log and identifies what changed. URLs alone are insufficient: record title, author/translator, publication/edition, source class, tradition where relevant, passage, access date and access depth. Mark a blocked full text as blocked, not read. Use a short original claim summary rather than copyrighted book reproduction.

Primary text, traditional commentary, modern scholarly interpretation, practitioner account, empirical finding and Shanivar synthesis are distinct record types. A synthesis cites its dependencies and also records what it adds. A glossary match is never evidence that two traditions mean the same thing. A traditional subtle-body statement is not silently converted into a neuroscience claim.

Source confidence has dimensions: confidence in attribution, confidence in translation/interpretation, empirical design quality, and confidence in transfer to Shanivar. Do not compress these into a decorative “high confidence” badge. Product pages can reliably document that a feature is advertised while providing no evidence of benefit or retention causality.

## Status transition protocol

New finding → **PROPOSED** → source/disagreement review → concrete recommendation → founder accepts, rejects or requests revision. Only a recorded explicit acceptance creates **ACCEPTED**. A suggestion to test does not accept the underlying claim as true. Lack of response is not acceptance.

An acceptance record must include entry ID/version, the exact proposition accepted, founder decision reference (message/date or approved record), date, scope, limitations, implementation authority if any, and review trigger. An agent must never fill the founder signature field from its own inference. Current ST-001–ST-008 all remain PROPOSED.

If evidence changes, create a proposed revision and flag affected product/content owners. Do not rewrite an accepted stance silently. Upon founder decision, mark the earlier entry **SUPERSEDED** with a link and preserve its history. **REJECTED** entries retain the reasoning so the same suggestion is not repeatedly rediscovered.

Existing source/code conflicts can be corrected under already authorized work, but a research agent cannot expand a release scope by calling its recommendation accepted. The latest founder brief itself overrides old authority/hierarchy and research-geography instructions; that administrative supersession is distinct from accepting new scholarly conclusions.

## Publication and retrieval boundary

Research files never directly drive runtime content. A future curated release manifest should reference exact reviewed script versions, source records, review evidence and allowed contexts. Retrieval must be filtered to accepted/released scopes, with provenance and access control; do not retrieve all research as equally authoritative. No runtime ingestion is needed for this foundation report.

A source can be trustworthy and still unsuitable for self-guided practice. A passage may be important for history while remaining research-only. Unsupported claims, conflicting interpretations, advanced practices and private participant material cannot leak into marketing as authoritative copy.

## Ongoing cadence

Owner-triggered weekly triage during active research; monthly source/decision review once pilots begin; immediate review after a material source correction or reported practice harm. These are procedures, not active scheduled jobs. Staff Manager assigns bounded tasks, records actual work and does not manufacture background activity. International audience research repeats with consented evidence; absence of data stays visible.
