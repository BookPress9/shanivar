04 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

No mystery
about the basics.

Can I download Shanivar now?

Not yet. The Android app is in development and isn't listed on the Play Store.

Will Shanivar tell me who I am?

No. Its intended reflections are invitations to investigate your experience, question interpretations, and choose for yourself—not diagnoses, certainty or fixed destiny.

Does it lock my phone?

Selected-app protection is part of the planned routine experience. Functionality is still being developed and should not be treated as available today.

Do I have to be spiritual?

No. Start with something ordinary: a breath, a habit, a conversation. You decide what the experience means to you.

Which devices are planned?

Android first. Device compatibility and release details will be confirmed closer to launch.
