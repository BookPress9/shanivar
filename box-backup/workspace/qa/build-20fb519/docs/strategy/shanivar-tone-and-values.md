# Shanivar tone and values

This is an original app-writing and product-decision guide. It operationalizes founder direction; it does not claim that a model has been fine-tuned. Apply it to UI strings, deterministic prompts, future AI prompts, marketing briefs and QA.

## Inspiration and evidence boundary

Dusan Djukich's *Straight-Line Leadership* presents moving from a current situation to a chosen objective through necessary action. Its introduction connects effective coaching with helping people solve problems themselves. Shanivar borrows clarity about the goal and action, together with growing independence. It adapts these ideas for voluntary everyday practice rather than importing an executive coaching posture.

Source: [officially hosted English book, introduction, printed pages 3–5](https://www.straightlinecoach.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/03/DusanDjukich_Straight-Line_Leadership_E-book.pdf). Selected passages were reviewed; this is not a chapter-by-chapter summary, endorsement or reproduction of the book. The rules below are Shanivar's own design choices.

## Values in action

| Value | Product behavior | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clear intention | One chosen activity per session | Can the participant say what this time is for? |
| Personal agency | Participant confirms and controls the session | Is a helper supporting rather than deciding? |
| Honest feedback | UI follows actual engine state | Is the wording true during failure as well as success? |
| Practical awareness | Brief observation and one optional review | Does this lead back to life? |
| Respectful faith | Optional Lord Shani connection | Can a user decline the framing without losing utility? |
| Independence | Less help when the user wants and can use less | Are we creating a reason to keep consuming? |
| Accessibility | Familiar controls, large text and clear verbs | Can this work without hidden gestures or explanation? |

## Writing rules

- State the goal or state first. Give one primary action.
- Use ordinary verbs: Choose, Start, Allow, End, Change, Skip.
- Address the participant as an adult. Do not infantilize parents or shame younger users.
- A missed session is information. Do not accuse, moralize or threaten loss of spiritual standing.
- Say what is known: “Protection did not start.” Follow with a concrete repair.
- The spiritual voice may mention practice and Shani respectfully. Never impersonate Lord Shani or claim that a planetary interpretation determines a user's mind.
- Use “focused time” publicly; treat hyperfocus as an aspiration, not a guaranteed state. Explain metacognition as noticing and regulating thinking when explanation is useful; no intelligence ranking or IQ claims.
- Do not promise that boredom produces ideas or that one minute repairs dopamine. Invite observation and accept “nothing changed.”
- Keep insight optional after meditation. No “undeniable truth” reveal or requirement to agree.

## Examples for implementation and review

| Your child wants you to stop scrolling | Choose an app you want a break from. |

| You failed your commitment | What would make the next session easier? |
| A little more context | Add details only when you want that feature. |
| Protection active, when status is unknown | Checking protection. |
| Keep discovering your hidden truth | Your session is complete. |

## Reusable future AI instruction

Use only when AI returns to the approved scope:

> Help the participant examine their own experience and choose one next action. Refer only to context they supplied or verified app state. Give one brief observation, one optional question, and room to disagree or stop. Use plain, respectful language. If they selected Shani-inspired wording, acknowledge that spiritual frame without speaking as a deity or treating astrology as fact about their mind. Do not assign intelligence, spiritual rank, diagnosis or destiny. Do not prolong the exchange to create engagement. Never change a lock, entitlement or alarm through reflection.

Initial MVP uses reviewed fixed prompts. Do not add an LLM dependency merely to implement this tone.
