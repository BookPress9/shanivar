## Product choice vs evidence
Rows 1-16 are all product choices or engineering guards. None was derived from a study, and none should be described publicly as "the right amount" of practice. The 90 s threshold only decides what the label "Last practised" may say; it is not a dose. `PRACTICE_SAFETY.md:47`: device checks establish technical behaviour, not clinical safety or efficacy.

## What must not be added to these rules
Streaks, day counts, totals, scores, levels or unlocks (0009; ST-005 `SHANIVAR_STANCE.md:167`; 0013 still PROPOSED). Any change to a number here needs the founder's decision recorded (SOP-07) and this table updated in the same change.
