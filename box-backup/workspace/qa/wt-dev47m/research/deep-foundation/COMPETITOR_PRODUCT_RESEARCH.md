# Product patterns: depth behind a simple next action

Status: **PROPOSED research synthesis**, 2026-09-29. Owner: High-Scale Product Pattern Researcher. No roadmap acceptance, app implementation or live competitor usability test is implied. See [source ledger](SOURCE_LEDGER_PRODUCTS.md) and [Clock / Focus research](CLOCK_FOCUS_RESEARCH.md).

## Method and limits

All 17 founder seed products and four additional relevant products are covered below. Evidence is official product/help material accessed on 2026-09-29. These sources establish what companies document, not independent efficacy or the cause of retention. No installation, signup, paid subscription, timed task, screen recording or first-run walkthrough was performed. Consequently **actual time-to-value and actual decision counts are unknown for every product**. A documented task step is not a measured cognitive decision. “First surface” below means the documented entry surface, not a directly observed fresh-install screen. Where even that is unavailable, it is explicitly unknown.

Confidence labels: **D-high** = directly available company documentation for the described feature; **D-medium** = historical company article or incomplete access; **I** = our interpretation/hypothesis. No source supplies comparative causal retention evidence. All retention explanations below are candidate mechanisms, not findings that the mechanism caused growth. Pricing, market-size claims and testimonial outcomes are deliberately outside this comparison.

## Seed products

### 1. Co–Star

**Problem:** personal interpretation and social connection through astrology. **Documented entry:** birth date/time/place, account verification, then chart/daily information; fresh-install appearance unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown; birth information and account are documented prerequisites, not a counted flow. **Hidden complexity:** chart calculations become short interpretations. **Learned navigation:** chart, updates and settings provide recurring destinations; learnability untested. **Personalization:** birth information and in-app behavior, according to its FAQ. **Deepening:** chart detail, friends and question-based readings. **Retention hypothesis (I):** daily personal relevance plus relationships. **Utility / extraction judgment (I):** reflection can invite inquiry; confident identity labels can encourage dependency. **Transfer:** precise recognizable moments, with an explicit invitation to disagree; never infer destiny or emotional truth from astrology. [Co–Star FAQ](https://www.costarastrology.com/faq). D-high for documented features, no validation of astrology.

### 2. Clubhouse

**Problem:** being with friends through voice. **Documented entry:** the 2023 redesign centers friend-oriented voice Chats; current first-run/home ordering unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown, likely contingent on relevant people, requiring testing. **Hidden complexity:** asynchronous voice can reduce coordination requirements. **Learned navigation:** documented push-to-talk, skip and swipe actions; no observed learning study. **Personalization:** friend networks. **Deepening:** conversations/private exchanges. **Retention hypothesis:** reciprocal relationships. **Utility / extraction:** meaningful conversation versus social obligation and repeated checking. **Transfer:** recognizable context can reduce explanation; do not add a social feed merely to create returns. Historical company redesign, search-accessible with direct fetch failing: [The new Clubhouse](https://blog.clubhouse.com/the-new-clubhouse/). D-medium; not evidence of current usage or business success.

### 3. Duolingo

**Problem:** continuing language study when choosing the next lesson is effortful. **Documented entry:** a sequenced learning path; current fresh-install setup unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** lesson ordering and practice are packaged in a path. **Learned navigation:** a recurring next lesson; empirical learnability not measured here. **Personalization:** placement/progression adapt the starting point and subsequent work as described in the redesign. **Deepening:** advancing curriculum. **Retention hypothesis:** visible progress and streak continuity. **Utility / extraction:** sequencing may help learning; streak anxiety may replace intrinsic purpose. **Transfer:** offer the next suitable practice, preserve skip/change, never equate spiritual progress with consecutive days. [Path rationale](https://blog.duolingo.com/new-duolingo-home-screen-design/), [streak definition](https://www.duolingo.com/help/what-is-a-streak). D-high for described design; historical redesign, no current onboarding audit.

### 4. Headspace

**Problem:** entering guided meditation and related everyday wellbeing activities. **Documented entry:** meditation/sleep/stress resources in the product offering; exact first screen unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** guided sessions package instructions that would otherwise require study. **Learned navigation:** repeatable content categories/player is an inference, not observed navigation. **Personalization:** the public app page presents need-oriented options; exact recommendation algorithm unknown. **Deepening:** broader sessions and ongoing practice. **Retention hypothesis:** familiar guidance and recurring needs. **Utility / extraction:** usable guidance versus endless wellbeing content selection. **Transfer:** beginner instructions should teach the action within the practice; source depth belongs behind an optional door. [Headspace app](https://www.headspace.com/app). D-high for offering, I for transfer. Do not import promotional neuroscience claims as clinical evidence.

### 5. Calm

**Problem:** finding accessible relaxation or sleep content. **Documented entry:** company beginner guidance points to daily content in the first Home carousel and sleep-specific content. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** a daily selection reduces library choice. **Learned navigation:** familiar Home/Sleep destinations, untested here. **Personalization:** user-selected needs/content, algorithm unknown. **Deepening:** programs and larger library. **Retention hypothesis:** a bedtime ritual and fresh daily guidance. **Utility / extraction:** a bounded sleep aid can serve leaving the screen; browsing can delay sleep. **Transfer:** a bedtime practice must end quietly without inviting another session. [Beginner guide](https://support.calm.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002474567-Getting-Started-With-Calm-A-Beginner-s-Guide-to-Meditation-Sleep). D-high for documented path. Calm, Calm Health and Calm Sleep are distinct offerings; do not merge them into one tested UX.

### 6. Waking Up

**Problem:** linking contemplative practice with understanding. **Documented entry:** an introductory course is recommended, located under Practice in Explore; actual landing screen unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** a guided foundation sequences a larger body of teaching. **Learned navigation:** course continuation plus later exploration. **Personalization:** choice of teachers/topics and saved playlists; algorithm unknown. **Deepening:** theory and practice across teachers/traditions. **Retention hypothesis:** continuity of inquiry rather than novelty alone. **Utility / extraction:** depth is useful when requested; theory can replace doing. **Transfer:** a short experience, then optional explanation and provenance, with doctrinal differences visible. [Introductory course](https://help.wakingup.com/article/77-whats-the-introductory-course), [product](https://wakingup.com/). D-high for offering.

### 7. Insight Timer

**Problem:** access to diverse guided practice and structured courses. **Documented entry:** Saved → Courses, with an alternative Home/Saved route depending on layout; first run unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** In Progress / Completed / Saved organize course state. **Learned navigation:** returning to saved material reduces rediscovery; learnability unmeasured. **Personalization:** explicit saves and teacher/content choice. **Deepening:** courses and teacher libraries. **Retention hypothesis:** continuity and preferred teachers. **Utility / extraction:** breadth supports different needs; abundant choice can become searching instead of practice. **Transfer:** remember a chosen practice locally and offer continuation before a catalogue. [Course access help](https://help.insighttimer.com/support/solutions/articles/67000719432-how-can-i-access-my-courses-). D-high for this task, not full-product coverage.

### 8. Finch

**Problem:** making small self-care actions approachable. **Documented entry:** Home goals, with goal creation and completion controls; first run unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** optional timing/customization sits under more options. **Learned navigation:** goal → checkmark is a recurring action. **Personalization:** user goals and recurrence. **Deepening:** self-care areas and tracking. **Retention hypothesis:** companion attachment and rewards alongside useful routines. **Utility / extraction:** encouragement can help initiation; caring for a virtual dependent can create guilt or compulsive checking. **Transfer:** retain warmth and small achievable actions; no pet obligation, spiritual points or penalty for absence. [Goals help](https://help.finchcare.com/hc/en-us/articles/37779940291213-Creating-and-Completing-Goals). D-high for task; interpretation is I.

### 9. Forest

**Problem:** committing attention for a chosen period. **Documented entry:** choose duration/tree and start growing; first-run prerequisites unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown; two named inputs are not a measured two-decision onboarding. **Hidden complexity:** visible tree growth represents elapsed focus. **Learned navigation:** repeated planting ritual. **Personalization:** duration/species; longer-term statistics. **Deepening:** accumulated forest, schedules and group focus in current marketing. **Retention hypothesis:** visible accumulated effort and loss avoidance. **Utility / extraction:** concrete progress helps some; withering/shared losses can moralize interruption. **Transfer:** show the commitment's status plainly; never represent an interrupted practice as spiritual damage. [Forest official product page](https://www.forestapp.cc/). D-high for claims, no platform feature parity or effectiveness verification.

### 10. Opal

**Problem:** intentional boundaries around distracting apps. **Documented entry:** current iOS help uses My Apps → + → Schedule; first run unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown, permissions and rule setup unmeasured. **Hidden complexity:** schedules collect times/days/apps behind a rule. **Learned navigation:** return to the same rule/app list. **Personalization:** selected apps, times and restriction preferences. **Deepening:** sessions, schedules, limits and optional stronger restrictions. **Retention hypothesis:** automation plus visible protection. **Utility / extraction:** deliberate commitment helps; emergency quotas and increasingly strict options can conflict with autonomy. **Transfer:** explicit rule preview, always-available essentials and clear recovery; reject quota-limited emergency safety. [Schedules](https://help.opalapp.com/article/how-to-use-schedules), [Emergency Pass](https://help.opalapp.com/article/how-to-use-emergency-pass). D-high, specifically documented iOS behavior; older Deep Focus terminology must not be assumed to describe the current UI.

### 11. one sec

**Problem:** automatic app opening. **Documented entry:** an intervention at opening a selected app; first-time setup unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** a pause is inserted into an existing habit rather than requiring a separate meditation visit. **Learned navigation:** the same interruption context repeats. **Personalization:** user-selected apps and intervention configuration; full setup untested. **Deepening:** repeated recognition of opening impulses. **Retention hypothesis:** useful friction operating in the background. **Utility / extraction:** restores a decision point; habituation or irritation may erase benefit. **Transfer:** interrupt the habitual transition gently, with external grounding available instead of mandatory breathwork. [one sec](https://one-sec.app/). D-high for advertised intervention; outcome claims need separate study assessment.

### 12. Oura

**Problem:** interpreting longitudinal wearable data. **Documented entry:** Today / Vitals / My Health organize immediate and longer-term information in the company redesign. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown; meaningful personal baselines may require repeated wear, not just opening the app. **Hidden complexity:** layered summaries over measurements. **Learned navigation:** time horizon becomes an organizing principle. **Personalization:** measured data and individual history. **Deepening:** trends. **Retention hypothesis:** repeated personally relevant feedback. **Utility / extraction:** useful trend interpretation versus checking scores for reassurance. **Transfer:** separate “this moment” from optional history; Shanivar cannot infer readiness, hormones or circadian phase from clock time. [Oura design](https://ouraring.com/blog/new-oura-app-experience/). D-high for described organization, not clinical validity of every metric.

### 13. Spotify

**Problem:** choosing something relevant to hear from a huge library. **Documented entry:** personalized Home; the 2026 Taste Profile announcement describes controls for shaping recommendations. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** ranking converts a catalogue into a short suggestion set. **Learned navigation:** repeatable play/save actions; first-run learning untested. **Personalization:** listening signals with explicit correction controls; availability varies by rollout. **Deepening:** personal library and evolving discovery. **Retention hypothesis:** reliable relevance and accumulated preferences. **Utility / extraction:** discovery supports intent; continuous recommendation can prolong use beyond it. **Transfer:** explain and let the user correct a recommendation; never optimize session count as a proxy for presence. [Spotify announcement](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-09-23/taste-profile-shape-home-feed/). D-high for announcement, not universal availability.

### 14. TikTok

**Problem:** discovering relevant short videos. **Documented entry:** For You recommendations; exact fresh-install entry unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** ranked selection lowers search effort. **Learned navigation:** repeated content consumption is a likely learned pattern, but no gestures were observed in this audit. **Personalization:** interactions influence recommendations according to help material. **Deepening:** increasingly tailored discovery rather than necessarily deeper understanding. **Retention hypothesis:** low-effort novelty. **Utility / extraction:** discovery can help; frictionless continuation is incompatible with Shanivar's chosen endpoint. **Transfer:** make the immediate next action obvious, then provide a real stopping point. [Recommendation help](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/exploring-videos/how-tiktok-recommends-content). D-medium: search extract available, direct open returned no readable text. No claims about ranking weights or causal addiction.

### 15. Instagram

**Problem:** connection, expression and discovery. **Documented entry:** ranked Feed and other distinct surfaces; fresh-install ordering unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** recommendations sort a large candidate set. **Learned navigation:** recurring surfaces; actual learnability untested. **Personalization:** signals and feedback as explained by Meta. **Deepening:** relationship graph and interests, not a contemplative curriculum. **Retention hypothesis:** social relevance and novelty. **Utility / extraction:** interpersonal value competes with continued feed consumption. **Transfer:** familiar interactions can help, but Shanivar should finish an experiment rather than load the next card forever. [Meta ranking explanation](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/06/how-ai-ranks-content-on-facebook-and-instagram/). D-high for company explanation, not endorsement of optimization objectives.

### 16. Notion

**Problem:** creating and organizing personal/team information. **Documented entry:** create a page and begin writing; first-run workspace setup unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** a simple page can later contain richer blocks. **Learned navigation:** reusable page/block operations. **Personalization:** user-built structure. **Deepening:** adding richer organization when needed. **Retention hypothesis:** useful accumulated work and collaboration. **Utility / extraction:** extensibility can serve a task; elaborate setup can displace the task. **Transfer:** one usable practice before building a personal taxonomy; advanced structure must remain optional. [Create a page](https://www.notion.com/help/create-your-first-page). D-high for documented task only.

### 17. ChatGPT

**Problem:** help expressed as an ordinary-language request. **Documented entry:** conversational assistance in the overview; first-run/account prompts unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** diverse capabilities behind a request. **Learned navigation:** conversational turns; untested as a novice journey. **Personalization:** memory/preferences with controls, according to current official help. **Deepening:** continuing context and more complex tasks. **Retention hypothesis:** reusable assistance and remembered context. **Utility / extraction:** useful dialogue can also encourage overreliance on confident output. **Transfer:** use understandable language and reversible preferences; a contemplative guide must distinguish source, interpretation and uncertainty, and sometimes say nothing more. [Overview](https://chatgpt.com/overview/), [Memory](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-in-chatgpt). D-high for documented offerings. Repository file search found no ChatGPT UX implementation/reference capable of verifying current behavior; official sources were the fallback. This does not authorize an AI coach in V1.

## Additional relevant products

### 18. Freedom

**Problem:** distraction across devices/sites/apps. **Entry:** sessions/blocklists/settings described by help; first run unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** reusable blocking configuration. **Learned navigation:** recurring session setup. **Personalization:** lists/schedules. **Deepening:** optional Locked Mode. **Retention hypothesis:** predictable automated boundaries. **Utility / extraction:** useful commitment; rigidity can obstruct necessary access. **Transfer:** try ordinary schedules before stronger friction, expose platform differences, and keep Shanivar recovery local. [Locked Mode](https://support.freedom.to/en/articles/1802927-locked-mode). D-high; not proof of equally strong enforcement on each OS.

### 19. ScreenZen

**Problem:** repeated impulsive app access. **Entry:** company describes choosing apps, limits, scheduled blocks and mindful use; actual first screen unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** repeated limits become a background rule. **Learned navigation:** pause/access cycle, inference. **Personalization:** app choices/schedules/limits. **Deepening:** tracking patterns. **Retention hypothesis:** customizable friction fitting ordinary life. **Utility / extraction:** useful boundaries can become tuning work. **Transfer:** offer one comprehensible schedule before advanced knobs. [ScreenZen](https://screenzen.co/). D-high for marketing. Its Android store disclosure mentions accessibility use for website blocking; this mechanism is excluded by Shanivar's stance, not a feature gap to copy.

### 20. Brick

**Problem:** putting physical distance between impulse and access. **Entry:** app plus physical object, as marketed; fresh setup unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown. **Hidden complexity:** a tangible action represents a digital boundary. **Learned navigation:** repeat physical ritual, untested here. **Personalization:** selected restrictions; full configuration unknown. **Deepening:** a routine of where the object lives. **Retention hypothesis:** environmental friction. **Utility / extraction:** environment may help intention; loss of the object must not trap essential access. **Transfer:** test placing the phone away, with no hardware purchase prerequisite. [Emergency access documentation](https://getbrick.com/pages/emergency-unbricks) describes a finite allowance and reset process; Shanivar must not copy safety scarcity. D-high for emergency documentation, lower for whole-product journey.

### 21. Coherence

**Problem:** advertised state-oriented breathing. **Entry:** minimal breathing guidance shown on its site; first-run app unknown. **Time-to-value / decisions:** unknown; “seconds” is marketing, not our measurement. **Hidden complexity:** sound/motion communicate timing. **Learned navigation:** a recurring practice player, inference. **Personalization:** outcome-oriented technique choice; algorithm unknown. **Deepening:** library and synchronized sessions as advertised. **Retention hypothesis:** convenient repeat ritual. **Utility / extraction:** eyes-free guidance can support practice; physiological certainty can overpromise. **Transfer:** clear player, original instructions and optional provenance; do not inherit paced breathing, synchronization or performance percentages as validated VBT benefits. [Coherence](https://www.coherencebreath.com/). D-high for advertised design, no app efficacy verification.

## Proposed decisions and falsifiable tests

These are design hypotheses, not accepted changes. Numerical thresholds below are initial decision rules for formative studies, not established benchmarks or statistically powered efficacy claims.

| ID / source / confidence | Principle | Shanivar implication | Experiment | Success / failure | Decision gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P01 Duolingo, Waking Up; D-high/I | Sequence the next suitable action | One recommended practice with Change and Skip | 12 diverse adults: catalogue-first versus recommendation-first, counterbalanced | ≥10/12 start a suitable practice unaided; fail if suitability errors increase or choices feel imposed | Founder reviews before changing navigation |
| P02 Finch, Notion; D-high/I | Put optional complexity after a useful action | Immediate grounding; no prerequisite taxonomy | Observe first-use pause then optional preference entry | Median entry-to-instruction under 30 seconds, no inaccessible path; fail if safety context is missed | Keep safety checks; simplify other fields |
| P03 Spotify, ChatGPT; D-high/I | Correctable personalization | “Suggested because…” and Change; no hidden diagnosis | Give an intentionally unsuitable suggestion and ask user to correct it | ≥10/12 correct unaided; fail if users infer Shanivar knows internal state | Reject opaque personalization |
| P04 Oura, Calm; D-high/I | Separate moment from history | Clock context supports, never measures, state | Shift workers/day workers explain sleep recommendation | Everyone understands it is schedule-based; any physiological misunderstanding triggers copy revision | No circadian claims without separate evidence |
| P05 one sec, Opal; D-high/I | Friction belongs at a chosen boundary | Protected-app interruption with one optional grounding action | 14-day voluntary pilot with stable baseline and opt-out | More chosen tasks reported complete without increased regretted blocks; uninstall/disable reasons analyzed, not punished | Test ordinary windows before persistent mode |
| P06 TikTok/Instagram; D-medium/high + I | Clarity does not require endless continuation | Practice ends with leave/return to chosen activity | Compare bounded end to optional further-reading link | More users leave for intended activity; fail if practice turns into browsing | No infinite practice feed |
| P07 Forest/Finch; D-high/I | Make effort legible without moral scoring | Optional neutral session history | Interview people who missed a week | No guilt/status ranking induced; fail on “I lost progress” interpretations | No streak-loss mechanics |
| P08 Freedom/Brick/Opal; D-high/I | Commitment and recoverability must coexist | Local, accessible recovery before strong modes | Simulated urgent access and offline recovery | All recover unaided; any trap is a stop, irrespective of retention | Strong mode remains deferred until gates pass |

## Remaining fieldwork

Run consented, version-labelled native walkthroughs of the most relevant six products: one sec, Opal, Freedom, Headspace, Waking Up and Coherence. Record OS, locale, app version, account state, permissions, purchase walls, exact entry task, stopwatch definition, distinct decisions, errors and first independent value. Do not purchase or create accounts without the needed authorization. Include failed and abandoned paths. Recheck Clubhouse and TikTok source access, then test whether documented navigation matches current apps. Interview users who disabled blockers as well as those who kept them; a company's retention narrative cannot answer that question.
