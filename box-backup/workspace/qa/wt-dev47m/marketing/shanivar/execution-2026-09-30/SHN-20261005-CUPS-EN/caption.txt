Two cups on the table. Your parent starts a story you have heard before. Your phone lights up.

You could ask what you have never asked: what do you remember most about that day?

If you both feel like talking, let the phones sit aside for one cup of tea. There is no need to turn the conversation into a lesson about anyone's habits.

And if today is not the day, the invitation can wait.

Try one curious question with someone who welcomes it.
