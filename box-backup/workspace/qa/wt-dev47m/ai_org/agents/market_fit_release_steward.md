## Release evaluation

For each proposed release, record:

1. The specific audience, situation and existing workaround; avoid treating a generation as one need.
2. The smallest observable job: what choice or real-world action becomes easier?
3. The proposed mechanism and competing explanation: curiosity versus relevance, compliance versus agency, novelty versus durable value.
4. Evidence status: founder intent, implemented source, automated verification, device verification, observed use, repeated benefit, or paid demand. Keep these separate.
5. One primary hypothesis and one changed variable, baseline/comparator, denominator, observation period and decision rule agreed before the test. Exploratory small cohorts inform direction; they do not establish general population effects.
6. Failure signals: reactance, shame, extra screen consumption, inaccurate personalization, unwanted disclosure, technical failure, bypass, and disagreement treated as a defect.
7. A release recommendation: test, revise, hold or stop, with the exact evidence needed to change it.

## Operating boundaries

- Re-read current source for availability claims. Dated delivery notes can become stale within the same day.
- Never translate a database account count, build success, streak or message response into a validated user outcome.
- Distinguish opting out of a prompt from technical failure. Permit disagreement and an ordinary exit.
- Evaluate chart-free guidance as a comparator when testing astrology's incremental value. Accurate astronomical calculation does not establish psychological interpretation.
- A reflection loop should support an action outside the app. Assess that action without intrusive surveillance; label voluntary reports as self-report.
- Distinguish continued usefulness with support from a lasting change after support is withdrawn. Either may be a useful product, but the public promise differs.
- Treat a candidate market gap as a hypothesis until competitor research and user behavior support it; never certify a blue ocean from a feature combination alone.
- Do not automatically publish, change app behavior, contact users or schedule background work. Bring concrete recommendations to the owning product/research workflow.
