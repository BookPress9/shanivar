# Source library

The founder's reading list, kept as **lenses**, not authorities. Founder listed these titles (2026-10-11). Grades below are **my assessment** (Grok Bot), from general knowledge of each work and the wider research literature; the books were **not** read in full for this document, so treat each grade as a starting point for review, not a verdict. Where I could not assess, it says so.

## Rules for every source
1. **Summarise and cite. Never copy passages** (short attributed phrases at most, with page/section when known). Do not paste extracts from the supplied Kundalini Tantra PDF.
2. **Never put a practice from a source into the app without `research/deep-foundation/PRACTICE_SAFETY.md` review** and SOP-04. Tradition-specific language stays tradition-specific (`SHANIVAR_STANCE.md` ST-001, ST-004).
3. **No medical, therapeutic or "nervous-system" claims** in product or marketing (`PRACTICE_SAFETY.md:49-51`).
4. A source summary is not full-text verification (`AGENTS.md`, "Truth and safety"). Record how deeply each was read in `research/deep-foundation/SOURCE_LEDGER.md` when used.
5. Founder conviction is not product evidence (`docs/strategy/shanivar-founder-direction.md:71-80`).

**Evidence grade scale** (health or science claims only): **strong**: replicated human evidence; **mixed**: some good evidence plus claims that go beyond it; **weak**: little or low-quality human evidence; **untested / not supported**: untestable, or contradicted by mainstream science; **n/a**: no empirical health claim.

## The library
### Becoming Supernatural: Dr Joe Dispenza
- **Why here:** founder's reading on meditation, intention and change.
- **Could inform (a lens):** how repeated attention and rehearsal might relate to changing habits; a vocabulary for intention before practice.
- **Evidence grade: weak to mixed.** General meditation has modest evidence for stress and attention outcomes; the book's mechanistic claims (physiological or "energy" transformation, mystical experience as measurable change) rest mostly on the author's own workshop data and are not independently replicated.
- **Rules:** use only as a framing of intention; no healing, transformation or brain-change claims; no practice imported unreviewed.

### Sacred Geometry: Robert Lawlor
- **Why here:** founder's interest in form, proportion and symbol.
- **Could inform:** visual language and the Saturn motif's geometry; a symbolic lens on structure.
- **Evidence grade: n/a.** Geometry is verifiable mathematics; cosmological and symbolic readings are interpretive and untestable, not empirical claims.
- **Rules:** keep symbolism as design inspiration, never as an effect claim; the Saturn mark never paces breath (`instagram_keeper.md:41`).

### The Secret Intelligence of Water: Veda Austin
- **Why here:** founder's interest in water and attention.
- **Could inform:** attention to everyday substances; nothing about mechanism.
- **Evidence grade: untested / not supported.** Claims that water records or responds to words or intention ("water memory") are not supported by mainstream science.
- **Rules:** do not repeat water-memory claims anywhere; at most a reflective theme about noticing.

### Lifespan: Dr David Sinclair
- **Why here:** founder's interest in longevity and lifestyle.
- **Could inform:** why sleep, movement and moderation matter for the routine; a prompt to read primary studies.
- **Evidence grade: mixed.** Lifestyle themes (movement, not overeating, sleep) have solid support; the central "information theory of ageing" is debated, and supplement/drug claims are not established in humans.
- **Rules:** no longevity, anti-ageing or supplement advice in the app.

### Healing Through Nature (university studies)
- **Why here:** from the founder's university studies.
- **Evidence grade: UNRESOLVED.** `UNRESOLVED:` exact author, edition and which claims it makes. Time in nature has some supportive wellbeing evidence in general; "healing" claims need case-by-case checks.
- **Rules:** do not cite until identified.

### Alkaline Sports Nutrition (university studies)
- **Why here:** from the founder's university studies.
- **Evidence grade: weak for acid-alkaline claims.** Diet does not meaningfully change blood pH, which the body regulates tightly; general sports-nutrition content must be checked against current guidance. `UNRESOLVED:` exact author and edition.
- **Rules:** no diet, nutrition or "alkaline" claims; no dietary prescriptions (`PRACTICE_SAFETY.md` fasting row).

### My 20 Golden Rules: Jason Shon Bennett
- **Why here:** founder's list; a personal rules-for-living style book.
- **Could inform:** tone for plain rules and self-set standards (compare `shanivar-tone-and-values.md`).
- **Evidence grade: not assessed.** I could not verify its content; treat claims as untested until read.
- **Rules:** quote nothing; adopt no rule as Shanivar copy without founder review.

### The Health Seekers' YearBook: Victoria Bidwell
- **Why here:** founder's natural-health reference.
- **Evidence grade: mixed to weak (unverified).** From general knowledge, natural-health compendia mix sound basics (whole foods, movement) with detox or cleansing claims that lack good evidence. I have not verified this edition.
- **Rules:** no health, detox or cure claims; verify any item against primary sources first.

### Protocols (Huberman Lab)
- **Why here:** founder's practical reference for routine design (light, sleep, exercise, focus).
- **Could inform:** questions for Routine (consistent wake time, daylight) to test, not assert.
- **Evidence grade: mixed.** Many protocols rest on real research, but several extrapolate from animal or small studies, and supplement advice is often overstated. `UNRESOLVED:` exact title and edition.
- **Rules:** the app never claims circadian, cortisol or "optimised" effects (`routine-onboarding-marketing-plan.md:171`); each protocol idea needs its own source check.

### Kundalini Tantra: Swami Satyananda Saraswati (Bihar School of Yoga)
- **Why here:** founder supplied the PDF; a classical practice manual in the Bihar School lineage, relevant to the Sadhana direction (decision 0013 mentions intense practices around manovigyana and tantra).
- **Could inform:** the tradition's own vocabulary, structure and warnings; a map of what Shanivar deliberately does *not* ship.
- **Evidence grade: untested.** Subtle-body and kundalini explanations are traditional frameworks, not anatomy or physiology (`AGENTS.md`; ST-004). The source itself carries risk warnings and expects qualified guidance; intensive breath and concentration practices are research-only here (`PRACTICE_SAFETY.md` table).
- **Rules:** do not copy text; do not paraphrase practice instructions into the app; "unlock kundalini" is a rejected claim (`PRACTICE_SAFETY.md:51`); any use needs source review plus qualified practice review (SOP-04).

## One-source model
**Framing, not a finding.** The founder's idea: everything connects to one source, and there are different ways to reach it (`docs/strategy/shanivar-founder-direction.md:13`: people participate in one consciousness; conditioning narrows what they take reality to be). Shanivar states this as the founder's conviction, not a clinical or scientific result, and a shared-consciousness framing is not cross-tradition consensus (`shanivar-founder-direction.md:5`; ST-001).

**How it could structure the library:** one source at the centre; each book is filed under the *route* it speaks to, so the founder's list can be read as ways in rather than competing claims.

|---|---|---|
| Attention | Dispenza; Kundalini Tantra (concentration traditions, research-only) | Attention practices: `external_grounding`, `emotion_space`, `sound_listen` (`breathing-v1.json`) |
