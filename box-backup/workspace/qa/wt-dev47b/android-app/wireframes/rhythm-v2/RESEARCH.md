# Research and adaptation register

This register explains what informed the V2 wireframe and what the design is allowed to infer. It is not a claim that every linked protocol is effective, safe for every person or ready for release. Research conclusions remain PROPOSED under [SHANIVAR_STANCE.md](../../../SHANIVAR_STANCE.md).

## Product foundation

The experience follows the [Deep Foundation V1](../../../research/deep-foundation/SHANIVAR_DEEP_FOUNDATION_REPORT_V1.md): a concrete interruption, an optional experience and a return to ordinary life. The [Sadhak journey](../../../research/deep-foundation/SADHAK_JOURNEY.md) rejects spiritual ranking. The [practice library](../../../research/deep-foundation/PRACTICE_LIBRARY.md) supplies bounded original cues. The [safety policy](../../../research/deep-foundation/PRACTICE_SAFETY.md) keeps intensive breath manipulation, retention, fasting, sleep restriction, altered-state, sexual, kundalini and trauma-oriented practices outside generic challenges.

## Dinacharya and sleep/wake rhythm

The Indian Ministry of AYUSH describes *dinacharya* as a daily routine and includes feasible routine components in its health-and-wellness guidance. That establishes a traditional/public-health context, not a personalized recommendation engine or proof that one schedule fits everyone. The wireframe borrows the concept of daily anchors while asking the person for their wake and intended-sleep times.

NHLBI guidance recommends making enough time for sleep and keeping a consistent schedule where possible. It also acknowledges shift-work adaptation. Shanivar therefore avoids rewarding sleep restriction and gives shift work its own route.

- [AYUSH Health and Wellness Centre operational guidelines](https://namayush.gov.in/sites/default/files/doc/AYUSH_HWCs_Operational_Guideline_English.pdf)
- [NHLBI healthy sleep habits](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep-deprivation/healthy-sleep-habits)

**Product boundary:** no dosha diagnosis, dawn requirement, cortisol prediction or inferred circadian phase. A suggestion follows the participant's stated schedule and can be removed.

## Huberman protocol material

The requested [mind map](https://mikgenalg.medium.com/a-mind-map-of-dr-huberman-protocols-a24b1e67d251) is an independent 2023 synthesis. Its author explicitly describes it as a curated selection and personal taxonomy covering circadian rhythm, nutrition, supplements, exercise and health. It is a discovery index rather than a primary clinical guideline. Individual claims must be checked against their actual source and current evidence before release.

The official Huberman Lab material is educational media. The wireframe uses its strong pattern of organizing broad material into actionable topics. It includes only restrained cards for daylight/wake regularity, quiet evening, movement/connection and source study. It excludes supplement doses, fasting, cold/heat exposure and forced breathing from progression.

- [Huberman Lab sleep toolkit](https://www.hubermanlab.com/newsletter/toolkit-for-sleep)
- [Mental Health Toolkit episode](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/mental-health-toolkit-tools-to-bolster-your-mood-mental-health)
- [Focus Toolkit episode](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/focus-toolkit-tools-to-improve-your-focus-and-concentration)

**Product boundary:** the product does not use the phrase “science-backed” as a blanket endorsement. Each exact practice needs claim, source, population, dose, contraindication and adaptation review. No affiliation with Huberman Lab is implied.

### Proposed curriculum routing

| Sadhana chapter | Relevant protocol themes | Product treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrive | User-chosen wake rhythm, daylight when practical, ordinary external or contact anchor | Low-intensity suggestions; skip and adapt freely |
| Return | Focus environment, chosen app boundaries, movement break, one natural breath if welcome | Measure whether the person returns to their activity, not a bodily state |
| Understand | Sleep, light, movement and social-connection context; Conti-inspired agency questions | Short source-labelled explanations; no diagnosis or causal certainty |
| Make it yours | Independent practice, quiet rest, personally selected routine cues | Fewer reminders and self-report; no proof requirement |
| Deepen | VBT and tradition study, physiology claims and the wider protocol catalogue | Study and review first; high-risk or medically specific practices remain unavailable |

Nutrition, supplements, fasting, heat/cold exposure, fixed exercise doses and intensive breath protocols may appear in a source catalogue only after qualified review. They do not become challenges because a chapter was completed.

## JKP tool review

The supplied `JKP_Mental_Health_Tools_Hinglish.pdf` is a 17-page Hinglish guide from Jeevan Ki Paathshala. It describes itself as an adaptation of Dr. Paul Conti's teachings and organizes 13 tools around self structure/function, humility and agency, three drives, motivation, self-awareness, salience and intrusive thoughts, childhood narratives, envy, narcissism, demoralization, social media, addiction/therapy and rational aspiration. It ends with a seven-day 10–15 minute plan and a one-page recall sheet.

Useful design patterns retained:

- `concept -> everyday example -> apply`;
- one concrete question or action rather than a long lecture;
- small, repeatable actions and rational aspiration;
- attention to agency, gratitude, values and generative activity;
- explicit acknowledgement that professional help has a place.

Claims or interactions not adopted:

- the statement that most decisions originate in an unconscious “90%”;
- treating intrusive thoughts as evidence of trauma or a required change;
- scoring three drives as a validated mental-health measure;
- diagnosing narcissism, addiction, learned helplessness or treatment need;
- implying medication is inappropriate for a motivation problem;
- asking an unattended user to excavate childhood narratives;
- presenting illustrative transformations as expected outcomes.

The wireframe's `One action that matters` card is original Shanivar wording inspired by the guide's agency, inventory, values and rational-aspiration exercises. It is not a reproduction of the PDF, a Dr. Conti protocol or treatment.

For comparison, [Huberman Lab's Conti series](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/guest-series-dr-paul-conti-tools-and-protocols-for-mental-health) is closer to the named speaker than the supplied adaptation, but it remains educational media rather than validation of a Shanivar assessment.

## Gamification toward independence

The design uses game elements as feedback about practice, not a reason to maximize time in the app.

Sailer and colleagues found that different game-design elements can affect different motivational outcomes; “gamification” is not one uniformly effective treatment. The Self-Determination Theory frame makes autonomy, competence and relatedness useful design questions. Duolingo's own published experiments show that lowering the initial threshold and separating a streak from a larger daily goal can affect retention. These are company-reported learning-product results, not evidence for spiritual practice or wellbeing. Trailhead demonstrates bite-sized instruction followed by hands-on challenges, but its points/ranks should not be copied into claims of inner development.

- [Sailer et al., How gamification motivates](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S074756321630855X)
- [Sailer & Homner, gamification of learning meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-019-09498-w)
- [Duolingo on separating streak and daily goal](https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak/)
- [Duolingo on streak flexibility](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/)
- [Trailhead hands-on challenges](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/trailhead-for-companies/get-started-using-trailhead-in-your-company)

**Adaptation:** chapters make the learning path legible; small challenges demonstrate use; the audit makes evidence visible; recovery and chapter choice protect autonomy; independent mode makes decreasing app reliance an explicit success. No public comparison, loss aversion or artificial scarcity is used.

## Proposed research questions

1. Can a new user explain the distinct jobs of Routine, Dashboard and Sadhana after one minute?
2. Does asking for wake/sleep preference before capability setup feel useful to shift workers as well as daytime workers?
3. Can users distinguish `saved plan`, `device access granted` and `protection active`?
4. Do chapters help people select and repeat a comfortable practice without being read as spiritual ranks?
5. Does independent mode increase voluntary off-app practice and reduce reminder dependence?
6. Does the audit feel informative without turning elapsed time into a score of self-worth?
7. Are source/adaptation disclosures understandable before and after a practice?
8. Do discomfort and early-stop paths prevent inappropriate challenge escalation?

Success requires comprehension, agency, reliable exit and useful off-app action. More sessions, longer sessions or higher retention cannot establish those outcomes alone.
