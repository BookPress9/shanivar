# V1 reference patterns: alarms and mindful locks

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Focused official-source review, not hands-on testing or a comparative effectiveness study.

[Alarmy's official site](https://alar.my/en) describes task-based dismissal, step and other missions, sound options and wake-up checks. Its [mission guide](https://alar.my/en/blog/how-to-choose-alarmy-mission) compares dismissal activities. Adapt clear setup and a chosen action using Shanivar's existing movement/ringer implementation. Defer the full mission catalogue, sleep tracking and wake-up rechecks. No competitor code, assets or proprietary screen copy were obtained.

[one sec's Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en-US&id=wtf.riedel.onesec) displayed 1M+ downloads, 4.7 stars and 45.9K headline reviews at inspection. This supports selection as an established mindful reference, not a verified number-one ranking or Indian parent fit. Its Android listing declares accessibility-service detection; Shanivar retains its own existing enforcement approach.

The [one sec FAQ](https://one-sec.app/faq/) describes brief interruptions before distracting apps and configurable timing. Its [customization guide](https://tutorials.one-sec.app/en/articles/3310978) documents Android phrase and duration controls. Do not assume every iOS or website feature exists on Android.

## Original adaptations

| Reference pattern | Shanivar v1 | Later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mission-based alarm dismissal | Time/repeat days, chosen movement or non-walking alternative, test sound and real next occurrence | Camera/QR, mission chains, multiple alarms |
| Brief mindful interruption | User's intention on block surface, optional local pause, device-home exit | All-day re-interventions outside protected sessions |
| Tailored protection | Editable activity presets and selected app set on one engine | Location rules and automatic focus schedules |
| Familiar setup | Two independent home actions; relevant permissions only | Configuration dashboards |

The pause never grants access during active protection. Pre-account preview explicitly blocks nothing. Whole-app protection is not feed-only control. Steps are counted movement, not proof of arrival or waking. Competitor outcome percentages are not Shanivar claims. Original screens and copy plus existing contracts are the implementation basis; public-source descriptions do not establish device reliability.
