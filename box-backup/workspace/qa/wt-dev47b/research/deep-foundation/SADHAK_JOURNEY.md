# A journey of inquiry, not a ladder of people

29 September 2026. **PROPOSED product synthesis** from the founder brief, [audience research](INTERNATIONAL_AUDIENCE_RESEARCH.md), [tradition distinctions](TRADITION_SEPARATION_MATRIX.md) and [UX evidence](UX_BEHAVIOR_PATTERNS.md). These are modes a person may choose between, not measured levels of consciousness. Readiness is contextual, not a score.

## Why progress must not be a compulsory sequence

A long-term practitioner may need a ten-second ordinary anchor while exhausted. A first-time visitor may want to read an edition note before trying anything. A returning person may prefer an entirely different contemplative tradition. Do not gate source reading by practice minutes or present advanced techniques as rewards. The underlying traditions also disagree about liberation, selfhood and method; there is no neutral app scale that measures all of them.

Do not ask “What spiritual level are you?” Ask a bounded present preference: “A short practice, or some context?” When a user chooses structure, explain what will happen and ask for explicit consent. “Explore more” must never enroll them in notifications, a community or a more intense practice automatically.

## Proposed teaching sequence through one example

**Notice:** a person reaches for a blocked feed during a chosen reading window. The system knows only the chosen plan and an attempted app opening, not the emotion behind it.

**Understand:** “You chose this time for reading.” This is enough explanation for the immediate task. Do not introduce karma, dopamine or avoidance as a diagnosis.

**Experience:** optional ordinary contact with the room or bodily support, only in an appropriate setting. A person can leave without practicing. The focus boundary is managed separately.

**Observe:** an optional question after the practice: “What do you notice now?” No required revelation. “Nothing” and no response are valid.

**Integrate:** return to the chosen activity or deliberately revise the plan through the normal controls. The app does not insist that returning to work is spiritually superior to rest or an emergency call.

**If curiosity appears later:** offer the source of a sourced practice and its adaptation note. For a modern exercise, say that honestly. Only on further request introduce textual disagreements or related practices. A philosophical explanation should not be presented during an active safety/override decision.

## Transition criteria and data minimization

Use explicit requests for context, preferred practice and optional usefulness reports. Do not store a consciousness rank or infer trauma, diagnosis, belief, caste or attainment. A pattern is provisional: distinguish what the user wrote from the app's inference and make correction/deletion easy. Long-term sensitive memory is not needed for the V1 research proposal.

Different forms of withdrawal need different interpretations: confirmed independent practice; continuing benefit with support; disappointed departure; overload; technical failure; unknown. The final group stays unknown even if analytics show fewer opens. A user may value continued assistance; independence is not an obligation imposed by the product.

## Two journey experiments

**J-01: Choosing depth.** Source: founder brief + audience/UX synthesis; confidence low for transfer to Shanivar. Invite 12 consenting adults across practice experience to use a practice-first and a context-first route in counterbalanced order. Both routes keep provenance reachable. Success: participants find the depth they want without an explanation from the moderator and can leave without losing progress. Failure: unwanted belief framing, content overload or basic practice hidden behind “levels.” Decision: TEST; no population inference from this cohort.

**J-02: Optional support taper.** Source: founder independence goal and documented distinction between practice and engagement; confidence low for product mechanism. After a useful basic loop is demonstrated, let participants choose unchanged reminders, fewer reminders or none for two weeks. Record choice, self-reported offline use and voluntary explanation of change. Success: reduced prompting without a reported loss of valued support; failure: abandonment from confusion or pressure to “graduate.” Decision: HOLD until the basic loop and consented follow-up work. Selection is voluntary, so this is descriptive learning, not a randomized efficacy claim.

## Boundaries for depth

The app may organize reading and ordinary practice; it does not grant initiation, identify a person's spiritual attainments, replace an existing teacher, guarantee awakening or prescribe intensive methods. Advanced textual information can be studied without publishing a self-guided execution script. Exact practice review is separate from founder acceptance of an intellectual stance.
