## What is new

- Eight starter options: Arrive with your breath; Notice the turn; Hum, then listen; One breath, one word; Make room for the feeling; Follow a fading sound; Let the day end; Notice the room.
- Sessions offer 1, 3 or 5 minutes where supported; not every practice supports every duration.
- Optional recommendations use self-reported feelings, available time and chosen wake/sleep schedule. This does not measure biological rhythms, infer emotions or optimize hormones.
- Modern adaptations carry VBT source details. Notice the room is a modern fallback, not a VBT attribution. Some other options are attention or sound practices rather than breathing exercises.
- Beginner scope excludes prescribed breath holds and forced breath pacing. No claims of universal safety or clinical effectiveness.
- Planned entry points: Home, before focus, optional pause within the block overlay and after verified alarm dismissal. Breathing never unlocks blocked apps or substitutes for alarm dismissal.
