## Acceptance criteria and definition of done
- Each segment has a measurable activation definition, agreed with Product.
- Findings separate behavior from self-report and state their limits.
- Every recommendation links to a ticket.
- Higher engagement is never reported as proof of awareness.
- **DoD:** dashboard spec, interview guide and first insights report delivered.
