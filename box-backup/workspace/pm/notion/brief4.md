## Objective
Communicate "Protect. Become Aware. Enter Flow." accurately and attract users whose problems Shanivar solves.
## Top work items (first release)
1. Positioning and messaging doc, plus a feature-claim verification checklist ([DEV-88](https://shanivar.atlassian.net/browse/DEV-88))
2. Plain explanation of how protection, routine and awareness relate; separate messages for distraction, routines and meditation adherence
3. Align landing page, onboarding, store listing and launch comms with the actual first-release features and limits
4. A test of whether people understand the promise before any larger campaign (no spend without founder approval)
## Acceptance criteria and definition of done
- No medical, therapeutic, energy or guaranteed-performance claims; no "impossible to bypass".
- No teacher or lineage named; a translator credit (for example Wallis) is fine; "33 sections" is fine.
- Every claim is checked against the feature-verification page and platform limits (browser and other devices are not blocked; brief flash).
- Success is the share of acquired users who activate and reach a first goal, not clicks or installs.
