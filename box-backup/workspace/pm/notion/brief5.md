## Objective
Keep Shanivar financially viable without monetisation undermining trust.
## Top work items (first release)
1. Unit-economics model and monthly opex forecast (see the Finance model request page)
2. Cost per activated and per retained user, with explicit assumptions
3. Pricing and monetisation scenarios; a free-vs-paid boundary that never paywalls essential controls, stop or privacy
4. Cost-risk register: notifications, usage growth, AI (only if Gemini is kept; currently it is not in the first release)
## Acceptance criteria and definition of done
- Every number carries a source or a labelled assumption.
- Prices are not set from competitors alone; willingness to pay is validated with target users.
- Essential controls and privacy are not weakened for conversion.
- **DoD:** model, assumption register and opex forecast reviewed with the founder.
