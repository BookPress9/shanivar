# 0007 — V1 includes alarms and focus modes

- Date: 2026-09-13
- Status: Accepted founder scope; implementation pending
- Supersedes: alarm deferral and one-target-only scope in 0006 and the PM review

## Context

The founder explicitly restored alarms and app locks/focus modes, asked for Alarmy and an established mindful competitor as references, and requested marketing refinement before handing v1 to Claude. The presence loop, pre-signup pause, parent activities, helper handback and optional Shani stance remain. Other feature families remain later.

## Decision

One home offers independent Set alarm and Start focus actions. V1 includes one editable alarm with repeat days, movement/non-walking dismissal and truthful readiness; focus includes editable activity presets and a selected app set on the existing engine. Alarm recurrence is required; automatic focus recurrence remains deferred. No alarm-to-lock automation.

Use Alarmy and one sec as behavior references with original UI/copy and Shanivar's existing contracts. The marketing team supplied original product copy and acquisition hypotheses; it found no validated winning audience, language or channel.

All ages remain intended. Accessible controls are required now; the adult pilot is the first validation step. Report school-age readiness gaps before claiming the release supports every age. A non-walking dismissal must not require standing, despite contradictory older enforcement wording.

## Tradeoffs and completion

This adds alarm recurrence, permission, coexistence and physical reliability work compared with the focus-only plan. Do not quietly defer alarms again for speed or claim a complete v1 from documentation. Preserve alarm safety and whole-app enforcement limits. Both tools must pass their gates; age, language and hardware gaps stay explicit.

Revisit breadth after voluntary repeat and observed friction. Larger mission catalogues, sleep tracking, charts, AI scroller, payments and family dashboards remain later. Founder approval of both tools controls over earlier PM advice.
