# 0015 - Sadhak and Hybrid Monk are core product ideology

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Status:** Accepted (founder, in chat, 12 October 2026)
- **Source:** `SHANIVAR_STANCE.md` ST-010 (founder wording, verbatim).

## Decision
The founder's definitions of becoming a **Sadhak** and of a **Hybrid Monk** (ST-010) are core product ideology. Every feature, piece of copy and practice must express them, within the safety and claims rules. Founder request: "Make it super immersive into the website and into the product ideology as we keep building Shanivar."

## What this means in practice
- **Routine** is how a Hybrid Monk lives in an ordinary, connected life with structure: connected with everything, attached to nothing. Discipline is chosen and stoppable.
- **Sadhana** is the Sadhak path: the right stance to face discomfort and grow through it. It is uncomfortable. If you cannot cope, accept it: stopping is valid, End is always available.
- **Presence** ("headless, just the body") is attention in the body, offered as an unpaced, optional invitation.

## Guardrails (unchanged, still binding)
No health, therapy or healing claims; "free of suffering" is a contemplative aim (`research/deep-foundation/PRACTICE_SAFETY.md:49-51`). "Living dangerously" never implies physical risk; stationary-setting rules hold (`PRACTICE_SAFETY.md:32`). No one is asked to endure an unsafe practice (`PRACTICE_SAFETY.md:47`). No Osho quotations. SOP-05 banned claims apply. No streaks, scores or ranks (0009, 0013).
